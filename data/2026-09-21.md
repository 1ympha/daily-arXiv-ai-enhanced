### [1] [High Reconstruction Quality and Restart Repeatability Do Not Guarantee Recovery of Ground-Truth Muscle Synergies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21394)
*Ye Ma,Dongwei Liu,Meijin Hou,Chenyi Guo*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 高重构质量与重启一致性并不能保证肌肉协同的真实恢复，VAF/肘部准则在秩选择与空间向量恢复上表现较差。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 检验常用的VAF/肘部准则能否恢复生成协同的数量与空间向量，判断高重启重复性是否代表真实恢复，并考察五种设计因素对恢复的影响。

Method: 对4,320个合成16肌肉数据集应用非负矩阵分解，变化生成秩、噪声、试次数量、空间相似度与激活重叠度；以正确秩及每个匹配空间向量余弦相似度≥0.80作为联合恢复标准，用异方差Wald检验与Benjamini-Hochberg校正分析因素及交互效应。

Result: 秩选择完全正确仅17.6%（偏低54.9%、偏高27.5%），联合恢复13.9%；VAF≥0.90者中仅11.3%实现联合恢复；空间重复性≥0.95的3,762个数据集中仅19.6%秩正确、15.7%联合恢复。五个因素均与恢复显著相关（校正p<0.001），恢复率随空间相似度和激活重叠增加而大幅下降，高秩、少试次时更低，噪声影响非单调。

Conclusion: 在该选择准则下，高重构质量和重启一致性不足以指示正确秩与空间恢复，肌肉协同解读应纳入秩敏感性以及空间与激活模式可分离性的考量。

Abstract: High reconstruction quality and agreement across repeated fits do not necessarily establish recovery of muscle synergies. We tested whether a variance-accounted-for (VAF)/elbow rule recovers the generating synergy count and spatial vectors, whether high restart repeatability indicates recovery, and how five design factors affect recovery. Non-negative matrix factorisation was applied to 4,320 synthetic 16-muscle datasets varying generating rank, noise, trial count, spatial similarity and activation overlap. Combined recovery required the correct rank and cosine similarity of at least 0.80 for every matched spatial vector. Factor effects and two-factor interactions were assessed using exploratory heteroscedastic Wald tests with Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment. Rank selection was exact in 17.6% of datasets, too low in 54.9% and too high in 27.5%; combined recovery was 13.9%. Among fits with VAF at least 0.90, only 11.3% achieved combined recovery. Among 3,762 datasets with spatial repeatability at least 0.95, 19.6% had the correct rank and 15.7% achieved combined recovery. All five factors were associated with recovery (adjusted p < 0.001). Recovery declined from 26.2% to 1.7% with increasing spatial similarity and from 26.2% to 2.2% with increasing activation overlap. It was lower at ranks 7-9 than at 3-5, increased from 11.0% with 3 trials to 15.8% with 80 trials, and varied non-monotonically with noise. Five noiseless signals synthesised from measured-sEMG reference factors also showed under-selection despite VAF above 0.918. Under this selector, high reconstruction quality and restart agreement were insufficient indicators of correct rank and spatial recovery. Muscle-synergy interpretation should account for rank sensitivity and the separability of spatial and activation patterns.

### [2] [Best Matches in Phylogenetic Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21700)
*Patricia A. Ebert,Peter F. Stadler,Marc Hellmuth*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文将有根树中最优匹配图（BMG）的概念推广到有色有根网络，用"sicolor-in-hub"条件刻画网络的BMG，并给出线性时间识别算法和二次时间构造解释网络的算法，同时对互最优匹配图（RBMG）得到类似结果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 最优匹配图（BMG）原本用于数学系统发生学，描述有根树的叶子上（代表不同物种中的相关基因）最亲近亲属的概念。然而，现实中的系统发生关系可能并非严格的树结构，而是网络结构（如基因水平转移、杂交等事件会导致网状进化）。在有根网络中，最小公共祖先一般既不唯一也不可比，因此需要将BMG的概念推广到有根网络，并研究其识别与构造问题。

Method: 作者将BMG概念从叶子着色的有根树推广到叶子着色的有根网络。由于网络中最小公共祖先一般既不唯一也不可比，他们提出了称为"sicolor-in-hub property"的易于检验的条件。在此基础上，刻画了有根网络的BMG为那些正常着色的顶点着色有向图且满足该条件。此外，对互最优匹配图（RBMG）进行了类似分析，其中边{x,y}对应不同颜色且互为最亲近亲属的顶点对。

Result: 作者成功刻画了有根网络的BMG：即那些正常着色的顶点着色有向图，且满足易于检验的"sicolor-in-hub property"。基于此刻画，BMG可以在线性时间内被识别，并且可以在二次时间内构造出解释网络。对于互最优匹配图（RBMG）也获得了类似的结果。

Conclusion: 本文将最优匹配图（BMG）的概念从有根树成功推广到有根网络，克服了网络中最小公共祖先非唯一且不可比的困难，通过"sicolor-in-hub property"给出了简洁的刻画，并提供了高效的识别（线性时间）与构造（二次时间）算法。这一推广及对应的RBMG结果，为在更一般的网状进化模型下分析基因最亲近关系提供了理论基础和算法工具。

Abstract: Best match graphs (BMGs) were introduced in mathematical phylogenetics to describe the concept of closest relatives for related genes (leaves of rooted tree) in different organisms (defining leaf colors). We generalize this concept here to leaf-colored rooted networks, where least common ancestors are in general neither unique nor comparable. We characterize BMGs of rooted networks as those vertex-colored digraphs that are properly colored and satisfy an easy-to-check condition that we call the sicor-in-hub property. BMGs can be recognized in linear time and an explaining network can be constructed in quadratic time. Analogous results are obtained for reciprocal best match graphs (RBMGs), where an edge $\{x,y\}$ corresponds to pairs of vertices with different color that are mutually closest relatives.

### [3] [Exact Counts of Binary Phylogenetic Networks with Four Reticulations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21772)
*Hao Yu,Louxin Zhang*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 该论文推导了含4个网状结构（reticulations）的无限定有根二叉系统发育网络在n个标记分类单元上的显式闭式计数公式。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 系统发育网络能灵活表示杂交、基因渗入、重组和水平基因转移等网状进化过程，但其组合复杂性使基本枚举问题非常困难。此前工作仅覆盖最多3个网状结构的情形，本文希望将精确枚举扩展到4个网状结构。

Method: 基于树分量图（tree-component graphs）方法，将对应4个网状结构的79种可能分量图分为十组，再结合已知的单分量网络、森林以及更少网状结构网络的计数结果，对每组相关网络进行枚举，最后求和得到公式。

Result: 得到了含4个网状结构的无限定有根二叉系统发育网络在n个标记分类单元上的显式闭式计数公式。

Conclusion: 该结果将无限定二叉系统发育网络的精确枚举扩展到4个网状结构，进一步证明了分量图方法在系统组织与计数日益复杂网络类方面的有效性。

Abstract: Phylogenetic networks provide a flexible framework for representing reticulate evolutionary processes, such as hybridization, introgression, recombination, and horizontal gene transfer. However, their combinatorial complexity makes even basic enumeration problems difficult. Building on our previous work for networks with up to three reticulations, we derive an explicit closed-form formula for the number of unrestricted rooted binary phylogenetic networks with four reticulations on \(n\) labeled taxa.
  Our approach is based on tree-component graphs. We classify the 79 possible component graphs corresponding to networks with four reticulations into ten groups. We then enumerate the networks associated with each group by combining known counts of one-component networks, forests, and networks with fewer reticulations. Summing these contributions yields the desired formula. This result extends the exact enumeration of unrestricted binary phylogenetic networks to four reticulations and further demonstrates the effectiveness of component graphs for systematically organizing and counting increasingly complex network classes.

</details>
