### [1] [Operationalizing open-ended biological discovery across single-cell representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00681)
*Ningxuan Zhang,Ziwei Wang,Ning Xie,Na Liu*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文提出PROSPECTor框架，将单细胞研究从预设问题驱动转变为开放式发现范式，通过跨多种表征空间（常规表达谱和基础模型嵌入）搜索可复现的生物学结构，并在未见数据集中验证其泛化性和表型关联，从而将回顾性单细胞数据转化为可驱动前瞻性实验投资的候选假说资源。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统单细胞研究通常由预设的研究问题驱动，导致已存数据中大量潜在生物学信息未被探索，缺乏系统性地从数据中发现新信号并对其他前瞻性实验投资价值进行定量评估的范式。

Method: PROSPECTor是一个端到端框架：(1) 在常规表达表征和多种基础模型嵌入空间中搜索可复现的生物学结构；(2) 将稳健的信号转化为可定量检验的候选假说；(3) 通过投影到未见数据集评估其泛化性和表型关联；(4) 提供一个可扩展的筛选管线，识别值得前瞻性验证的候选信号。

Result: 来自不同表征空间和搜索策略的信号均得到支持。成纤维细胞细胞外基质程序在独立小鼠队列（含干预背景）中表现出可转移性；患者层面的胃癌T细胞程序在单细胞、bulk和空间队列中均能重现，验证了候选假说的生物学意义和跨平台泛化性。

Conclusion: PROSPECTor建立了一个可审计的框架，能够系统地重新审视单细胞数据集并跨越不断扩展的表征空间，将回顾性数据集合转化为可激发新研究问题、驱动前瞻性生物学发现的前瞻性资源。

Abstract: Single-cell studies are typically initiated from predefined research questions, leaving much of the biological information encoded within existing data unexplored. We formalize open-ended discovery as an analytical paradigm, in which data-derived signals are identified before biological context is interrogated and subsequently evaluated according to their potential to justify prospective experimental investment. Here we develop PROSPECTor, an end-to-end framework that searches for reproducible biological structures across conventional expression representations and diverse foundation-model embeddings, translating robust signals into quantitatively testable candidate hypotheses. Projection into unseen datasets then evaluates their generalizability and phenotype association, providing a scalable screen for candidates that warrant prospective validation. Supported signals emerged from different representation spaces and search strategies. PROSPECTor-nominated hypotheses were then examined in independent biological settings: fibroblast extracellular-matrix programmes demonstrated transferability to an independent mouse cohort with an intervention context, while a patient-resolved gastric-cancer T-cell programme recurred across single-cell, bulk and spatial cohorts. PROSPECTor establishes an auditable framework for systematically revisiting single-cell datasets across expanding representation spaces, turning retrospective collections into prospective resources for biological discovery that can motivate new research questions.

### [2] [Efficiently classifying shocks in complex systems requires dormant reporters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00725)
*David A. Brewster,Philippe Cluzel*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 该研究探讨了在复杂动力系统中，如何选择稀疏的监测位点（报告器）来分类外部扰动。研究发现，在实验存在噪声（初始条件无法完美复现）的情况下，传统基于灵敏度的报告器选择远非最优，最佳面板应混合两类报告器：广谱响应型（响应多数扰动，但易受噪声干扰）和休眠型（只强烈响应少数扰动，但稳定不易波动）。随着噪声增大，两类报告器的重要性趋于均衡。令人惊讶的是，只需极少数量的报告器即可准确识别系统遭遇的扰动，研究提出了高效算法来组装这种最优面板。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 许多自然和工程系统是大型复杂网络，外部扰动驱动系统沿不同路径演化。识别具体扰动的类型对于诊断、控制和预测至关重要，但实际中通常只能监测少数组件。传统实验实践倾向于在最敏感的位置放置报告器（扰动影响最大的位点），但需要验证此策略在现实噪声条件下是否最优。

Method: 使用一个具有异构连接性的简单动力学模型来描述复杂系统。研究稀疏报告器面板的选择问题，目标是在多次试验只能近似复现理想初始条件（即存在噪声）的情况下，从部分轨迹中分类识别不同的扰动。

Result: 研究发现：(1) 当初始条件复现不完美时，基于灵敏度排序的报告器面板远非最优，且差距随噪声增大；(2) 最佳面板应混合两类报告器：广谱响应型（响应多数扰动但受试验间波动影响大）和休眠型（仅强烈响应少量扰动但试验间稳定）；(3) 随噪声增大，两类报告器的重要性差距缩小；(4) 单一类型的面板分类效果均不如混合型；(5) 极少数报告器即可准确识别扰动类型。研究还实现了高效的面板组装算法。

Conclusion: 该研究推翻了传统"最有效扰动位置监测"的实验直觉，提出了一种低成本、实用的监测面板设计原则：在噪声环境中，应混合选择广谱型与休眠型报告器，而非一味选择灵敏度最高的位点，这为大型复杂动力系统的监测设计提供了新的指导原则。

Abstract: Many natural and engineered systems are large complex networks of interacting components, and external perturbations drive them along different dynamical paths. Identifying which perturbation occurred matters for diagnosis, control, and prediction. Yet often times only a few components can be jointly monitored. Which components should be monitored? Experimental practice usually favors placing reporters at the most sensitive sites, where perturbations produce the largest effects. Using a simple dynamical model for complex systems with heterogeneous connectivity, we ask how sparse reporter panels should be chosen to classify shocks from partial trajectories when repeated trials only approximately reproduce an ideal initial condition. Once that reproduction is imperfect, sensitivity ranked panels fall far short of optimal, and the shortfall grows with the noise. We find that the best panels mix two kinds of reporters. A promiscuous reporter responds to most shocks, so it separates them mainly by degree, and degree fluctuates from trial to trial. A dormant reporter responds to only a few shocks but strongly, and its answers do not scatter as much between trials. As noise grows, the cost of losing a dormant reporter rises to meet the cost of losing a promiscuous one. Panels of either kind alone classify worse than the mixture, and no property of the members collected individually explains the ordering. Most of all, we find that only a minuscule number of reporters are needed on a panel to accurately identify which shock hit the system. We implement an efficient algorithm to assemble such a panel. Together these results provide a low cost practical design principle for monitoring large complex dynamical systems.

</details>


### [3] [On the interpretation of the kinetics of ligand-receptor binding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01228)
*David Colquhoun,James P Higham*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文研究在配体结合伴随受体构象变化的条件下，通过表面等离子体共振等动力学方法测得的结合/解离速率常数估算平衡常数的可靠性。研究分析了三态模型中的三种特殊情况，发现大多数情况下估算的平衡常数接近表观（宏观）平衡常数，但结合远快于构象变化的情况除外。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在实际实验中，常用动力学速率常数之比来估算配体结合的平衡常数，并将其与平衡状态下测得的EC50进行对比以验证动力学测量的正确性。然而这种验证只有在受体不发生构象变化时才严格成立，因此需要系统评估构象变化存在时该方法的适用性。

Method: 作者构建了一个简单的三态模型，其中配体结合后伴随受体构象变化。针对三种时间过程接近单指数的特殊情况进行分析：（1）结合远快于构象变化；（2）构象变化远快于结合；（3）配体解离速率与受体激活速率均较快。

Result: 研究结果表明，在多数情况下，由动力学速率估算的平衡常数接近表观（宏观）平衡常数，即EC50，该值同时依赖于描述配体结合和构象变化的两个微观平衡常数。唯一的例外是结合远快于后续构象变化的情况，但即便如此，估算的配体结合平衡常数仍取决于两个微观平衡常数。

Conclusion: 当受体存在构象变化时，通过动力学速率估算的平衡常数通常与EC50接近，但该方法不能分别解析出单个微观平衡常数，在特定条件下（结合远快于构象变化）可能产生偏差，需谨慎解读动力学数据。

Abstract: When the rates of ligand binding are measured by methods such as surface plasmon resonance, it is common practice to use the observed rate constants for the onset and offset of binding to estimate an equilibrium constant for ligand binding. If this agrees with the equilibrium constant found as the EC50 for binding at equilibrium, this is taken as validation of the measured rates. This is correct only when binding produces no conformation change in the receptor, and ligand binding follows a single exponential time course. Here, we investigate a simple 3 state model in which binding is followed by a conformation change in the receptor. Three special cases of this model in which the time course of onset and offset of ligand binding are close to being single exponentials are analysed. These cases are (1) when binding is much faster than the conformation change, (2) when the conformation change is much faster than binding, and (3) when the rates of ligand dissociation and receptor activation are both fast. It is concluded that the measured rates will often yield an estimate of the equilibrium constant for ligand binding that is close to the effective, or macroscopic, equilibrium constant, the EC50 found by measuring binding at equilibrium, which depends on both of the underlying microscopic equilibrium constants describing ligand binding and the conformation change. The exception to this conclusion is the case when binding is much faster than the subsequent conformation change, though the estimate of the equilibrium constant for ligand binding still depends on both of the underlying microscopic equilibrium constants.

</details>

### [4] [BME-like Quartet Weights for Phylogenetic Trees](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00614)
*Peter J. Waddell*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文提出并分析了三类类BME（balanced minimum evolution）四元组权重，用于在系统发育树评分中降低四元组的冗余性和相关性，并推导了这些权重在不同树形下的数学性质与归一化方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 系统发育分析中四元组（quartets）信息的数量随物种数n以n^4阶增长，远多于成对距离的n^2，且高度冗余和相互关联。作者希望探索类似BME（balanced minimum evolution）的权重方案，对四元组评分进行加权后求和来评价整棵树，以降低冗余，提升有限数据下的统计效率。

Method: 作者考虑三种作用于无根二叉树的四元组权重：w_ext(q)=2^(-I_ext(q))、w_int(q)=2^(-I_int(q))和w_tot(q)=2^(-I_tot(q))=w_ext(q)w_int(q)，其中指数分别统计四元组最小连接子树中指定的内部节点数目。通过对6-10个物种所有无标记无根二叉树形的精确树计形、四元组权重总和以及内部边穿越总和的数值计算，以及理论上对任意n叶树的总权重和穿越边权重的证明，推导出解析公式。同时针对多歧树给出度校正的硬多分（hard-polytomy）扩展，并证明在底层四元组估计本身一致时，这些正权重不会破坏一致性。

Result: 对于w_ext，总四元组权重与树形无关，穿越内部边的权重和仅依赖于分裂大小；证明任意n叶树满足sum_q w_ext(q)=(n-2)(n-3)/8，穿越分裂为a|b的内部边的四元组权重和为(a-1)(b-1)/4。同时推导出w_int和w_tot的树形特异的精确归一化常数，并给出了多歧树的度校正扩展以及权重方法的一致性保持结果。

Conclusion: 这些结果为实现和应用类BME四元组权重提供了数学基础，目标是降低四元组间的冗余和相关性，从而在有限数据下提高统计效率。

Abstract: Like pairwise distances, quartets can be highly redundant and correlated on a phylogenetic tree, and their number grows on the order of n^4 rather than n^2. I explore BME-like weights for reweighting quartet scores before summing them to score a full tree. Three weights are considered on an unrooted binary tree: w_ext(q)=2^(-I_ext(q)), w_int(q)=2^(-I_int(q)), and w_tot(q)=2^(-I_tot(q))=w_ext(q)w_int(q), where the exponents count specified internal nodes in the minimal connecting subtree of a quartet. Exact tree-shape counts, total quartet-weight sums, and internal-edge crossing sums are calculated for all unlabeled unrooted binary tree shapes on 6-10 taxa. For w_ext, the total quartet weight is tree-shape-invariant and the edge-crossing sum depends only on split size. For any n-leaf tree, we prove sum_q w_ext(q)=(n-2)(n-3)/8, and the sum over quartets crossing an internal edge with split a|b equals (a-1)(b-1)/4. Exact tree-shape-specific normalizers are also derived for w_int and w_tot. A degree-corrected hard-polytomy extension is given for multifurcating trees, and a conditional consistency result shows that these positive weights preserve consistency when the underlying quartet estimates are themselves consistent for the true induced quartet states. These results provide a mathematical foundation for evaluating and applying BME-like quartet weights to reduce redundancy with the particular aim of improving statistical efficiency with finite data.

</details>
