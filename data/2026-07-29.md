### [1] [Beyond Predictive Accuracy: A Reliability-Aware Audit of Molecular Representations for Human Olfaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24848)
*Kai Lun Huang,Wei Chieh Sun*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文对通用分子编码器（MoLFormer和ChemBERTa）在人类嗅觉任务中的表现进行了可靠性审计，发现这些编码器在全局感知几何、增量预测价值、跨数据集复制和混合物迁移等方面均未显著优于传统化学描述符（RDKit和Morgan指纹），人类评分几何结构具有高可重复性，而模型与人类的对齐性很弱。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的分子编码器评估仅依赖下游预测准确率，但这不足以证明表征捕捉到了可复现的科学结构、提供了超越传统基准的增量信息或在分布外具有迁移能力。

Method: 使用Keller-Vosshall和Bierling单分子评分数据集以及Ma二元混合物数据集，在身份控制和匹配评估条件下，比较MoLFormer和ChemBERTa与RDKit描述符和Morgan指纹的表现，从全局感知几何、增量预测价值、跨数据集复制和混合物迁移四个方面进行审计。

Result: 人类三属性评分几何在不同参与者划分下具有高可重复性（中位数RSA 0.743和0.855），但模型-人类对齐性很弱（RSA 0.019-0.158）。学习到的嵌入在全局对齐上未持续优于传统表征，MoLFormer在单分子数据集中未提供超出RDKit-Morgan基线的增量预测价值。在严格的不见组分混合物划分下，增量效应取决于结果和表征，所有区间均跨越零。

Conclusion: 这些结果为评估的通用分子编码器设定了经验边界，并提出了更广泛的评估原则：科学领域的表征质量应分别从目标可靠性、结构对齐、增量信息、复制和分布外迁移等方面进行评估。

Abstract: Pretrained molecular encoders are commonly evaluated through downstream prediction, but predictive accuracy alone does not establish that a learned representation captures reproducible scientific structure, adds information beyond strong conventional baselines, or transfers out of distribution. We present a reliability-aware audit of generic molecular representations for human olfaction across four distinct claims: global perceptual geometry, incremental predictive value beyond chemistry, cross-dataset replication, and mixture transfer to unseen components.
  Using the Keller-Vosshall and Bierling single-molecule rating datasets and the Ma binary-mixture dataset, we compare MoLFormer and ChemBERTa against RDKit descriptors and Morgan fingerprints under identity-controlled and matched evaluations. Human three-attribute rating geometry, based on intensity, pleasantness, and familiarity, is reproducible across participant splits (median RSA 0.743 and 0.855), whereas model-human alignment is substantially weaker (RSA 0.019-0.158). Learned embeddings do not consistently outperform conventional representations in global alignment, and MoLFormer provides no clear incremental predictive value beyond a combined RDKit-Morgan baseline in either single-molecule dataset. Human geometry shows positive but incomplete agreement across 63 shared molecules (RSA 0.331; 95% bootstrap interval [0.204, 0.507]). Under one strict unseen-component mixture split, incremental effects are outcome- and representation-dependent, with all intervals crossing zero. These results establish empirical boundaries for the evaluated generic molecular encoders and motivate a broader evaluation principle: representation quality in scientific domains should be assessed separately for target reliability, structural alignment, incremental information, replication, and out-of-distribution transfer.

### [2] [A Hierarchical Validity-Audit Framework for Neural Mass Models in Simulation-Based Inference: From Observational Coverage to Mechanistic Interpretation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24874)
*Tianming Cai,Ning Jiang,Yuan Yang,Jiayuan He*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 提出NMM-SBI Audit框架，用于检验神经质量模型在基于模拟的推理中的有效性，区分四种失败来源，防止将仿真恢复误读为生理解释。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 神经质量模型通过仿真推理进行后验估计，但良好的仿真恢复不能保证模型覆盖真实观测数据、摘要统计保留目标信息或参数独立可解释，缺乏系统的有效性验证框架。

Method: 提出层次化有效性审计框架NMM-SBI Audit：先评估候选模型是否覆盖观测数据，再训练分离的后验估计器评估摘要信息损失，使用多轨道联合后验检验解释一致性，结果以分级证据报告。

Result: 在SOZ-local iEEG-Epileptor实验中，波形PCA仿真近似观测信号，但动态诊断持续不匹配，阻止参数解释为患者特定机制；在CMC实验中，条件覆盖良好、负补偿增益关系稳定，但存在摘要信息损失和活跃子空间不稳定性。

Conclusion: NMM-SBI Audit能区分四种失败来源：模型-配置不匹配、摘要信息损失、目标信息不足、联合参数补偿/跨轨道耦合，为可支持的结论提供明确边界。

Abstract: Neural mass models describe population level neural activity using low-dimensional dynamical parameters and, through simulation-based inference, enable posterior estimation when explicit likelihoods are intractable, but good posterior recovery on simulated data does not guarantee that the model covers real observations, that summary statistics retain target information, or that multiple parameters stay independently interpretable. We introduce NMM-SBI Audit, a hierarchical validity-audit framework. It first evaluates whether a candidate model configuration covers the observed data. It then trains separate posterior estimators for parameter coordinates at multiple levels to assess summary-induced information loss. Multi-track joint posteriors are used to examine consistency across interpretations, with outcomes reported as graded evidence. We applied the framework to two real datasets: an SOZ-local iEEG-Epileptor model and an ERP CORE MMN-CMC model, using three summary representations. In the Epileptor experiment, waveform PCA simulations approximated observed signals, but persistent mismatch in preregistered dynamical diagnostics prevented interpreting recovered parameters as patient-specific mechanisms. The five population CMC configuration showed good conditional coverage, stable negative compensatory gain relationships, and coherent posteriors, while revealing summary information loss and active-subspace instability. NMM-SBI Audit thus distinguishes four failure sources: model-configuration mismatch, summary-induced information loss, insufficient target information, and joint parameter compensation/cross-track coupling. This prevents strong within-simulator recovery from being misread as valid physiological interpretation, providing a scalable constraint with explicit boundaries on supportable conclusions.

### [3] [GraphRareBench: An Auditable Graph-Evidence Benchmark for Phenotype-Driven Rare-Disease Diagnosis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24878)
*Guiling Guo,Jia Yang,Jiahao Xu,Shuyuan Zheng,Zhonghai Sun,Qiyuan Li*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: GraphRareBench是一个包含2,365个本体病例和18,093个目标-混淆者对的基准测试，通过保留证据来源的方式评估表型驱动诊断系统。在237例基因组件不相交测试集上，监督排序器和智能体模型表现各有优劣，揭示了全库检索、硬混淆者区分和可观察证据获取是互补的行为维度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有表型驱动诊断基准通常只报告参考疾病的排名，很少揭示哪些合理的替代方案排在它之上，也不展示工具模型在决策前检查了哪些证据。为了更透明和可解释的评估，需要一种保留证据来源的基准。

Method: 构建包含2,365个本体病例和18,093个目标-混淆者对的GraphRareBench基准，每个病例包含粗化的HPO查询、固定候选池、图定义的硬混淆者和源链接证据记录。使用监督排序器（21特征接口）和智能体模型进行评估。

Result: 监督排序器MRR 0.640-0.740，目标-混淆者准确率0.898-0.916；Agent模型MRR分别为0.746和0.718；22.1%-43.7%的Hit@10成功案例中仍有硬混淆者排在目标之上。

Conclusion: 全库检索、硬混淆者区分和可观察证据获取是互补的行为维度，GraphRareBench为更透明和证据感知的表型诊断系统评估提供了基础。

Abstract: Phenotype-driven diagnostic benchmarks usually report the rank of the reference disease, but they rarely reveal which plausible alternatives are ranked above it or what evidence a tool-using model examines before making its decision. We introduce GraphRareBench, a provenance-preserving benchmark containing 2,365 ontology-derived cases and 18,093 target-confounder pairs. Each case includes a coarsened HPO query, a fixed candidate pool, graph-defined hard confounders, and source-linked evidence records. On the 237-case gene-component-disjoint test split, supervised rankers using a shared 21-feature interface achieved MRRs ranging from 0.640 to 0.740 and case-averaged target-over-confounder accuracies ranging from 0.898 to 0.916. Agents instantiated with Agents-A1 and DeepSeek-V4-Flash achieved MRRs of 0.746 and 0.718, respectively. Their paired MRR difference was not statistically significant, whereas their target-evidence coverage differed by 0.561. Together with the observation that 22.1% to 43.7% of selected Hit@10 successes still ranked at least one graph-defined hard confounder above the target, these results indicate that full-pool retrieval, hard-confounder discrimination, and observable evidence access capture complementary aspects of model behavior. GraphRareBench therefore provides a foundation for more transparent and evidence-aware evaluation of phenotype-driven diagnostic systems. Code and data are available at https://github.com/GUI0609/GraphRareBench.

### [4] [A Tuning-Free Variational Framework for Muscle Redundancy Resolution: Torque Fiber Proximal Dynamics with Active-Set Switching and EMG-Validated Activation Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25013)
*Morteza Ganji*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 提出了一种名为 Torque Fiber Proximal Dynamics (TFPD) 的肌肉冗余求解方法，将激活演化建模为凸多面体上的欧几里得投影，无需可调成本参数即可自然产生拮抗肌共激活，并在三肌肉肘关节模型上取得与EMG数据0.68-0.73的Pearson相关性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 肌肉冗余问题传统上依赖成本函数（如最小能量）来求解，但拮抗肌共激活通常需要人为添加惩罚项。本文旨在提出一种无需显式成本权重、仅通过几何投影机制即可自然产生拮抗肌激活的框架。

Method: 将肌肉激活演化视为时间变化凸可行集上的欧几里得投影（TFPD），等价于后向欧拉离散化的 sweeping process 和变分不等式。通过KKT条件推导拮抗肌激活的充分条件，利用主动集转移和力矩耦合几何结构解释共激活机制。

Result: 在三肌肉肘关节模型上，TFPD与10名受试者的肱三头肌EMG包络线Pearson相关性达0.68-0.73，优于5种经典方法及随机基线。敏感性分析和三维激活轨迹可视化验证了方法的鲁棒性。

Conclusion: TFPD将神经肌肉协调与邻近点理论、变分不等式和投影动力系统联系起来，为肌肉冗余提供了一种无参数、几何驱动的求解范式。

Abstract: Muscle redundancy can be formulated as a constrained selection on a time-varying convex set of feasible activations. We introduce Torque Fiber Proximal Dynamics (TFPD), where activation evolves as the Euclidean projection of the previous state onto a convex polytope defined by torque equality and physiological bounds. TFPD is equivalent to a backward-Euler discretization of a sweeping process and a variational inequality with a maximal monotone normal cone operator. Within this framework, antagonist recruitment emerges naturally under a projection-based control hypothesis as a structural consequence of active-set transitions induced by the geometry, not as an imposed cost. We derive sufficient KKT-based conditions for antagonist activation under generic moment-arm asymmetry, linking boundary projection, strict complementarity, and torque coupling. We validate TFPD on a three-muscle elbow model using exact quadratic programming, benchmark it against five classical methods and a random baseline, and achieve a Pearson correlation of 0.68-0.73 with triceps EMG envelopes across ten subjects without any tunable cost-weight parameters. Sensitivity analysis and a three-dimensional activation trajectory visualization demonstrate robustness. TFPD connects neuromuscular coordination to proximal point theory, variational inequalities, and projected dynamical systems.

### [5] [Disconnectivity in Multistationarity Regions of Cascade of Goldbeter--Koshland Loops](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25456)
*Nidhi Kaihnsa,Kaizhang Wang*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文研究了Goldbeter-Koshland级联网络的参数空间中多稳态区域的连通性，证明当n≥2且至少有一个共享磷酸酶时，反应速率空间中的区域是连通的，但在完整参数空间中未必如此。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 反应网络的动力学常由参数化多项式建模，描述系统达到多个正平衡态的参数集合是一个具有挑战性的问题。

Method: 聚焦于Goldbeter-Koshland环的级联网络，分析n≥2个磷酸化位点且至少有一个共享磷酸酶的情况。

Result: 证明了反应速率空间中的区域是连通的，但在完整参数空间中可能不连通；对于n=2的情况，明确显示连通区域数量的上下界存在差距。

Conclusion: 现有方法可给出多稳态区域连通分量的上界，本文则揭示了特定网络结构下参数空间连通性的差异。

Abstract: Dynamics of reaction networks is often modelled by parameterised polynomials and describing the set of parameters for which the system attains multiple positive equilibrium states is a challenging problem. In the full parameter space, determined by the reaction rate constants and the total concentrations, the existing methods can give an upper bound on the number of connected components of regions that enable multistationarity and this can give sufficient condition to establish path connectivity of these regions. In this article, we focus on cascade network of Goldbeter-Koshland loops and show that for this network, with $n\geq 2$ phosphorylation sites and at least one shared phosphatase for dephosphorylation, the region in the space of reaction rates is connected while it may not be true in the full parameter space. We explicitly show that there is a gap between the upper and lower bound for the number of connected regions for the case when $n=2$.
