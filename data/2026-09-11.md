### [1] [scDEFT: A deep learning framework for drug-effect prediction and counterfactual reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10831)
*Murthy Devarakonda*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 提出scDEFT，将药物视为作用于细胞表征的条件算子，实现药物诱导状态变化与应答者状态的预测及机制解释，并在116万细胞的IBD图谱上验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 纵向单细胞图谱已能捕获同一药物下应答者与非应答者的治疗前后配对状态，为从机制上解释"同药不同效"提供了机会，但缺乏能同时预测并解释药物效应的计算框架。

Method: scDEFT以特征级线性调制生成药物条件化的细胞隐变量，在充足的逐细胞监督下学习后冻结；两个独立预测头在共享转录邻域上聚合隐变量，分别预测药物诱导的状态变化与应答者状态；反向阶段按区分应答者与非应答者的能力对隐变量维度排序，并在细胞组成控制下映射到基因。

Result: 在协调后的炎症性肠病图谱（116万细胞、三个队列、两类药物）上，scDEFT的状态变化预测达到基线到可重复性上限之间45%的余量；治疗前应答者分层AUROC为0.70，而标准预测器仅处于随机水平。

Conclusion: scDEFT的预测结果及其驱动因素可支持靶点与共靶点提名、患者分层，以及对未见药物队列效应的反事实预测。

Abstract: Longitudinal single cell atlases now capture matched pre treatment and post treatment states from responders and non responders, presenting an opportunity to mechanistically explain why two patients on the same drug diverge. We introduce scDEFT (single cell Drug EFfect Transducer), which treats a drug as a conditioning operator on cell representations, enabling prediction and explanation. In scDEFT, feature wise linear modulation produces drug conditioned cell latents, learned under abundant per cell supervision and then frozen. Two independent heads aggregate those latents over shared transcriptional neighborhoods to predict drug induced state change and responder status. A backward stage ranks the latent dimensions by how strongly they separate responders from non responders and maps them to genes under a cell composition control. On a harmonized inflammatory bowel disease atlas of 1.16 million cells, three cohorts and two drug classes, scDEFT predicts state change at 45% of the baseline to reproducibility ceiling headroom and stratifies responders before treatment at AUROC 0.70, where standard predictors remain at chance. These predictions and the drivers behind them support target and co target nomination, patient stratification, and counterfactual prediction of unseen drug cohort effects.

### [2] [Biology-in-the-loop: Amortized Adaptive Hit Discovery in CRISPR Screens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11877)
*Carl Edwards,Edward De Brouwer,Xiner Li,Namkyeong Lee,Ehsan Hajiramezanali,Anne Biton,Sara Mostafavi,Gabriele Scalia*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 该论文提出AssayBench-Loop大规模基准（1,389个CRISPR筛选，5类表型）以及AssayLoop顺序实验设计框架（含AssayFormer摊销采集策略与LLM先验的自适应交接），并进一步提出AssayLLM，在时间留出筛选上实现5.67倍富集并恢复27.7%的命中。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 许多生物学发现需在预算约束下顺序选择实验，CRISPR筛选是典型例子，需在多轮实验中对候选扰动排序。然而现有自适应命中发现基准在规模与多样性上均有限，难以系统评估并学习采集策略。

Method: 1) 构建AssayBench-Loop：覆盖五类表型、共1,389个CRISPR筛选的大规模自适应命中发现基准；2) 提出AssayLoop框架：将AssayFormer（基于Transformer、跨历史筛选训练的摊销采集策略，能从实验反馈中自适应）与LLM衍生生物学先验通过自适应交接结合；3) 提出AssayLLM：通过任务特定后训练，将该原则直接扩展到LLM。

Result: 在时间留出筛选上，AssayLoop相对随机选择实现5.67倍富集，在仅测定约5%候选文库后恢复27.7%的命中，优于现有自适应设计方法与独立LLM，也优于单独的AssayFormer。性能随历史训练数据增加而提升，并可迁移到训练中排除的表型类别。

Conclusion: 结果表明，跨历史实验学习采集策略，并与广泛生物学先验结合，可有效实现高效的自适应命中发现。

Abstract: Many biological discovery problems require experiments to be selected sequentially under constrained budgets. CRISPR screening is a prominent example, as exhaustive perturbation testing is often infeasible and candidate perturbations must instead be prioritized over multiple experimental rounds. Despite the importance of this problem, existing benchmarks for adaptive hit discovery remain limited in scale and diversity. Here, we introduce AssayBench-Loop, a large-scale benchmark for adaptive hit discovery comprising 1,389 CRISPR screens across five phenotype categories. Beyond enabling systematic evaluation, its scale makes it possible to learn acquisition strategies across historical experiments. Building on this resource, we introduce AssayLoop, a sequential experimental design framework combining AssayFormer, a transformer-based amortized acquisition policy trained across historical screens to adapt from experimental feedback, with LLM-derived biological priors through an adaptive handoff. In this view, completed experiments become training data for learning how accumulated evidence should guide what to test next, while LLMs provide prior biological knowledge to seed the search. We further introduce AssayLLM, showing that the same principle can be extended directly to an LLM through task-specific post-training. On temporally held-out screens, AssayLoop achieves a 5.67-fold enrichment over random selection and recovers 27.7% of hits after assaying approximately 5% of the candidate library, outperforming existing adaptive-design methods and standalone LLMs, and AssayFormer alone. Performance improves with increasing historical training data and transfers to phenotype categories excluded from training. These results demonstrate the value of learning acquisition policies across historical experiments and combining them with broad biological priors for efficient adaptive hit discovery.

Only our method recovers subtypes that correspond significantly to clinical motor subtypes and genetic variants of Parkinson's Disease.

### [4] [On the formulation and analysis of a stage-structured eco-evolutionary model with pulsed disturbances](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10581)

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文建立并分析了一个n维阶段结构种群的常微分方程模型，研究周期性扰动下种群的生态-进化耦合动态，并将模型应用于斑点翅果蝇的杀虫剂抗性演化问题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 受脉冲控制及其对有害生物种群演化影响的启发，作者希望理解周期性扰动（如杀虫剂施用）如何同时驱动种群的生态动态与抗性等位基因频率的进化动态。

Method: 构建n维阶段结构种群的常微分方程模型，耦合生态动态（通过生活史信息）与进化动态（等位基因频率变化）；基于已有生态-进化模型和线性系统理论进行数学分析，并用参数化模型应用于斑点翅果蝇。

Result: 证明若种群中存在抗性等位基因，种群将总是趋向完全抗性；建立了持久性条件，并表明这些条件仅取决于完全抗性状态下的系统动态；通过斑点翅果蝇案例展示了模型的应用价值。

Building off prior work in eco-evolutionary models and theory of linear systems, we show that if a resistant allele is present in the population, the population will always approach full resistance. Furthermore, we establish persistence conditions and show that these depend solely on system dynamics at full resistance. Finally, we demonstrate the utility of this model via an application to the Spotted-Winged Drosophila, an invasive species often treated periodically with insecticides. ### [5] [Competition drives excessive recruitment in collective search](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11715)
*Hyunjoong Kim,Gayashan Jayavilal,Joshua B Plotkin*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文分析了两个群体在单一觅食地点通过招募率进行竞争的最优策略，长期结果或为共存或为垄断，并探讨了二者的边界及参数影响。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 群体集体觅食时常通过招募（一名成员将其他成员引向已发现的觅食地点）来利用资源。招募增加觅食者数量，但边际回报随人数增加而下降，因此存在中间最优招募率；且一个地点可能被多个群体共同使用。作者希望理解两个群体仅通过共享回报相互作用时的竞争动态。

Method: 构建两个群体从单一地点觅食的模型，该地点的回报随在场觅食者总数下降。两群体仅通过共享回报相互作用。分析长期均衡（共存或垄断）及其边界，并考察最佳反应函数与模型参数的影响。

Result: 群体对对手的最佳反应非单调：为维持垄断会随对手招募率提高而提高自身招募率，当维持垄断不再最优时招募率会不连续下降。群体在以下情形放弃垄断：地点在较少觅食者时就饱和，且对手群体较小。当两群体规模相近时存在多个纳什均衡，任一方都可能获得较大份额。两个势均力敌的群体竞争时，双方招募率都高于使共同回报最大化的水平。

Conclusion: 共享地点的回报递减机制导致两群体竞争出现共存或垄断两种长期结果，且势均力敌的竞争会使双方过度招募，导致每个个体的收益低于同等总规模下单一未分裂群体的水平。

Abstract: Groups that search collectively often exploit what they find by recruiting: one member directs others to a site it has found. Recruitment raises the number of members foraging at a known site, but the return per forager may fall as that number grows, so there is an intermediate optimal recruitment rate. In addition, a site may be used by more than one group. Here we analyze a model of two groups that forage from a single site whose return declines with the total number of foragers present. The two groups interact only through this shared return. The long-run outcome is either coexistence at the foraging site or monopoly by one group, and we analyze the boundary between these two outcomes. A group's best response to its rival is not monotone: it increases its own recruitment rate with the rival's recruitment rate in an attempt to preserve a monopoly, and then its recruitment rate drops discontinuously when it is no longer optimal to preserve a monopoly. We analyze how model parameters govern this shift: a group relinquishes monopoly when the site saturates at few foragers and when the rival group is small. When the two groups have comparable size there are multiple Nash equilibria, so either group may end up with the larger share. And when two equally matched groups compete, both recruit above the rate that maximizes their common return, so that each individual ends with less than it would in a single undivided group of the same total size.

</details>
