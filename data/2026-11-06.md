### [1] [Leveraging deep generative modelling at the agent level in discrete-event simulations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07995)
*Margherita Molaro,Matthew M. Graham,Oliver Watson,Rachel E. Murray-Watson,Martin Chalkley,Joseph H. Collins,Bingling She,Asif U. Tamuri,Timothy B. Hallett*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 该文提出在基于事件的智能体仿真（ABS）中于"智能体层面"使用仿真器（emulator），以替代部分子模型来降低仿真本身的计算开销，并在多疾病ABS模型 Thanzi La Onse 中用条件表格生成对抗网络替换道路交通事故伤害（RTI）的建模，使运行时间缩短86.6%，同时以13%的误差复现死因别平均DALY的演化。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有ABS参数推断通常依赖对已有大量仿真输出训练的仿真器，但对计算量极大的仿真，生成训练样本本身代价过高；作者希望绕过这一瓶颈，用仿真器直接加速仿真过程而非仅做后处理近似。

Method: 在基于事件的ABS中引入智能体级仿真器：将条件表格生成对抗网络（conditional tabular GAN）嵌入仿真框架，用于替代对某一类疾病（道路交通事故伤害RTI）的显式建模，同时保留完整的ABS结构与多疾病交互动态；采用bootstrap给出运行时间与误差的置信区间。

Result: 仿真运行时间提升86.6%（95% bootstrap置信区间[85.2, 88.1]）；在致命性RTI假定发病率仅约2%的低频情形下，死因别平均DALY演化仍以13%的误差被复现；多疾病动态亦被正确还原。

Conclusion: 结果表明，在保留完整ABS框架的前提下用智能体级仿真器替代子模型是可行的，是迈向为极高计算量仿真构建全模型仿真器、并使大规模ABS可进行严格参数推断与全面参数敏感性分析的关键一步。

Abstract: Emulators are often invoked to facilitate parameter inference in computationally intensive agent-based simulations (ABSs). Most approaches, however, require a large sample of pre-existing outputs on which such emulators can be trained. For computationally intensive simulations, this sample can be prohibitively expensive to generate. We propose a new approach to leverage emulators at the agent level in event-based ABSs to reduce the computational requirements of the simulation itself. We demonstrate this in a computationally intensive ``multi-disease'' ABS, Thanzi La Onse, which self-consistently simulates a wide range of medical conditions in a population. By applying conditional tabular generative adversarial networks to replace the modelling of one of the simulated causes of ill health, road traffic injuries (RTIs), we are able to improve its simulation run-time by 86.6% (95% bootstrap confidence interval [85.2, 88.1]), while recovering the evolution of cause-specific mean disability adjusted life years with a 13% error, despite the low assumed incidence of fatal RTIs (~2%). Crucially, we do so while retaining the full ABS framework, and correctly recovering multi-disease dynamics. This represents a crucial step towards enabling full-model emulators for very computationally intensive simulations, and making large ABS amenable to rigorous parameter inference and a full understanding of parameter sensitivity.
