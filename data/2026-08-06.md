### [1] [Influence of passenger head-position uncertainty on infection risk predictions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04024)
*Philipp Bahavar,Florian Webner,Andrei Shishkin,Daniel Schmeling*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文使用基于CFD的前向风险预测模型，结合准蒙特卡洛模拟研究了自然头部运动对空气传播病原体感染风险的影响。研究发现前后和侧向头部位置对感染风险有显著影响，而基于统计矩的方法（如一阶二阶矩法）无法充分捕捉头部运动各分量与感染风险之间的依赖关系。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在飞机、列车等密闭空间中，乘客的自然头部运动会改变其呼吸区的位置和方向，从而影响吸入空气中病原体的暴露量和感染风险。现有的风险评估模型通常假设乘客静止不动，可能低估或高估实际的感染风险，因此需要量化头部运动对感染风险的影响，以提高风险评估的准确性。

Method: 采用CFD（计算流体力学）前向风险预测模型模拟气流和病原体传播；使用概率分布描述每位乘客呼吸区的位置和方向参数来表征头部运动；采用准蒙特卡洛（QMC）模拟获取感染风险分布；同时使用一阶二阶矩法（FOSM）和高阶方法计算风险分布的统计矩和敏感性，并与QMC结果进行对比。

Result: 研究发现前后（fore/aft）和侧向（lateral）头部位置对感染风险有显著影响，这些因素应被纳入考虑以提高局部座位特异性感染风险预测的稳健性。与基于采样的蒙特卡洛方法不同，基于统计矩估计的方法（包括一阶二阶矩法和高阶方法）未能充分捕捉头部运动各分量与感染风险之间的依赖关系。

Conclusion: 头部运动对密闭空间中感染风险的准确预测具有重要影响，尤其是前后和侧向头部位置变化不可忽略。对于依赖局部座位特异性感染风险预测来指导设计和政策决策的应用场景，需要采用基于采样的方法（如准蒙特卡洛）而非仅仅依赖统计矩方法来确保预测的稳健性和准确性。

Abstract: We investigate the influence of natural head movement on the infection risk posed by airborne pathogens using a CFD-based forward risk prediction model. Quasi-Monte-Carlo simulations are used to obtain the resulting infection risk distributions by representing head movement via probability distributions of parameters describing the position and orientation of each passenger's breathing zone. A significant impact of fore/aft and lateral head position on infection risk was found and should be accounted for to increase robustness when predictions of local, seat-specific infection risks are used to guide design and policy decisions. Unlike the sampling-based Monte Carlo approach, estimates of the statistical moments of the risk distributions and sensitivities, calculated using first-order second-moment and higher-order methods, were found to inadequately capture the dependencies between head movement components and infection risk.

### [2] [The Cost of Binarizing Survival Outcomes in Clinical Prognostic Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04046)
*Shashank Yadav,David M. Routman,Andrew Y. K. Foong*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文指出许多临床机器学习研究在建模前将生存数据二值化，这会导致删失患者被排除、时间信息丢失、预后特征选择不准确。作者提出"Survival-Aware Bayesian network"方法，将Cox偏似然替代二值打分函数用于贝叶斯网络特征选择，在头颈癌及其他多种癌症数据上验证了该方法的优势。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 临床机器学习研究中普遍存在将生存数据二值化的做法，这一做法排除了删失患者、压缩了时间信息，且可能影响所选预后特征的准确性，因此有必要量化二值化的代价并提出改进方案。

Method: 作者以两个已发表研究为案例，将贝叶斯网络中的特征-结局边打分函数从二值评分替换为Cox偏似然，形成Survival-Aware Bayesian network方法，并通过消融实验分离了评分公式改进与样本量增加各自的影响。

Result: 该方法能恢复被二值化遗漏的预后特征，消融实验证实改进主要来自时间-事件评分公式本身而非保留更多患者；结果在五个头颈癌终点-队列组合中泛化，并扩展到乳腺癌、结直肠癌和肾癌三种癌症类型。

Conclusion: 临床研究中具有生存结局的数据应默认使用时间-事件分析方法，因为二值化会丢弃生存分析所保留的预后信号。

Abstract: Survival analysis is an established framework for analyzing time-to-event data, yet many clinical machine learning studies still binarize the outcome before model training. This practice excludes censored patients, collapses temporal information into a single threshold, and can affect which features are selected as prognostically relevant. We examine the cost of this binarization in the context of Bayesian network (BN) feature selection, using two recent publications as case studies: one that applies BN-based feature selection to a head-and-neck cancer cohort and a second surgical cohort study that, while not BN-based, likewise binarizes its survival endpoint. We replace the binary scoring function with the Cox partial log-likelihood for feature-to-outcome edges, a modification we call the Survival-Aware Bayesian network, and recover prognostic features that binarization misses. Our ablation experiment confirms that the improvement is driven by the time-to-event scoring formulation rather than by retaining more patients. The results generalize across five endpoint-cohort combinations in head-and-neck cancer and extend to three further cancer types (breast, colorectal, and kidney). We propose that clinical studies with survival outcomes should use time-to-event methods by default, as binarization discards the prognostic signal retained by survival analysis.
