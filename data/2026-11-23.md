  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 综合结构分析、机制建模和纵向观测三种手段，评估CSD类早期预警信号在蜂群崩溃案例中的动力学合理性、可检测性与预测准确性；并通过实证蜂箱温度记录进行检验。

Abstract: Early warning signals (EWS) based on critical slowing down (CSD) have been proposed as model-independent indicators of approaching ecological regime shifts and may help anticipate honeybee colony collapse. It includes an edge of the feasible region with correction factor $e^{-1/4}$, extending our earlier result at the symmetric point.
