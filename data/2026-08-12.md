### [1] [Remote Sensing-Based Hybrid Statistical and Machine Learning Ensemble Modeling of Yellowfin Tuna Catch in the Western Equatorial Indian Ocean](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10003)
*Avishka Wijepala,Sharukshan Niranjan,Lisitha Abeysekara,Pasindu Weerasinghe*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本研究开发了一种基于遥感的统计与机器学习堆叠集成模型，用于预测2003-2024年西印度洋赤道五个渔场的黄鳍金枪鱼月度渔获量。集成模型结合线性混合模型、广义加性混合模型和随机森林，利用叶绿素浓度和海表温度数据，将log尺度R²从最优单模型GAMM的0.137提升至0.177，RMSE降至1.254。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 黄鳍金枪鱼是印度洋重要经济鱼种，但其月度渔获量受海洋生产力、热栖息地、季风季节性、渔场特征和船队行为等多重因素影响，难以准确预测，亟需建立有效的预测模型。

Method: 将月度渔获记录与卫星遥感叶绿素浓度（CHL）和海表温度（SST）数据整合；使用线性混合模型（LMM）和广义加性混合模型（GAMM）捕捉可解释的当前及滞后环境效应，随机森林（RF）捕捉非线性交互作用；采用扩展窗口验证（2008-2019年）减少时间泄漏，并通过非负Ridge堆叠集成组合各模型预测。

Result: 单模型中GAMM表现最佳，log尺度R²为0.137，RMSE为1.284；RF-E6在千克尺度上RMSE最低。非负Ridge堆叠集成将log尺度R²提升至0.177，RMSE降至1.254，MAE为1.000。

Conclusion: 卫星遥感的CHL和SST数据对月度渔获量预测具有有用但有限的预测信息，堆叠集成方法优于任何单一模型，但仍需探索更多预测因子以提升预测精度。

Abstract: Yellowfin tuna (Thunnus albacares) is an economically important pelagic species in the Indian Ocean, but its monthly catch variability is difficult to predict because reported catch is influenced by ocean productivity, thermal habitat, monsoon seasonality, fishing-ground characteristics, and fleet behavior. This study develops a remote sensing-based statistical and machine learning stacked ensemble for modeling monthly yellowfin tuna catch across five fishing grounds in the western equatorial Indian Ocean during 2003-2024. Monthly fishery records were integrated with satellite-derived chlorophyll-a concentration (CHL) and sea surface temperature (SST). Linear mixed models (LMMs) and a generalized additive mixed model (GAMM) were used to represent interpretable current and lagged environmental effects, while Random Forest models captured nonlinear interactions among lagged, rolling, seasonal, anomaly, and spatial predictors. Expanding-window validation over the pre-COVID evaluation period from 2008 to 2019 was used to reduce temporal leakage. Among the individual models, the GAMM achieved the best log-scale performance, with an R-squared value of 0.137 and an RMSE of 1.284, while RF-E6 achieved the lowest RMSE on the kilogram scale. The nonnegative Ridge stacked ensemble improved the log-scale R-squared value to 0.177, with an RMSE of 1.254 and an MAE of 1.000. These results show that satellite-derived CHL and SST contain useful but incomplete predictive information for monthly catch modeling.

### [2] [HIPNO: Symmetry-Aware Physics-Informed Neural Operators for Noninvasive Hemodynamic Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10011)
*Yunbei Pan,Jiahang Sha,Simon A. Lee,Maxime Cannesson,Wei Wang,Jeffrey N. Chiang*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文提出HIPNO（基于物理信息的神经算子）方法，利用无处不在的无创信号恢复血流动力学状态，解决物理信息推断中的尺度对称性问题，并通过在商空间中参数化网络来获得符合物理意义的生理坐标。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 连续血流动力学监测对手术和重症监护中的治疗决策至关重要，但金标准信号因侵入性测量风险仅用于重症病例。需要从无创信号中推断血流动力学状态，以扩大先进监测的可及性。

Method: HIPNO通过识别观测模型的对称群并在其商空间中参数化网络来解决尺度对称性问题。针对3元件Windkessel模型，商坐标包括顺应性归一化流量U=Q/C、衰减时间常数τ_WK=R2C和特征阻抗坐标κ=R1C。

Result: 在2562名患者的945499个术中窗口中，HIPNO预测τ_wave的对数误差比群体基线低32%，同时保持平均动脉压精度；在至少90%的窗口中产生预期方向的反事实扰动响应；坐标还可用于校准有创心输出量监测。

Conclusion: HIPNO通过分离血管衰减和流量驱动坐标，实现了超越仅依赖压力基线的方法，并识别出恢复绝对物理尺度所需的外部顺应性或流量参考。

Abstract: Continuous hemodynamic monitoring guides treatment decisions in surgery and intensive care. However, gold-standard signals are only measured in severe cases due to risks associated with invasive measurement. In this work, we introduce HIPNO (Hemodynamic Inference via Physics-informed Neural Operators) to recover hemodynamic state from ubiquitous, non-invasive signals and expand access to advanced monitoring. HIPNO addresses a problem of scale symmetry in physics-informed hemodynamic inference, where different combinations of flow, resistance, and compliance can generate the same observed pressure. We identify the symmetry group of the observation model and parameterize the network in its quotient space. For the 3-element Windkessel model, the quotient coordinates are the compliance-normalized flow $U=Q/C$, the decay time constant $τ_{WK}=R_2 C$, and the characteristic-impedance coordinate $κ=R_1 C$. Across 945499 intraoperative windows from 2562 patients, HIPNO predicts $τ_{wave}$, a proxy for vascular decay derived from pressure, with 32% lower error on the log scale than a population baseline while preserving mean arterial pressure accuracy. Because vascular decay and flow drive occupy separate coordinates, counterfactual perturbations produce the expected directional responses in at least 90% of windows in almost all prespecified scenarios, a separation unavailable to pressure-only baselines. The coordinates are also used as inputs to a calibration model for monitored cardiac output. Finally, the formulation identifies the external compliance or flow reference required to recover absolute physical scale.

### [3] [A Physics-Informed Neural Network Approach to Multiphysics Continuum Modeling of Cancer Growth via Chemo-fluid Coupling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10014)
*Celia Taboada,Pedro Navas,Miguel Molinos*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种基于物理信息神经网络（PINN）的框架，用于肿瘤生长的化学-流体连续介质模型。通过解耦固体力学平衡，得到一个三方程系统（肿瘤体积分数的对流-扩散-反应方程 + 准静态达西压力方程），网络可同时求解正问题（仅从物理约束学习状态变量）和反问题（从稀疏含噪数据恢复隐藏的传输参数）。正问题与高分辨率有限差分参考解对比，平均绝对误差低于0.002；反问题中，在初始渗透率估计偏差4倍、仅5%空间稀疏观测且含5%高斯噪声的条件下，渗透率恢复的相对误差低于5%。

Motivation: 肿瘤进展是多物理场耦合现象，涉及间质流体动力学、生化输运和细胞力学的多尺度相互作用。经典网格求解器尽管精确，但在逆参数识别和患者特异性预测管线所需的重复评估中计算成本过高，亟需高效可扩展的替代方案。

Method: 引入PINN框架求解一个可处理的化学-流体肿瘤生长连续模型，该模型将肿瘤体积分数的对流-扩散-反应方程与准静态达西压力方程耦合。通过有意解耦固体力学平衡简化系统，确保梯度结构在自动微分下稳定。网络同时学习正问题（状态变量）和反问题（用DA-PINN从稀疏含噪合成测量中恢复隐藏输运参数）。

Result: 正问题验证中，与高分辨率有限差分参考解对比，平均绝对误差低于0.002。反问题中，从初始渗透率估计0.08（真实值0.02的4倍偏差）出发，仅使用5%空间稀疏且含5%高斯噪声的观测，DA-PINN将渗透率恢复的相对误差控制在5%以内。

Conclusion: 物理信息深度学习是可行的、计算高效的肿瘤多物理场建模途径，为未来将物理约束深度学习方法整合到临床数据同化管线奠定了数学基础。

Abstract: Tumor progression is an inherently multiphysical phenomenon in which interstitial fluid dynamics, biochemical transport, and cellular mechanics interact across multiple spatiotemporal scales. Classical mesh-based solvers, although accurate, impose prohibitive computational costs for the repeated evaluations demanded by inverse parameter identification and future patient-specific predictive pipelines. In this work we introduce a Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) framework for a tractable chemo-fluidic continuum model of tumor growth that couples an advection-diffusion-reaction (ADR) equation for the tumor volume fraction with a quasi-static Darcy pressure equation for the interstitial fluid pressure. By intentionally decoupling the solid-mechanical equilibrium, we obtain a three-equation system whose gradient structure is stable under automatic differentiation, enabling robust deep-learning optimization. The network simultaneously learns both state variables from physics constraints alone (forward problem) and recovers hidden transport parameters from sparse, noisy synthetic measurements (Data-Assimilation PINN, DA-PINN, inverse problem). We verify the forward solver against a high-resolution finite-difference (FD) reference, achieving a mean absolute error below 0.002. For the inverse problem, starting from an initial permeability estimate of 0.08 (a factor of 4x above the true value of 0.02) with only 5% spatially sparse observations corrupted by 5% Gaussian noise, the DA-PINN recovers the permeability with a relative error below 5%. These results demonstrate that physics-informed deep learning constitutes a viable, computationally efficient route to multiphysics oncology modeling and lays the mathematical groundwork for future integration into clinical data assimilation pipelines.

### [4] [A fast diagonalization algorithm to enable singular value decomposition of large matrices for efficient template matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10029)
*Matthew Giammar,Bronwyn Lucas,Alexander Strang*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种并行化算法，用于快速对角化任何分块循环矩阵（block-circulant matrix）。该算法利用问题对称性对特征函数的约束，将存储成本降低m倍，在w个工作进程下实现m²加速比。在模板匹配和cryo-EM图像分解应用中，该算法分别实现了205倍加速和25倍时间节省。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 许多计算问题具有三个特征：(1)若能对角化线性算子则可高效求解；(2)由于规模过大无法直接对角化；(3)算子与置换可交换（因坐标对称或分块循环结构）。例如高分辨率模板匹配需要对大规模高分辨率图像与大量相关模板进行重复卷积，虽然可通过低秩近似压缩，但对角化代价高昂。需要利用对称性约束来降低计算复杂度。

Method: 提出一种并行化算法，对任何分块循环矩阵进行快速对角化。当索引空间可划分为l类、每类m个可互换元素时，利用对称性约束特征函数，将存储成本降低m倍。算法利用w个worker并行计算，每worker的浮点运算复杂度为O(l²m log(m)/w) + O(ml³/w)。

Result: 在高精度模板匹配矩阵分解中，快速流程比传统方法快205倍（每个恢复特征），同时恢复22.5倍的特征数量。在典型模板匹配矩阵上，分解时间比计算矩阵元素本身快25倍。在cryo-EM图像（2Å分辨率）的完整投影集分解中，约30倍规模更大的矩阵仅需14分钟即可完成。

Conclusion: 该算法比现有方法更稳定，可恢复任意精度的面内旋转，有效解决了大规模对称计算问题中对角化效率低下的瓶颈。

Abstract: Many computational problems possess the following three features: (1) the problem could be solved efficiently if a linear operator could be diagonalized, (2) direct diagonalization is infeasible due to scale, but, (3) the operator is known to commute with a permutation since the problem is (a) symmetric with respect to a change of coordinates, or (b) the operator is block circulant. For example, high-resolution template matching demands repeated convolution of large, high resolution images with an exhaustive list of related templates. The associated linear operator could be compressed, via a low rank approximation, if diagonalized. The required decomposition expensive, but, the entire problem is symmetric to in-plane rotations. In these cases, the eigenfunctions are constrained by the symmetry. These constraints allow efficient decomposition. When the index space can be partitioned into $l$ classes of $m$ interchangeable elements, the algorithm reduces storage costs by a factor of $m$ and, if $w$ workers are available, the algorithm demands $\mathcal{O}(l^2m\log(m)/w) + \mathcal{O}(ml^3/w)$ floating point operations per worker yielding a $m^2$ speedup. We use this procedure to decompose a high precision template matching matrix. We compare runtime on a reduced problem, where the fast procedure ran 205 times faster per recovered feature, for 22.5 times as many features. On a typical template matching matrix, the decomposition required 25 times less time than computing the matrix entries. We also demonstrate decomposition of a $\sim$30 times larger matrix that covers the complete set of possible projections represented in a cryo-EM image at 2~Å resolution in 14 minutes. This procedure is more stable and allows recovery of in-plane rotations to arbitrary precision.

### [5] [An Information Theory Analysis of Whole Slide Image Pathology AI and Diagnostic Field Selection AI Under Limited Resources](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10846)
*Tatsuaki Tsuruyama*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本研究比较了两种在有限计算资源下处理病理图像的方式：WSI-AI（AI自动压缩全切片图像信息）和DFS-AI（专家先选择诊断所需区域、倍数和对比，AI再分析选定视野）。通过三类图像模型（罕见局灶病变、非均匀背景中的病灶检测、空间连续病变）的对比发现，两种方法的相对性能随粗视图位置信息、背景异质性、局部上下文和病变空间结构系统性变化，并提出了根据诊断任务信息结构选择方法的实用策略。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: AI用于病理诊断的关键问题在于应该向AI提供什么图像信息，以及如何在有限的分析资源下最有效地利用这些资源。不同信息结构和病变类型下，AI处理方式的选择缺乏系统性理解。

Method: 构建三种图像模型进行比较：（1）罕见局灶性病变；（2）非均匀背景中的病灶检测（如寻找感染性病原体）；（3）空间连续病变。分别用WSI-AI（AI自动处理全切片图像）和DFS-AI（专家预先选择视野后由AI分析）两种处理方式在有限资源条件下进行对比评估。

Result: WSI-AI在粗视图无法提供足够病变位置信息时表现更好（如淋巴结中非常小的肿瘤灶）；DFS-AI在低成本的粗视图能提供有用位置信息时，在中等有限资源范围内表现更好；非均匀背景下搜索特定对象时DFS-AI相对价值增加；对于空间连续病变，病变越大WSI-AI越容易发现存在，但DFS-AI利用连续结构选择分析视野，在完整刻画整个病变方面可能更优。

The second is Diagnostic Field Selection (DFS)-AI in which an expert first selects several regions, magnifications, and comparisons needed for diagnosis, and the AI then analyzes those selected fields. To compare these two types, we built three image models: a rarely localized lesion, lesion detection in a nonuniform background, and a spatially continuous lesion. As a result, WSI-AI was better when a coarse view did not give enough information about lesion location in advance, as may occur with very small tumor foci in lymph nodes. In contrast, when a low-cost coarse view provided useful location information, DFS-AI was better in an intermediate range of limited resources. When searching for a specific object such as infectious organism in a nonuniform background, the relative value of DFS-AI increased. However, for complete characterization of the entire lesion, DFS-AI could be better because it used the continuous structure to select fields for analysis. In conclusion, both relative performance changes in a systematic way with location information from the coarse view, background heterogeneity, local context, and the spatial structure of the lesion. A practical strategy under limited resources is for physicians to choose between WSI-AI and DIS-AI according to the information structure of the diagnostic task.
