Result: 全局扩散可解析得到平衡点及其局部稳定性；两种邻居扩散下种群先逐步扩张后趋于稳定平衡。随机邻居扩散下，人类化对迁移的影响比其对动物容纳量的影响对稳态密度作用更大；受限邻居扩散下扩散明显更慢，且许多斑块维持极稀疏种群。入侵速度方面，随机邻居扩散下新增人类化区域缩短入侵时间，而受限邻居扩散下人类化区域提高或不影响入侵时间；以拉伸行波近似，人类化区域依扩散类型充当"入侵高速公路"或"入侵屏障"。

Conclusion: 人类化景观对媒介入侵的作用方向取决于动物扩散方式，既可能加速也可能阻碍入侵；亟需更多动物扩散的经验数据来支撑这类模型的预测。

Abstract: We develop a multi-patch model for sylvatic hematophagous vector--(wild) animal interactions with nonlinear migration terms that depend on landscape anthropization.
  Every patch is a suitable habitat for both animals and vectors, but anthropization reduces the animal carrying capacity and induces animal and vector displacement. We considered three dispersal types. Under unconstrained global dispersal, displaced individuals can move to any other patch. Under the more realistic random and constrained neighbor dispersal, displaced animals move to a neighboring patch chosen either at random or preferentially among less anthropized ones.
  We parameterized our model for tsetse flies (Glossina spp.) in the districts of Campo, Nyé'été, and Ma'an, Ocean division, Cameroon. We characterized the equilibria and their local stability analytically for unconstrained global dispersal, and found numerically that, under both neighbor dispersal regimes, populations progressively expand before settling into a stable equilibrium. Under random neighbor dispersal, the effect of anthropization on migration had a much larger impact on steady state densities than its effect on animal carrying capacity. Under constrained neighbor dispersal, populations spread noticeably more slowly, and numerous patches maintained extremely sparse populations. We further investigated invasion speed under different anthropization scenarios. For random neighbor dispersal, invasion times decreased upon introduction of newly anthropized regions, while for constrained neighbor dispersal, anthropized regions either increased or did not affect invasion times. Approximating invasions by pulled traveling waves, we found that anthropized regions act as ``invasion highways'' or ``invasion barriers'' depending on the dispersal type. We conclude that more empirical data on animal dispersal are needed to understand these dynamics.

### [8] [A predictive scaling framework for hierarchical living systems: From biomacromolecule to the Anthropogenic Earth system](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33965)
*Jie Changa,Ying Ge,Xiaoyu Hou,Zhaoping Wu,Yong Min,Feng Mao,Guofu Yang*

Main category: q-bio.OT

TL;DR: 论文提出HELIX模型，将生命系统14个已知层级按16个性状聚类为自主型、组分型、重复自主型三类，并把层级间定量性状的指数标度耦合入性状分类，形成螺旋带状交替、周期自相似且遵循标度律的统一框架，并预测三个新层级。

Motivation: 生命系统从生物大分子到生物圈呈层级组织，定量性状（尺度、质量、寿命）跨多个数量级，而结构等定性性状在相邻层级间不同、非相邻层级间相似却仅被零散认识；尚缺乏同时整合定量与定性性状的跨层级统一框架。

Method: 构建HELIX模型：以跨层级的指数标度耦合"基于性状的分类"，用16个性状对14个已知生命层级进行聚类分型，考察组内自相似性、组间周期性交替（螺旋带）以及定量性状的标度规律。

Result: 14个层级被分为自主型（Autonomous）、组分型（Component）、重复自主型（Repeat-autonomous）三类；同组层级自相似，三组在螺旋带中周期性交替，定量性状遵循标度律。

Conclusion: HELIX整合多学科既有原理形成统一理论，并进一步预测三个新出现的层级，可用于指导生命系统的跨层级与跨学科研究。

Abstract: Living systems, from biomacromolecules to the biosphere, are organized hierarchically with quantitative traits (such as size, mass and lifespan) spanning many orders of magnitude. However, qualitative traits such as structure differ between adjacent levels, and similarities among non-adjacent levels have been recognized only case by case. Yet no framework integrates quantitative and qualitative traits across the hierarchy of living systems. Here, we propose HELIX, a model that couples exponential scaling across levels with trait-based classification. Clustered by 16 traits, the 14 known levels are classified into three groups: Autonomous, Component, and Repeat-autonomous living systems. Levels within each group are self-similar, and the three groups alternate periodically in a helical belt and the quantitative traits follow scaling law. HELIX integrates existing principles from multiple disciplines to form a unified theory. HELIX further predicts three newly emerged levels, illustrating that it can guide cross-level and interdisciplinary research on living systems.
