### [1] [Dynamics of a Harvested Predator-Prey Model with a Generalized Holling Type~III Response Capturing Group Defense](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32306)
*Wael El Khateeb,Farjana Akter,Chanaka Kottegoda,Muner Mustafa Abou Hasan*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 研究带常数捕获率和广义Holling III型功能反应的捕食者-食饵模型，允许参数b取负值（高猎物密度下捕食效率下降，反映群体防御），揭示新的共存平衡点、余维三Bogdanov–Takens分支、余维三幂零鞍点展开以及余维二广义Hopf分支与多极限环。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统Holling III型功能反应假设b为正，忽略高猎物密度下捕食效率可能下降的现象；作者希望探讨b为负时（群体防御）对系统动力学与种群持续性的影响，并厘清收获强度如何与群体防御相互作用。

Method: 构建含常数猎物收获和广义Holling III型功能反应的捕食者-食饵模型；在b<0情形下进行平衡点分析，证明余维三Bogdanov–Takens分支的存在性，推导余维三幂零鞍点展开，并分析余维二广义Hopf分支及多极限环的产生。

Result: b<0时系统出现额外的共存平衡点及新的分支机制；证实存在余维三Bogdanov–Takens分支与相应的幂零鞍点展开，并存在余维二广义Hopf分支，可产生多个极限环。

Conclusion: 中等强度收获会削弱高猎物丰度带来的保护作用；过度收获使猎物过少而无法支撑捕食者增长；低强度收获则保留较强的群体防御，此时种群持续需要较高的捕食者密度。

Abstract: We investigate a predator-prey model with constant prey harvesting and a generalized Holling type III functional response, focusing on the biological and dynamical consequences of allowing the parameter $b$ to be negative. In this regime, predation efficiency declines at high prey densities, potentially reflecting group defense. This change permits an additional coexistence equilibrium and introduces new bifurcation mechanisms affecting population persistence. We establish the existence of a codimension-three Bogdanov--Takens bifurcation, derive a codimension-three nilpotent-saddle unfolding, and investigate a codimension-two generalized Hopf bifurcation and the emergence of multiple limit cycles. Our analysis show that moderate harvesting weakens the protection conferred by high prey abundance; excessive harvesting leaves too few prey to sustain predator growth; and low harvesting preserves strong group defense, requiring high predator density for population persistence.

### [2] [Stochastic predator-prey compartment model with cyclic and non-cyclic transition pathways and mortality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32535)
*Thomas M. Michelitsch,Ayten D. Baher,Michael Bestehorn,Andrzej F. Nowakowski,Alejandro P. Riascos,Bernard A. Collet*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 研究带症状前传染性与疾病死亡率的随机SEIRS-SEID隔室模型，隔室停留时间服从任意分布（尤其Weibull），分析循环与非循环传播路径的平衡点稳定性，并揭示停留时间分布与分数阶算子的联系。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 经典隔室模型通常假设指数分布的停留时间，忽略症状前传染性、时变死亡率以及记忆效应；作者希望更真实地刻画停留时间的任意分布如何影响传染病动力学与随机演化方程的形式。

Method: 构建S-E-I-R-D五状态随机隔室模型，含症状前感染与疾病死亡；区分循环路径（有限停留时间+暂时免疫）与非循环路径（永久免疫）；用Hopf分岔分析平衡点稳定性；通过停留时间分布推导分数阶演化方程；用Monte Carlo-Runge-Kutta混合仿真与Barabasi-Albert网络随机游走验证。

Result: 无死亡情形下，循环路径在传播阈值以上存在地方性平衡，或全局稳定或振荡（Hopf）不稳定；非循环路径趋于无病平稳态。厚尾等待时间分布必然导致分数阶导数。得到个体存活感染循环次数的期望显式表达式，仿真验证了模型。

Conclusion: 隔室停留时间分布决定了模型的稳定性行为与演化方程类型，厚尾分布引入分数阶动力学；模型可推广至野火蔓延、化学反应、种群动力学、金融、经济与生物学等跨学科领域。

Abstract: We study variants of a stochastic SEIRS-SEID compartmental model incorporating presymptomatic infectiousness and disease-related mortality, with independent compartmental sojourn times drawn from arbitrary distributions, in particular Weibull distributions. We consider a large population of agents that occupy the states (compartments) S (susceptible to infection), E (exposed: infected but asymptomatic during the latency period and already infectious, including diseases with presymptomatic infections), I (infected and infectious, with symptoms), R (recovered, immune, and non-infectious), or D (dead). We consider (i) cyclic transmission pathways, where compartmental sojourn times are finite and immunity is temporary, and (ii) non-cyclic pathways, where immunity is permanent. In the absence of mortality, cyclic transmission pathways support an endemic equilibrium above the spreading threshold which is either globally stable or oscillatory (Hopf) unstable, whereas for non-cyclic pathways a stationary disease-free state is emerging. From a stochastic perspective, we elucidate the connection between compartmental sojourn-time distributions and the appearance of fractional operators in the evolution equations: fat-tailed compartment waiting-time distributions necessarily lead to fractional derivatives. For cyclic transition pathways we obtain an explicit expression for the expected number of infection cycles that an agent survives. Our model is validated by a combined Monte Carlo -- Runge -- Kutta simulation scheme and random-walk simulations on Barabasi-Albert networks. It has cross-disciplinary applications beyond epidemiology, including wildfire spreading, certain classes of chemical reactions, population dynamics, finance, economics, and biology.

### [3] [Kinetic Proofreading Activation Times Beyond the Weibull Limit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31788)
*Thomas Mourier,Cameron Hopkinson,Kenny Wong*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 该论文指出常用的Weibull型首激活时间近似忽略了kTcell动力学校对所依赖的解离率koff，并以无量纲数Λ界定其适用范围，提出保留koff的闭式解。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: T细胞需在大量自身pMHC中识别极少数外来pMHC，动力学校对（kinetic proofreading）机制以koff区分配体；但多受体同时结合时，首激活时间常用Weibull律近似，该式不含koff，无法反映校对机制的核心判别依据，其可靠性缺乏定量判据。

Method: 采用底层连续时间马尔可夫链的精确首达时分布，并结合随机模拟；定义无量纲数Λ（以nKP+1个结合态寿命为单位度量的Weibull预测激活时间）来量化Weibull律的相对误差；进而推导将"成功通过校对链"与"失败尝试所耗时间"分离处理的闭式表达式，其中保留koff，仅在需要时做一维求根。

Result: Λ<1时首个激活受体在配体解离前完成激活，Weibull律成立；Λ增大后激活越来越依赖反复解离—再结合，真实激活时间在参数网格上最多超出Weibull预测2900倍，有效Weibull形状参数降至1，激活由"计时器式"变为无记忆。新闭式解在25组参数中有19组与精确中位数偏差在2%以内，最差约6%，并再现了计时行为的丧失。

Conclusion: Λ由Weibull预测本身计算得出，可事前判断该近似是否可信；在不可信区域，新表达式只需一维求根即可给出含koff的准确激活时间刻画。

Abstract: T cells can detect a few foreign peptide-major histocompatibility complexes (pMHCs) among a far larger number of self pMHCs. Kinetic proofreading explains this discrimination: a receptor that has bound its ligand must complete nKP signaling steps, each at rate kp, before it activates, and dissociation at any step resets its progress. When many receptors are engaged at once, the time to the first activation has been approximated by a Weibull law taken from the short-time limit of this process. The Weibull law is convenient, but it does not contain the dissociation rate koff, even though koff is what proofreading uses to tell ligands apart. Using the exact first- passage law of the underlying continuous-time Markov chain together with stochastic simulations, we show that the relative error of the Weibull law is set by a single dimensionless number Λ, the Weibull-predicted activation time measured in units of nKP + 1 bound-state lifetimes. For Λ < 1 the first receptor to activate does so before its ligand has ever dissociated, and the Weibull law holds. As Λ grows, activation relies increasingly on repeated unbinding and rebinding. The true activation time then exceeds the Weibull prediction by up to 2900-fold on our parameter grid, and the effective Weibull shape falls to one, so activation becomes memoryless instead of timer-like. We derive a closed form that keeps koff by treating the successful pass through the proofreading chain separately from the time lost to failed attempts. It matches exact medians to within 2% in 19 of 25 parameter sets, with a worst case of about 6%, and reproduces the loss of timer-like behavior. Because Λ is computed from the Weibull prediction itself, it shows in advance how far that formula can be trusted; where it cannot, the new expression requires only a one-dimensional root find.

</details>


### [4] [Shared clones make random cell halves look reproducible in pooled screens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32962)
*Xuanrui Huang*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文指出， pooled 扰动筛选（如 Perturb-seq）中按随机对半划分细胞来评估可重复性的做法存在伪重复问题：两半细胞来自同一批转导克隆，导致基准与可靠性标签被高估；应采用独立转导间比较来评估可重复性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 单细胞扰动筛选被用于训练和评估预测扰动效应的模型，模型得分通常以效应测量的可重复性为判据。常规做法是把每个扰动的细胞随机分为两半，并假定两半是独立重复。但在 Perturb-seq 这类 pooled 筛选中，携带 guide RNA 的细胞源自数量有限的转导细胞，随机两半实际上包含相同克隆，构成数据中未标记的伪重复，可能系统性高估可重复性和模型性能。

Method: 以应为无效应的扰动作为阴性对照，比较随机对半划分与来自不同转导的独立重复；并直接构造仅克隆组成不同、其他条件相同的对半样本。在基因组规模的原代 T 细胞筛选中检验；进一步利用 T 细胞受体恒定区或克隆条码，把细胞划分为不共享克隆的两半，验证超额一致性是否消失。最后跨 16 个筛选量化克隆效应大小，并考察模型在"输入与评分答案同源转导"与"跨转导"两种设定下的表现差异。

Result: 在基因组规模原代 T 细胞筛选中，无效扰动在随机对半间几乎总是一致，而在独立重复间仅约一半一致（即噪声预期水平）；增加测量细胞数并未消除这种一致性。按 TCR 恒定区或克隆条码划分出无共享克隆的两半后，超额一致性大幅消失。克隆效应量约等于克隆决定的表达差异占比除以每个扰动的有效克隆数；跨 16 个筛选，该效应在转入小鼠的 T 细胞和原代 T 细胞筛选中最大，细胞系中小 1–2 个数量级，非分裂神经元中最小。共享克隆抬高了基准上限和可靠性标签；当模型输入与评分答案来自同一转导时，多数已发表的跨细胞状态扰动预测模型优于"忽略扰动"的基线，而面对另一转导的细胞时多数低于该基线。

Conclusion: 随机对半划分掩盖了克隆共享带来的伪重复，导致可重复性、基准上限与模型可靠性被高估，尤其在原代细胞和体内场景中。可重复性应在独立转导之间评估，基准构建与模型评价需明确区分克隆来源，否则跨细胞状态泛化的结论可能不成立。

Abstract: Single-cell perturbation screens are used to train and evaluate models that predict perturbation effects, and model scores are judged against how reproducible the measured effects are. Reproducibility is usually judged by splitting each perturbation's cells into two random halves. This assumes that the halves are independent replicates. In pooled screens such as Perturb-seq, however, the cells carrying a guide RNA descend from a limited number of transduced cells, so two random halves contain the same clones: pseudoreplication whose unit is not marked in the data. Using perturbations that should have no effect as controls, we compared random halves with independent replicates from separate transductions, and tested directly with halves that differ only in their clones. In a genome-scale screen of primary T cells, null perturbations agreed between random halves almost always and between independent replicates only half the time, the rate expected from noise alone; measuring more cells did not remove this agreement. Splitting the cells into halves that share no clone, by the T-cell receptor constant region or by a clone barcode, largely removed the excess agreement. Its size equals the share of expression differences set by clones divided by the effective number of clones per perturbation; across 16 screens it was largest in T cells transferred into mice and in a genome-scale primary T-cell screen, one to two orders of magnitude smaller in cell lines, and smallest in non-dividing neurons. Shared clones inflate benchmark ceilings and reliability labels, and when a model's input and its scoring answer come from one transduction, published models that predict perturbation effects in a new cell state appear better than a baseline that ignores the perturbation; against cells from another transduction, most fall below it. Reproducibility should be judged across separate transductions.

</details>
