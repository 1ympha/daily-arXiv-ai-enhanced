### [1] [The Shape of Individual Fitness Distributions and the Dynamics of Ecological Communities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24047)
*Julie Rowlett,Leo Tingqvist*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文提出一个可视为Hubbell中性理论扩展的模型：目标物种与局域群落中其余所有物种的集合体竞争，每个个体具有自身的竞争能力，并以竞争能力分布（而非单一物种层面数值）刻画，在同质混合、对称且无平局的竞争下退化为中性模型；再引入来自更大集合群落的迁入，对目标物种相对丰度导出扩散近似，表明均值与方差相同的竞争能力分布仍可产生不同的局域丰度格局。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 群落生态学的核心目标是建立能预测物种丰度、分布与波动的生物地理学和生物多样性机制性理解。中性理论假设物种在生态上等价，而现实中个体存在竞争能力差异；论文希望打通"个体层面变异"与"种群层面持久性与丰度"之间的联系，并考察竞争与迁入如何共同塑造生态结果，特别是检验"以性状统计量被概括为等价的物种，是否真的生态等价"。

Method: 构建局域固定大小群落中的目标物种模型：焦点物种与其余所有物种的集合体竞争，每个个体携带自己的竞争能力，模型以竞争能力的分布形式纳入而非单个物种级取值。采用完全混合、对称且不发生平局（no ties）的竞争设定；进一步引入来自更大集合群落的迁入，推导焦点物种相对丰度的扩散近似。

Result: 在完全混合、对称竞争且无平局的条件下，模型恢复到Hubbell的中性模型，构成一致性检验。分析显示，均值与方差相同的竞争能力分布仍可产生不同的局域丰度格局，说明仅凭性状统计量（如均值和方差）判定为等价的物种，未必在生态上真正等价。

Conclusion: 该框架将个体层面的竞争能力变异与种群层面的持久性和丰度联系起来，提供了考察竞争与迁入如何共同决定生态结果的一般性工具；同时提示以统计量刻画物种等价性的做法存在局限，个体水平的能力分布形状本身具有生态后果。

Abstract: Developing a mechanistic understanding of biogeography and biodiversity that predicts species abundances, distributions, and fluctuations is a central goal of community ecology. We develop a model, that can be seen as an extension of Hubbell's neutral theory, in which a focal species competes with the aggregate of all other species in a local community of fixed size. Each individual has its own competitive ability, and the shape of the distribution of these abilities is incorporated in the model, represented by distributions rather than single species-level values. Under complete mixing, symmetric competitive encounters without ties recover Hubbell's neutral model. We further incorporate immigration from a larger metacommunity and derive a diffusion approximation for the focal species' relative abundance. The model shows that competitive ability distributions with the same mean and variance can nevertheless yield different patterns of local abundance. Thus, species that appear equivalent when summarized by trait statistics need not be ecologically equivalent. Our framework links individual-level variability to population-level persistence and abundance and provides a way to examine how competition and immigration jointly shape ecological outcomes.

### [2] [A multilayer network-based modelling framework for epizootic haemorrhagic disease virus transmission in France](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24620)
*Viney Kumar,Gaël Beaunée,Sandie Arnoux,Fabien Corbière,Benoît Durand,Hélène Cecilia*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 该研究利用为蓝舌病开发的随机、空间显式房室模型，重建了2023–24年法国本土牛群中EHDV-8疫情，并用罐装牛奶血清阳性率数据通过ABC-SMC完成校准。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: EHDV-8于2023年9月在法国出现，但其传播动力学特征尚不清楚，需要在全国尺度上重建疫情传播格局，并为未来疫苗接种策略的评估与优化提供基础框架。

Method: 将原本用于蓝舌病的随机、空间显式房室模型改造用于EHDV-8；模型以行政区（canton）为单位，纳入牛群人口学、牧草覆盖、相对库蠓丰度及温度依赖的媒介过程；用三个网络刻画区间传播：短程媒介扩散（牧场网络）、同一农场内牧场间的非商业性牛只移动（农场网络）和牛只贸易（贸易网络）；采用ABC-SMC，以罐装牛奶检测得到的canton-周血清阳性率估计值进行校准，并开展基于模拟的参数可识别性检验。

Result: 校准缩小了基线媒介传播参数的可信范围，并倾向于农场网络具有更高的移动强度（但移动强度仍存在不确定性）；基于模拟的参数恢复显示，校准通常能识别出可再现与伪观测数据相当疫情格局的参数区域，但预测表现因数据集而异，且一般低于使用生成参数时的表现；应用于实际观测数据时，模型再现了采样canton周中位血清阳性率的若干特征，但部分采样时点仍存在偏差，预测不确定性较大。

Conclusion: 研究表明罐装牛奶监测可用于在全国尺度传播框架内重建EHDV-8疫情格局，该框架为未来疫苗接种策略的评估和优化提供了基础。

Abstract: Epizootic haemorrhagic disease virus serotype 8 (EHDV-8) emerged in France in September 2023 but its transmission dynamics remain poorly characterised. We adapted a stochastic, spatially explicit compartmental model developed for bluetongue to reconstruct the 2023-24 EHDV-8 epidemic in cattle in mainland France. The model represented transmission across cantons, incorporating cattle demography, pasture cover, relative Culicoides abundance and temperature-dependent vector processes. Three networks represented between-canton spread through short-range vector dispersal (pasture network), non-commercial cattle movements between pastures of the same farm (farm network), and cattle trade (trade network). Using approximate Bayesian computation with sequential Monte Carlo (ABC-SMC), we calibrated the model to canton-week seroprevalence estimates derived from bulk-tank milk measurements. Calibration narrowed the plausible range of the baseline vector-mediated transmission parameter and favoured higher movement intensities through the farm network, although movement intensity remained uncertain. Simulation-based parameter recovery showed that calibration generally identified parameter regions capable of reproducing epidemic patterns comparable to the pseudo-observed data, although predictive performance varied among datasets and was typically lower than that obtained with the generating parameters. When applied to observed data, the calibrated model captured several features of the observed weekly median seroprevalence across sampled cantons, although discrepancies remained at some sampling times and predictive uncertainty was substantial. These results demonstrate how bulk-tank milk surveillance can inform the reconstruction of EHDV-8 epidemic patterns within a national-scale transmission framework. This framework provides a basis for future evaluation and optimisation of vaccination strategies.

### [3] [STORM: A Lightweight Structure-Free Model for Binding Affinity Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23777)
*Ayush Pandit,Rohan V. Koodli,Wei Deng,Roxana Daneshjou,Ron O. Dror*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 提出 STORM（Similarity Transfer from Observed Reference Molecules），一种快速、简单的结合亲和力预测模型，通过集成大量由多样蛋白与分子表示构成的小型模型融合，在相同训练数据下精度匹配或超过 Boltz-2，而推理与训练速度快五个数量级以上。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 小分子-蛋白结合亲和力预测是药物发现的关键任务。基于物理的自由能扰动（FEP）计算成本极高且需专家配置；近期基于机器学习共折叠模型（如 Boltz-2）精度接近 FEP 且已被广泛采用，但对大规模计算筛选仍昂贵，在新采集实验数据上重训练更昂贵。

Method: STORM 采用"从已观测参考分子进行相似性迁移"的思路，将多种蛋白质表示与分子表示的小模型融合进行集成（ensemble of many small model fusions），模型简单、训练与推理高效。

Result: 在相同训练数据下，STORM 在多个评估数据集上精度匹配或超过 Boltz-2，同时推理与训练速度提升超过五个数量级。结果还表明，将历史实验亲和力数据与结构、生物物理信息结合，有望进一步提升预测精度。

Conclusion: STORM 以极低成本达到甚至超越先进共折叠模型的亲和力预测精度，为大规模筛选与快速再训练提供了实用方案，并指出融合历史实验数据是提升精度的有前景方向。

Abstract: Accurately predicting a small molecule's binding affinity to a protein, a critical task in drug discovery, remains challenging. Physics-based approaches like free-energy perturbation (FEP) have very high computational costs and require expert setup. Recent machine learning--based cofolding models have reported accuracy approaching that of FEP---and at least one of them, Boltz-2, has already been widely adopted for affinity prediction---but they remain expensive for large computational screens and even more expensive for retraining on newly collected experimental data. To address these challenges, we introduce STORM: Similarity Transfer from Observed Reference Molecules. STORM is a fast, simple machine learning model that ensembles many small model fusions of diverse protein and molecule representations for binding affinity prediction. Trained on the same data, STORM matches or exceeds the accuracy of Boltz-2 on multiple evaluation datasets, while delivering a speed improvement of over five orders of magnitude for both inference and training. Our results also suggest opportunities to substantially improve affinity prediction accuracy by integrating historical experimental affinity data with structural and biophysical information.

</details>
