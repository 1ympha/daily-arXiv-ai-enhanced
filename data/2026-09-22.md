### [1] [From daylight to darkness: a nonlocal model of circadian activity cycles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22713)
*Dan Bell,Timothé Boyer,Hao Wang*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 该论文建立偏微分方程数学框架，将活动周期和日光依赖感知纳入动物运动模型，研究感知模式、日照长度对聚集强度的影响，并用博弈论分析捕食-被捕食系统的均衡睡眠策略。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 动物的感知能力及其经历的生态互动会受到自身和周围物种睡眠模式的强烈影响。感官能力有限的动物在夜间难以导航，而在捕食-被捕食系统中，被捕食者在捕食者清醒狩猎时处于不活跃状态可能不利。因此需要研究睡眠、感知与生态互动之间的关系。

Method: 使用偏微分方程建立通用数学框架，将活动周期和日光依赖的感知能力纳入已有的动物运动模型；随后将框架扩展到两物种捕食-被捕食系统，并采用博弈论方法分析各物种的均衡睡眠策略。

Result: 主导感知模式和日照长度都是决定社会性物种聚集强度的关键因素。在捕食-被捕食系统中，各物种的均衡睡眠策略不仅受自身感知能力影响，也受其生态对手感知能力的影响。根据两物种的感知能力，均衡策略可能表现为单一睡眠模式，也可能是多种睡眠模式的组合。

Conclusion: 感知模式、日照长度和生态对手的感知能力共同塑造动物的睡眠与聚集行为，均衡睡眠策略可以是单一模式或多种模式的组合，为理解睡眠、感知与生态互动提供了一般性理论框架。

Abstract: An animal's ability to perceive its surroundings, as well as the ecological interactions it experiences, can be strongly influenced by the sleep patterns of both itself and surrounding species. Animals with limited sensory capabilities may struggle to navigate their habitat at night, while in a predator-prey system, it may be detrimental for prey to be inactive while predators are awake and hunting. Here, we develop a general mathematical framework to study these interactions using partial differential equations, incorporating activity cycles and daylight-dependent perception into existing models of animal movement. We show that both the dominant sensory mode and the length of daylight are key factors determining the strength of aggregation in social species. Extending this framework to a two-species predator-prey system, we use game-theoretic approaches to show that the equilibrium sleep strategy of each species is shaped not only by its own sensory capabilities, but also by those of its ecological opponent. Depending on the sensory capabilities of the two species, equilibrium strategies may consist of a single sleep pattern or a combination of multiple sleep patterns.

### [2] [Demographic inference of pathogen-infected populations from partially observed transmission forests](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23624)
*Matthew Hall*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文将传播树与抽样框的交集建模为标记有根森林，利用全子式矩阵树定理推导出独立节点集森林数的闭式解，从而得到样本中无关联个体（singletons）的概率及任意聚类配置的似然，无需假设病原动力学，可用于功效计算、均匀抽样检验和人口学推断。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 基因组流行病学通常将无采样邻居的个体（singletons）排除在分析之外，但作者认为这些孤立个体本身携带信息。样本中关联与未关联个体的比例取决于抽样框大小和独立谱系引入次数，因此这种平衡本身就是关于这些量的数据，值得被正式建模利用。

Method: 将传播树与抽样框的交集视为标记有根森林，从中均匀随机抽取固定数量节点。运用全子式矩阵树定理（all-minors matrix-tree theorem），推导N个节点上指定节点集为独立的标记有根k-森林数量的闭式表达式，进而得到给定大小样本中无任何关联个体的概率，以及任意观测聚类配置的似然（分簇内传播结构已知和仅知簇大小两种情形）。

Result: 获得了样本不含任何关联个体概率的闭式解。该方法接近调查抽样或标记重捕，信息仅来自单一样本的连接结构，不涉及病原动力学假设。文中概述了三个应用：前瞻性研究的功效计算、对"将簇解读为传播热点"所依赖的均匀抽样假设的检验，以及人口学推断。

Conclusion: 孤立个体（singletons）具有信息价值，应纳入分析而非舍弃。基于森林模型的闭式结果提供了一种不依赖病原动力学、类似调查抽样的推断框架。作者最后列出了更灵活实现所需放宽的假设。

Abstract: Genomic epidemiology has several methods for identifying which sampled individuals are linked by close proximity in the transmission chain, whether by grouping them into clusters under a genetic distance threshold or by identifying probable direct transmission pairs. Individuals found to have no sampled neighbours---singletons---are usually set aside. We argue that they are informative. Whether any two sampled individuals prove to be linked depends on the size of the sampling frame and the number of independent lineage introductions into it, and thus the balance of linked and unlinked individuals is itself data about these quantities.
  We formalise this by treating the intersection of a transmission tree with a sampling frame as a labelled rooted forest, from which a fixed number of nodes are sampled uniformly at random. Using the all-minors matrix-tree theorem, we derive a closed-form expression for the number of labelled rooted $k$-forests on $N$ nodes in which a specified set of nodes is independent. From this we obtain, again in closed form, the probability that a sample of a given size contains no linked individuals at all, and the likelihood of an arbitrary observed configuration of clusters, both when the transmission structure within each is known, and when only the cluster sizes are.
  The approach is closer to a survey, or to mark-recapture, than to conventional phylodynamic model fitting: the information comes from the linkage structure of a single sample alone with no assumptions regarding pathogen dynamics. We outline three applications: power calculations for prospective studies, a test of the uniform sampling assumption that underlies the reading of clusters as transmission hotspots, and demographic inference itself. We finally set out the assumptions that a more flexible implementation would need to relax.

</details>


### [3] [Phylogenetic Inference and the Stickiness of Fréchet Means, via Precise Asymptotics of an Embedded Random Walk](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24816)
*Adam Quinn Jaffe*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文研究BHV树空间中Fréchet均值树的"粘性"现象，通过嵌入多维随机走来刻画粘性发生时刻，并提出非参数方法估计三叉分支未来分裂的概率，应用于灵长类等物种树的系统发育问题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在系统发育树的统计中，Fréchet均值树常出现多叉分支（多分叉），需判断这反映真实的群体水平分支（硬多分叉）还是仅为采样变异（软多分叉）。这是非欧几里得统计中"粘性"现象的一个实例，即样本Fréchet均值在非正曲率分层空间中被永久困于低维分层。

Method: 识别嵌入Fréchet均值过程中的一个多维随机游走，并证明粘性发生时刻由该随机游走坐标的最大末次通过时间（last-passage time）是否超过零决定。基于此表示，构建完全非参数的程序来估计三叉分支在未来获得更多观测后发生分裂的概率。

Result: 建立了粘性时刻与随机游走末次通过时间之间的等价刻画，并给出估计三叉分支未来分裂概率的非参数方法；将其应用于灵长类、啮齿类与树鼩的物种树中观察到的三叉分支是否在群体水平上真实存在的系统发育问题。

Conclusion: 该工作为判断Fréchet均值树中多分叉源自群体结构还是采样噪声提供了理论工具与实用方法，并将粘性问题与随机游走的末次通过时间联系起来，为系统发育及其他非欧统计应用提供新视角。

Abstract: A well-known phenomenon in statistical analyses of populations of phylogenetic trees in the Billera-Holmes-Vogtmann space is that the topology of the Fréchet mean tree can contain multifurcations (i.e., internal nodes with more than two children), which raises the practical question of whether this reflects a population-level branching structure (hard polytomy) or merely sampling variability in the data (soft polytomy). This is an instance of the more general phenomenon of "stickiness" in non-Euclidean statistics, whereby the sample Fréchet mean in certain non-positively curved stratified spaces becomes permanently trapped in a lower-dimensional stratum. In this work, we identify a particular multidimensional random walk embedded within the Fréchet mean process, and we show that the time at which stickiness occurs is determined by the largest last-passage time above zero of the coordinates of this random walk. Using this representation, we develop a fully nonparametric procedure for estimating the probability that trifurcations in a sample Fréchet mean tree will bifurcate at some future time if more observations are collected. Lastly, we apply our methodology to a problem in phylogenetics where we consider whether an observed trifurcation in the species tree of primates, glires, and tree shrews is genuinely trifurcated at the population level.

### [4] [Foundation-model-based multi-label phenotyping of combined hyperkinetic movement disorders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22369)

TL;DR: 该研究将SAM 3与TabICLv2组成冻结骨干网络，实现无标记视频对八种多动性运动障碍表型的多标签分类，并在跨年龄、跨中心数据上验证了可迁移性与稳健性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 运动障碍常共病，但其现象学与严重度评估存在较大的评分者间差异。无标记视频有望提升可重复性，但既有工作多为单症状、依赖标准化采集，且缺乏跨年龄与跨站点的验证与迁移能力。

Method: 构建单一冻结骨干：用SAM 3进行逐帧稠密无标记分割并归纳为几何、轮廓与网格运动学信号；用表格基础模型TabICLv2进行上下文学习的多标签分类，识别八种多动性运动障碍表型。在21名成人患者与4名对照的标准化录制上训练，原样迁移至两个独立数据集（儿童n=12、震颤为主成人n=20），用CODY-SAMP量表评估，仅对站点层面的患者级决策步骤进行重新校准。与YOLOv8稀疏关键点方法对比。

Abstract: Movement disorders (MDs) frequently co-occur, yet phenomenological and severity assessment shows substantial inter-rater variability. Markerless video could improve reproducibility, but prior work is largely single-symptom, depends on standardized acquisition, and lacks validation and transfer across ages and sites. We combined two foundation models into one frozen backbone: Segment Anything Model 3 (SAM 3) for dense, per-frame markerless segmentation summarized into geometric, contour and grid kinematic signals, and TabICLv2, a tabular foundation model, for in-context multi-label classification of eight hyperkinetic MD phenomenologies. This frozen foundation-model backbone with light per-site calibration yields transferable, interpretable, conservative multi-label phenotyping of co-occurring hyperkinetic MDs across ages and from standardized to routine video, adding robustness on high-confidence, clinician-agreed labels. Prospective multi-centre validation is required before clinical use.

TL;DR: 该研究利用基于Ridge回归的大规模特征选择策略，从940个癌细胞系的转录组数据中预测8,423种蛋白质的丰度，识别出全局性和情境特异性的预测基因特征，为可解释的蛋白表达建模和转录后调控研究提供了框架。

The models identified stable candidate gene-protein associations that remained interpretable at the level of individual proteins and recurrent transcriptomic predictor patterns.

This framework may support hypothesis generation, protein imputation in incomplete datasets, and deeper understanding of post-transcriptional regulation in cancer biology.
