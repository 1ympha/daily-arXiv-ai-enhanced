*Maïri Souza Oliveira,Maxime Lenormand,Sandra Luque*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本研究开发了一个综合空间指标，结合开放获取的生物多样性与土地利用数据，识别生态潜力与人为压力之间的避难区和冲突区。以哥斯达黎加为案例，该指标能有效支持保护优先区规划，并评估现有保护网络的有效性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 热带森林生物多样性受到日益严重的人为威胁，但国家层面的保护规划常受限于监测框架不统一、生物多样性与人为压力数据整合不足的问题，亟需一个可操作的综合工具来指导保护决策。

Method: 利用254种优势冠层树种的物种分布模型估算生态潜力，结合土地利用模式量化人为压力，构建避难区-冲突区空间指标，并在哥斯达黎加进行国家级示范验证。

Result: 避难区与大型连续森林核心区重合，冲突区集中于破碎化景观并可能延伸至保护区内。该指标能够清晰区分需要严格保护、恢复、可持续管理或压力减缓的区域。

Conclusion: 该框架灵活可迁移，可作为实用的决策支持工具，用于评估保护网络、增强生态连通性并指导国家生物多样性规划。

Abstract: Tropical forests harbour exceptional biodiversity but are increasingly threatened by anthropogenic pressures, making their conservation central to achieving the Convention on Biological Diversity targets. However, national conservation planning is often constrained by heterogeneous monitoring frameworks and limited integration of biodiversity and anthropogenic pressures. We develop a synthetic spatial indicator that combines open-access biodiversity and land-use data to identify refuge and conflict areas between ecological potential and anthropogenic pressure. Using Costa Rica as a national-scale demonstrator, we assess its capacity to support conservation prioritisation and evaluate the current conservation network. Ecological potential was estimated using multi-species distribution models for 254 dominant canopy tree species, serving as an operational proxy for tropical forest biodiversity and ecological structure. We quantified anthropogenic pressure from land-use patterns and combined it with ecological potential to identify refuge and conflict areas. Flexible and transferable, the framework distinguishes areas requiring strict protection from those where restoration, sustainable management, or pressure mitigation should be prioritised. Rather than prescribing conservation actions, the refuge-conflict indicator provides a practical decision-support tool to assess conservation networks, strengthen ecological connectivity, and guide national biodiversity planning.

  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 为布尔网络的动力学空间赋予半环结构，从而能够系统地将全局动力学分解为各组成模块动力学的组合。

This algebraic framework provides a systematic way to analyze how local dynamical behaviors combine to produce global dynamics. ### [5] [Jointly estimating transmissibility and prior immunity from epidemic time series](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21657)
*David J. D. Earn,Todd L. Parsons*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种利用传染病流行动量守恒定律，从单一流行时间序列中分离估计病原体基本再生数(R0)和流行前易感人群比例的方法，并通过1918年流感大流行数据验证了该方法的有效性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统上，传染病时间序列模型只能推断有效再生数(Reff)，而无法将病原体传播力与人群先存免疫力分离开来，因此无法独立估计基本再生数(R0)和流行前易感比例。

Method: 利用与流行动量相关的守恒定律——即按感染潜力加权的流行率——从单一流行时间序列中解耦传播力与先存免疫力，从而分别推断R0和x^-。

Result: 通过随机流行病模拟验证了该方法，并对1918年流感大流行进行了重新评估：美国费城秋季波次的R0≈2.7，x^-≈0.8，表明约20%的人群在该波次前已具备免疫力。

Conclusion: 该方法能够从单一流行波中分别估计R0和先存免疫水平，对理解病原体传播力和人群免疫状态具有重要价值。

Abstract: Infectious disease time series are often used to estimate a pathogen's basic reproduction number, $R_0$. However, fits of epidemic models to time series conflate pathogen transmissibility with pre-existing population immunity, so only the *effective* reproduction number, $R_{eff}$, can be inferred. This composite parameter is the product of the underlying $R_0$ and the pre-epidemic susceptible fraction, $x^-$. We show that a conservation law associated with epidemic momentum---prevalence weighted by potential to infect---makes it possible to disentangle transmissibility from prior immunity and to infer $R_0$ and $x^-$ separately from a single epidemic time series. We test the methodology using stochastic epidemic simulations, and illustrate the approach with a reappraisal of influenza transmissibility during the 1918 pandemic, estimating rather than assuming the degree of prior population immunity. For the autumn wave in Philadelphia, USA, we find $R_0\approx2.7$ and $x^-\approx0.8$, implying that about 20\% of the population was already immune before that wave, plausibly as a result of infection during the spring 1918 herald wave.
