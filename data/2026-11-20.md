### [1] [Ab-initio cryo-EM reconstruction from moments of denoised images](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25525)
*Yunpeng Shi,Amit Singer,Yuguan Wang,Andy Zhang*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文提出一种基于矩量法（method of moments）的cryo-EM ab-initio三维重建方法，利用去噪图像的前三阶矩直接恢复三维初始模型，无需估计粒子取向，可快速为高分辨精修提供可靠初始化。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 单颗粒冷冻电镜高分辨三维精修依赖可靠的ab-initio初始模型。现有精修与ab-initio方法都需同时估计图像取向与三维结构，对大规模、高噪声数据集收敛慢。作者希望绕过取向估计，直接获得初始模型以加快流程。

Method: 方法基于矩量法：先对图像去噪，再计算去噪图像的前三阶矩，并由此直接恢复三维ab-initio模型，全程不进行取向估计；且不假设取向服从均匀分布。

Result: 在实验数据集上，该方法能快速生成ab-initio模型，并成功初始化高分辨精修；这是矩量法类ab-initio建模首次在不假设取向均匀分布的情况下成功应用于实验数据。

Conclusion: 绕过取向估计的矩量法为cryo-EM提供了一种快速、有效的ab-initio建模途径，可加速高分辨结构解析流程。

Abstract: In single-particle cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM), high-resolution three-dimensional (3-D) refinement requires a reliable ab-initio model to converge to the correct structure. Both refinement and existing ab-initio methods estimate the orientations of the particle images together with the 3-D structure, which can be slow for large and noisy datasets. We present a method of moments approach that bypasses orientation estimation and recovers a 3-D ab-initio model from the first three moments of denoised images. On experimental datasets, our method produces ab-initio models rapidly and successfully initializes high-resolution refinement. This is the first successful application of method-of-moments-based ab-initio modeling to experimental datasets without assuming a uniform distribution of orientations.

### [2] [The long-term divergence exponent and the temporal structure of step-to-step perturbations: a simulation study in two passive walking models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26182)
*Philippe Terrier*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 在无神经肌肉控制的被动行走模型中，长期发散指数λ_L随扰动的时间结构（ARFIMA参数d）单调增大，且与步长时距的标度指数高度相关，支持将其命名为"吸引子复杂度指数(ACI)"。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 发散指数（长期指数λ_L）被用于评估步态局部动态稳定性，人体实验已将其与步态波动的复杂性联系起来；但以往模型研究施加的扰动缺乏受控的时间结构，因此不清楚λ_L本身是否会对扰动的时间结构作出响应，以及在缺乏神经肌肉控制时是否仍然如此。

Method: 采用两个被动行走模型M1（预试验）和M2（探索性），施加阶跃式逐步扰动，覆盖5种扰动幅度σ和9种时间结构（由ARFIMA参数d刻画），并同时分析自由行走与节拍器行走的人体步幅序列（含替代数据作为对照）。

Result: 在扰动幅度固定时，两模型的λ_L均随d增大；自由行走序列的λ_L高于节拍器序列及替代序列；λ_L在M1中不随σ变化，在M2中随σ变化；λ_L与短期指数的双分离仅在M1中得到支持；描述性分析显示各条件的λ_L均值与步长时距的标度指数高度相关（M1 r=0.980，M2 r=0.906）。

Conclusion: 在力学结构固定的两个被动行走模型中，λ_L确实跟随扰动的时间结构变化，但其特异性依赖于具体模型。这一响应支持将人体实验中的λ_L命名为"吸引子复杂度指数(ACI)"。

Abstract: Purpose. Divergence exponents are used to assess the local dynamic stability of gait. Human experiments have related the long-term exponent ($λ_L$) to the complexity of gait fluctuations. Model studies used perturbations without controlled temporal structure. The question was whether $λ_L$ responds to the temporal structure of perturbations in walkers without neuromuscular control. Methods. Two passive walkers, M1 (pilot) and M2 (exploratory), received step-to-step perturbations of five amplitudes ($σ$) and nine temporal structures (ARFIMA parameter $d$), and human stride series of free and metronome walking. Results. At constant amplitude, $λ_L$ increased with $d$ in both models. It was higher with free series than with metronome series or with surrogates. It did not follow $σ$ in M1 and followed $σ$ in M2. Its double dissociation from the short-term exponent was supported in M1 only. In a descriptive analysis, the condition means of $λ_L$ correlated with the scaling exponent of the step durations ($r = 0.980$ in M1 and 0.906 in M2). Conclusion. In two passive walkers with fixed mechanics, $λ_L$ followed the temporal structure of the perturbations; its specificity depended on the model. This response supports the name attractor complexity index (ACI), proposed for $λ_L$ from human experiments.

### [3] [Fractional Order or False Memory: Identifiability of Caputo SIR Models from Incidence Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25440)
*Md Babul Hossain,Mohammad Shah Alam*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 该文检验分数阶Caputo SIR模型的阶数能否从发病率数据中识别，证明早期线性增长仅依赖两个参数组合且其KL散度有界，并在模拟与COVID-19数据中表明阶数基本不可识别。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 文献中常把分数阶模型拟合更好当作"记忆效应"的证据，但作者怀疑这种阶数提升可能只是拟合灵活性的产物，因此想追问：Caputo SIR的阶数能否真正从数据中恢复出来。

Method: 理论上分别考察传播率随时间自由变化与恒定两种情形；在恒定传播率、精确数据下推导早期线性化增长的主项，指出其只依赖增长率 r=(beta-gamma)^(1/alpha) 与幅度 beta*I0/alpha；并证明共享这两个组合的参数集对应的Poisson计数分布KL散度在任何线性期长度下均有界，非线性模型随人口增大继承该界。数值上用模拟（含过度离散、峰值前后截断、日步长离散化）以及孟加拉国和美国早期COVID-19数据做拟合与预测检验。

Result: 若允许传播率随时间自由变化，任意容许的阶数、移除率和初始计数都能精确复现数据。恒定传播率下，数据截止于峰值前两周或更早时错误阶数最多在5%重复中被拒绝，包含峰值后升至95–100%，区分仅在峰值前几天开始；轻度过度离散时需约60%人口感染才能识别阶数，更强过度离散则无法识别。早期数据无法区分的拟合可给出相差75%的峰值预测，忽略参数不确定性的plug-in预测严重过度自信；无记忆机制产生的数据在传播率变化或按日离散化时也拟合出小于1的阶数；真实COVID-19数据中0.6到1的阶数给出几乎相同的增长率。

Conclusion: 分数阶SIR模型阶数在现实发病率数据中通常不可识别，拟合出非整数阶并不能作为记忆存在的证据；相关预测需谨慎对待参数不确定性。

Abstract: When a fractional order improves the fit of an epidemic model, it is usually taken as evidence of memory. We ask whether the order of the Caputo susceptible-infectious-removed (SIR) model can actually be recovered from incidence data. If the transmission rate is allowed to vary freely in time, as in hybrid forecasting models, every admissible order, removal rate and initial count reproduces the data exactly. With constant transmission and exact data the order is identifiable in principle, but the dominant term of early linearized growth depends on only two parameter combinations: the growth rate r = (beta - gamma)^(1/alpha) and the amplitude beta*I0/alpha. What is left is transient, and it is limited. We prove that parameter sets sharing these two combinations give Poisson count distributions whose Kullback-Leibler divergence stays bounded no matter how long the linear phase is observed, and that the nonlinear model inherits this bound as the population grows. In simulations, wrong orders were rejected in at most 5% of replicates when the data ended two weeks or more before the epidemic peak, and in 95-100% once the peak was included; separation began only a few days before the peak. With mild overdispersion the order was identified only once about 60% of the population had been infected, and with stronger overdispersion not even then. Fits that the early data could not tell apart predicted peaks that differed by 75%, and plug-in forecasts that ignored parameter uncertainty were badly overconfident. Data generated without any memory produced fitted orders below one when transmission changed and when the fitted model was discretized in daily steps. In early COVID-19 data from Bangladesh and the United States, orders from 0.6 to 1 gave almost the same growth rate, whatever value we assumed for the removal rate.

</details>

### [4] [An Explainable and Scalable Hierarchical Tensor Framework for Integrating Multi-Omics and Digital Pathomics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25584)
*Ahmad Hussein,Ali Anaissi,Mukesh Prasad,Ali Braytee*

Main category: q-bio.GN

TL;DR: HIT-Fuse 是一种分层可解释张量融合框架，整合六种组学与病理图像模态，通过两阶段 CP 张量分解、基于临床协变量与观测组学的缺失模态重建，以及可数学回溯的 xAI 归因机制，在四个 TCGA 队列中实现可解释的癌症预后分层与生物学验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 精准肿瘤学需要整合异质的分子与病理数据，但现有多模态融合模型普遍存在两个问题：一是可解释性缺乏生物学依据，二是难以应对模态缺失（部分队列缺少某些组学或影像数据），导致样本利用率低、跨队列泛化受限。

Method: 构建六模态输入（体细胞拷贝数变异、DNA 甲基化、RNA 测序、miRNA 表达、RPPA 蛋白丰度、ViT 提取的病理组学特征），覆盖 TCGA 胶质瘤、肺腺癌、乳腺癌、肾癌四个队列；先用模态特异性自编码器降维压缩高维输入，再进行两阶段 CP 张量分解获得分层潜表示；对缺失的潜模态利用临床协变量与已观测组学进行重建以扩充队列并重训下游模型；最后通过数学回溯机制给出全局与局部 xAI 归因。

Result: HIT-Fuse 在胶质瘤、乳腺癌和肾癌队列中识别出具有显著生存分层能力的潜亚组，且在完整模态与重建模态两种条件下均成立；队列扩充带来队列特异的性能提升，胶质瘤加权 F1 相对提升 56.4%。生物学验证显示：解码驱动基因在 DepMap CRISPR 筛选中必需基因比例为 6.2%（胶质瘤）至 7.6%（肺癌），代表驱动包括 ANAPC11、SNRPD1、PLK1、RPL14；解码 miRNA 中 71–92% 有 miRTarBase 调控证据，所选基因中 77–84% 被 DepMap 覆盖。

Conclusion: HIT-Fuse 将分层张量融合、缺失模态重建与可追溯归因相结合，为异质队列中基于多组学与数字病理的可解释癌症预后分析提供了可行框架；其局限在于验证仍限于 TCGA 回顾性队列、重建模态引入的偏差未充分量化，且生物学证据以关联性为主，尚需前瞻性外部验证。

Abstract: Background: Precision oncology requires integration of heterogeneous molecular and pathology data, yet many fusion models offer limited biologically grounded interpretability and struggle with missing modalities.
  Methods: We present Hierarchical Interpretable Tensor Fusion (HIT-Fuse), integrating six modalities: somatic copy number variation, DNA methylation, RNA sequencing, microRNA expression, RPPA protein abundance, and vision transformer-derived pathomics across four TCGA cohorts (glioma, lung adenocarcinoma, breast, and kidney cancer). Modality-specific autoencoders compress high-dimensional inputs, followed by two-stage CP tensor decomposition. Missing latent modalities are reconstructed using clinical covariates and observed omics to expand cohorts and retrain downstream models. A mathematical traceback mechanism enables global and local xAI attributions.
  Results: HIT-Fuse identifies distinct latent subgroups with significant survival stratification in glioma, breast, and kidney cohorts, both with complete and reconstructed modalities. Cohort expansion yields cohort-specific gains, including a 56.4% relative increase in weighted F1 for glioma. Biological validation against DepMap CRISPR screens, miRTarBase and RPPA annotations supports decoded drivers across all four cohorts. Essential gene rates range from 6.2% in glioma to 7.6% in lung cancer; example drivers include ANAPC11, SNRPD1, PLK1 and RPL14. Of decoded miRNAs, 71-92% have miRTarBase regulatory support, while 77-84% of selected genes are covered in DepMap.
  Conclusions: HIT-Fuse combines hierarchical tensor fusion, missing-modality reconstruction and traceable attributions to support interpretable multi-omics and digital pathology-based cancer prognosis across heterogeneous cohorts.

</details>
