### [1] [Molecular genetics of Aedes aegypti: historical advances and oversights](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22868)
*Moshe E Jasper,Thomas L Schmidt,Ary A Hoffmann*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 该综述回顾了埃及伊蚊（Aedes aegypti）分子遗传学技术的发展历程，重点分析基因组资源的涌现如何推动并同时限制了种群遗传学与杀虫剂抗性研究，并指出文献中存在的两大空缺：全基因组数据对印太地区代表性不足，以及因重复区域序列比对困难而忽视拷贝数变异（尤其涉及谷胱甘肽S-转移酶epsilon类基因）在抗性中的作用。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 埃及伊蚊分子遗传学研究历史悠久，但近年来落后于按蚊等研究对象，原因在于防控工作对该蚊种的聚焦方式及其基因组结构复杂。随着大量个体全基因组序列公开，研究迎来"全基因组时代"，为解决长期困扰该物种的若干核心遗传学问题以及开发新的基因组分析提供了可能，因此有必要系统梳理其技术史与现状。

Method: 综述性研究：考察分子遗传技术在埃及伊蚊上的应用历史，结合历史与当代视角，分析新出现的基因组图景如何促进、又在某些情况下制约种群遗传学与杀虫剂抗性研究的发展，并据此诊断文献中的空白与偏倚。

Result: 识别出埃及伊蚊文献中的两处关键缺口：其一，相对于其登革热负担，印太地区在全基因组数据集中的代表性不足；其二，由于重复区域（特别是谷胱甘肽S-转移酶epsilon类基因附近）序列比对困难，抗性研究普遍忽视了拷贝数变异的作用。

Conclusion: 埃及伊蚊已进入全基因组分子遗传学时代，但现有研究格局存在明显的地理与基因组学偏倚。未来需加强印太地区全基因组采样，并改进重复区域序列分析以纳入拷贝数变异，从而更全面地理解抗性演化与种群遗传结构。

However, Ae. aegypti has now entered the whole-genomic era of molecular genetics, with many whole genome sequences of individuals now publicly available for analysis. These resources enable an unprecedented view on several core genetic questions that have vexed research on the species for decades. They also create the possibility for new genomic analyses to be developed in the future. In this review, we consider the history of molecular genetic technology as it applies to Ae. aegypti, with a special emphasis on how emerging pictures of the genome both enabled and in some cases constrained the development of work on population genetics and insecticide resistance. We integrate historical and contemporary perspectives to diagnose gaps and biases within the Ae. aegypti literature that require further clarification and research. In particular, these highlight underrepresentation of the Indo-Pacific region in whole genome datasets relative to its dengue burden, and the overlooking of copy number variation in resistance studies owing to difficulties of sequence alignment in duplicated regions, particularly around the glutathione S-transferase epsilon class of genes.

### [2] [The nearest neighbor interchange proximity measure for the tree-basedness of rooted binary phylogenetic networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23426)
*Martin Frohn,Takatora Suzuki*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 论文针对根系统发育网络提出新的接近度指标 δ_rNNI，即把网络通过最少多少次根NNI操作转化为树型网络所需的最少次数，并研究其与已有指标的关系、界与精确算法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 树型网络假定进化由树状过程与杂交事件组成；对非树型网络，需要量化其违反树型模型假设的程度，而已有接近度指标不能刻画"距离成为树型网络"的差异，故需引入新度量 δ_rNNI。

Method: 对根网络定义 δ_rNNI 为将其转换为树型网络所需的最少根最近邻交换（rNNI）次数；对二叉网络证明 δ_rNNI 与已有各接近度指标两两不同，且与其它指标之间不存在近似保证；刻画一类网络使其线性时间下界可达（tight）；分析上界并给出计算 δ_rNNI 的精确求解算法。

Result: δ_rNNI 对二叉网络与所有已知接近度指标两两不同，且与它们之间无近似保证；刻画了一族网络，其 δ_rNNI 的线性时间下界是紧的；此外给出了上界分析与精确计算 δ_rNNI 的算法。

Conclusion: δ_rNNI 提供了刻画网络偏离树型模型程度的新视角，与既有度量不可互相替代，并具备可计算性（精确算法）与可界性（紧线性下界），为树型网络近似性研究提供新工具。

Abstract: Tree-based networks are a broad graph class in phylogenetics which posit a model of evolution composed of tree-like processes and hybridization events. Hence, for any non-tree-based phylogenetic network, it is meaningful to study the magnitude of violation of the tree-based model assumptions. For rooted networks, we introduce the proximity measure delta_rNNI, defined by the minimum number of rooted nearest neighbor interchanges (NNIs) required to transform a network into a tree-based network. For binary networks, we prove that delta_rNNI is pairwise distinct from all previously known proximity measures in the literature and that no approximation guarantees between delta_rNNI and other known proximity measures holds. In addition, we characterize a family of networks for which a linear time lower bound on delta_rNNI is tight. Moreover, we analyze upper bounds and give an exact solution algorithm to compute delta_rNNI.

### [3] [Multiplayer games on regular graphs under aspiration-driven imitation: Fixation probabilities and the dilution principle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23468)
*Dhaker Kroumi*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 论文研究了度数为k的正则图上多人博弈中合作的演化，个体决策同时依赖自身抱负水平与邻居的成功。作者用配对近似与扩散方法推导了弱选择下一阶固定概率的显式公式，将抱负效应与经图过滤的收益效应分离。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有合作演化理论多关注收益驱动的选择，而对个体抱负水平（aspiration）与网络结构（图度数）如何共同、交互地影响合作演化缺乏解析刻画，尤其缺少把抱负效应与收益效应分离、并显式给出二者对度数依赖性的弱选择一阶理论。

Method: 在度数为k的正则图上构建多人博弈模型，个体基于自身抱负水平和邻居成功更新策略；采用配对近似（pair approximation）结合扩散方法，在弱选择下计算固定概率的显式一阶公式；并将框架应用于公共品博弈、鹰鸽（stag-hunt）与雪堆（snowdrift）博弈。

Result: 一阶公式可将抱负效应与经图过滤的收益效应分离：增大图度数会削弱收益驱动的选择效应，而抱负差Δα=α_D−α_C的贡献在一阶上与图度数无关。公共品与stag-hunt博弈在大度数下条件仍保留成本项，需足够正的抱负差补偿贡献成本；snowdrift博弈在局部树状大度数极限下收益与成本贡献均消失，抱负差成为主导的一阶项。度数k的连通随机正则图上的Monte Carlo模拟在形式有效范围内与一阶预测一致，范围外的模拟仅作定性趋势检验。

Conclusion: 抱负与图结构对合作演化的作用在一阶上可加分离：度数只调节收益通道，抱负通道对度数不敏感。这为理解不同博弈类别下合作成功条件提供了统一解析框架，并指明大度数、局部树状区域中抱负差的关键作用。

Abstract: We investigate how cooperation evolves in multiplayer games on regular graphs of degree $k$ when individuals base their decisions on both their own aspiration levels and the success of their neighbors. We calculate explicit first-order formulas for fixation probabilities using pair approximation and diffusion methods under weak selection. These formulas separate aspiration effects from payoff effects filtered by the graph. We show that increasing the graph degree attenuates the selection effects driven by payoffs. In contrast, the contribution of the aspiration gap $Δα=α_D-α_C$ is independent of graph degree at first order. We apply the framework to public goods, stag-hunt, and snowdrift games and obtain corresponding conditions for the evolutionary success of cooperation. In the public goods and stag-hunt games, the conditions at large degree retain cost terms, so cooperation requires a sufficiently positive aspiration gap to compensate for the cost of contributing. In the snowdrift game, both benefit and cost contributions vanish in the locally tree-like regime at large degree, leaving the aspiration gap as the leading first-order contribution. Monte Carlo simulations on connected random regular graphs of degree $k$ are consistent with the first-order predictions within their formal regime of validity, while simulations outside this regime are interpreted only as qualitative checks of the predicted trends.

Method: 将显式的人口学阶段结构嵌入广义Lotka-Volterra模型，刻画物种生活史中个体发育变化对种间相互作用的影响，并据此分析群落局部渐近稳定性的参数空间。

### [5] [MultiPASE: Shape-Aware Comparative 3D Genome Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22786)
*N. Nipu,G. Johnson,C. Roth,D. Rogers,S. R. Starkenburg,C. R. Steadman,G. E. Marai*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文提出MultiPASE，一个以基因层级空间点云表示三维基因组结构并进行比较分析的计算流程。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 由Hi-C重建的三维基因组结构存在噪声、变异性和任意朝向，跨条件与时间点的比较困难；现有基于接触矩阵摘要的方法不保留重建结构的空间几何，也难以与基因表达和染色质可及性数据整合，因此需要稳健的结构对齐与空间抽象方法。

Method: MultiPASE将重建结构表示为基因级点云，先用基于PCA的轴向消歧与稳健ICP精化的由粗到细刚体配准将点云对齐到共享坐标系，再用两种互补的形状抽象方法生成稳定、嵌套、多尺度的表示，从致密核心区到更广的结构范围。

Result: 在病毒感染实验的Hi-C重建数据上，方法揭示了难以从原始三维结构直接解读的条件依赖结构变化；结合基因表达与染色质可及性数据支持空间组织与基因重定位的交互分析。补充材料的定量验证表明形状抽象保留非凸特征、跨样本稳定，且对齐流程是有效比较所必需的；使用案例与八名参与者的可用性评估证明了流程的有效性。

Conclusion: MultiPASE为三维基因组结构的比较分析提供了稳健的对齐与多尺度空间抽象框架，并可与功能基因组数据整合，支持跨条件、跨时间点的交互式结构分析。

Abstract: 3D genome structures reconstructed from Hi-C data provide valuable insights into chromatin organization, but comparing them across conditions and time points remains challenging because of noise, variability, and arbitrary orientation. State-of-the-art comparison approaches often rely on contact-matrix-based summaries, which do not preserve the spatial geometry of the reconstructed structures and make integration with gene expression and chromatin accessibility difficult. There is therefore a need for robust structural alignment and spatial abstraction methods that support integrated analysis across conditions and time points. We present MultiPASE, a computational pipeline for comparative analysis of reconstructed 3D genome structures represented as gene-level spatial point clouds. MultiPASE aligns point clouds into a shared coordinate frame using coarse-to-fine rigid registration with PCA-based axis disambiguation and robust ICP refinement, then summarizes spatial organization using two complementary shape abstraction methods that produce stable, nested, multi-scale representations from dense core regions to broader structural extent. Using Hi-C-derived reconstructions from viral infection experiments, we show how the approach reveals condition-dependent structural changes that are difficult to interpret from raw 3D structures alone. By integrating spatial structure with gene expression and chromatin accessibility, MultiPASE supports interactive analysis of spatial organization and gene relocation patterns. A quantitative validation, reported in the Supplementary Materials, shows that the proposed shape abstractions preserve non-convex features, remain stable across samples, and that the alignment pipeline is necessary for meaningful comparison. A usage case and a usability evaluation with eight participants demonstrate the pipeline's effectiveness for comparative structural analysis.

### [6] [Harness-Driven Agentic Operation of Optical Pooled Screen Analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22617)
*Matteo Di Bernardo,Claire Peterson,Ege Topkoc,Matt Might,Iain M. Cheeseman*

Main category: q-bio.GN

TL;DR: Brieflow-auto 是一个 Claude Code 插件形式的流水线专用执行框架，让编码大模型自动操作开源光学混合筛选流水线 Brieflow，完成参数调优、配置校验、执行监控、失败恢复与质量检查，并在五个已有筛选数据集上获得比原始人工分析更多的单基因可信分配细胞。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 光学混合筛选能把基因扰动与细胞形态和行为变化关联起来，但把显微图像数据转化为可解释结果需要复杂、耗时数天的分析。已有流水线虽能处理数据，但每个实验仍需研究者手工选择参数、检查输出、解决故障，人工负担重且结果依赖经验。

Method: 提出 Brieflow-auto：针对 Brieflow（开源端到端光学混合筛选流水线）构建的专用 harness，以 Claude Code 插件实现，由编码 LLM 驱动。研究者提供实验事实并批准启动；智能体在交互式 notebook 中调参；harness 负责配置校验、执行监控、常见故障恢复与输出质量检查；遇到流水线缺陷时，智能体向上游提交向后兼容的修复建议。

More broadly, we propose that wrapping an established pipeline in a harness can substantially accelerate its use and the long-term reliability of its codebase.
