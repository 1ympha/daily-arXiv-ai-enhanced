### [2] [EMBER: Predicting TCR--pMHC Binding Without a Docked Complex](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27334)
*Lin Ha,Chen Zhou,Haozheng Luo,Han Liu,Xiaoyu Zhang,Yan Chen*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: EMBER is a structure-based TCR–pMHC binding prediction framework that folds the two partners independently, encodes them as SE(3)-invariant residue graphs, and compares learned representations without forming a shared coordinate frame, thereby eliminating docked-complex placement error and cutting preprocessing cost from O(NM) to O(N+M).


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Traditional structure-based TCR–pMHC predictors rely on co-folded complex structures and read binding off the contact surface, so all downstream features inherit any deviation introduced by the structure-prediction tool; this placement error dominates prediction error. A method that avoids the docked complex altogether would remove this error source and reduce computational cost.

Method: EMBER folds TCR and pMHC independently, encodes each as an SE(3)-invariant residue graph, and compares their learned representations without constructing a shared coordinate frame. Relative pose is not an input, so placement error cannot propagate. Preprocessing requires O(N+M) monomer folds instead of O(NM) operations.

Result: Under epitope-disjoint evaluation against six retrained baselines, EMBER ranks first on every metric under all four folding tools, improving micro-AUROC by 0.9–14.4%, micro-AUPRC by 18.5–25.3%, and MCC by 10.6–24.1%; its ranking is up to 85% more stable across folding tools.

Conclusion: Decoupling structure prediction from complex assembly is an effective and efficient route for structure-based TCR–pMHC binding prediction, yielding consistent gains and robustness across folding tools.

Abstract: We propose EMBER, a structure-based framework for TCR--pMHC binding prediction that eliminates the need for a predicted docked complex, and with it the placement error that dominates such predictions. Traditional structure-based predictors utilize the co-folded complex structure of the receptor and the antigen and interpret binding from its contact surface. Consequently, every downstream feature inherits any deviation the structure-prediction tool introduced. Methodologically, EMBER folds the TCR and pMHC independently, encodes them as SE(3)-invariant residue graphs, and compares their learned representations without constructing a shared coordinate frame. A relative pose is therefore not an input of the model, so placement error cannot propagate into the prediction. Computationally, EMBER preprocesses $O(N{+}M)$ monomer folds where a complex-based predictor needs $O(NM)$ operations. Experimentally, under epitope-disjoint evaluation with six retrained baselines, EMBER is first on every metric under all four folding tools, by 0.9 to 14.4% in micro-AUROC, 18.5 to 25.3% in micro-AUPRC and 10.6 to 24.1% in MCC. Its ranking is also up to 85% more stable across folding tools.

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文首次对开源成人生理模拟器Pulse的七个操作输入开展Morris基本效应法的敏感性分析，构建覆盖四种病情严重度、男女两类体型共1,280次仿真的可靠性图谱，识别出三类失效模式并给出对应修正措施。

### [4] [Spatially Informed and Direction Sensitive Representation Learning for Spatial Transcriptomics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28579)

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: Tessera 是一个空间转录组表征学习框架，结合空间信息指导的基因选择与保留方向信息的几何归纳偏置，在表征质量上超越现有空间基础模型，并具备训练分布外的大尺度零样本泛化能力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Conclusion: 空间表征学习需兼顾分子信号的空间结构与具方向性、尺度无关的邻域几何建模；Tessera 的平移不变加方向保留设计在此上有效，并展现出跨组织、跨技术的可迁移性与大尺度零样本泛化潜力。

Consequently, cells with similar neighbor distances but distinct spatial organization may receive overly similar representations, despite experiencing different biological contexts that shape their cellular states. We introduce Tessera, a spatial representation learning framework that addresses both molecular selection and cellular context. Tessera combines spatially informed gene selection with a geometric inductive bias that contextualizes each cell within its local tissue environment. By modeling relative cell-cell relationships, Tessera is translation-invariant while preserving direction, allowing neighboring cells to contribute differently according to their spatial context and cellular state. Across held-out tissues and technologies, Tessera outperforms existing spatial foundation models in representation quality and generalizes zero-shot to contexts up to $16\times$ larger than those seen during training.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 实验元数据是解读归档测序数据的前提。自2015年Alnasir与Shanahan考察SRA中片段化、接头连接与富集注释以来，数据规模与建库方法均已大幅变化，但缺乏对其测量结果的可比性复核，因此有必要在相同关键词定义下重访该问题，评估注释质量的演变。

Method: 采用原始SQL关键词定义，在2013与2026两个时点的NCBI元数据快照上进行同口径比较；将2026年数据重建为历史SRAdb模式以保证可复现性。分别在library_construction_protocol字段及联合design_description字段检索三步关键词，按测序文库策略分层统计覆盖率，并抽取150条实验记录与源XML进行独立核验。

Coverage varied across sequencing library strategies, ranging from 0.26% for AMPLICON to 7.69% for RNA-Seq.   Conclusions: The original criteria identify a smaller proportion of experiments in the designated protocol field than a decade earlier. The findings support more consistent protocol annotation and the need to distinguish keyword occurrence from the adequacy of experimental documentation.

### [6] [Caterpillar trees are asymmetric, balanced trees are symmetric](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27151)
*Kalle Leppälä*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 证明了Allman、Long与Rhodes提出的两个猜想：在超度量可交换模型中，模型维数随物种树叶数的变化以毛毛虫树（caterpillar tree）最大、以平衡树（balanced tree）最小。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 超度量可交换模型刻画了数理系统发育中，超度量机制性合并与替换模型里等位基因分布所具备的对称性。作者希望弄清该模型的维数如何依赖于物种树的形状与叶数，从而验证此前文献中关于维数极值出现的两个猜想。

Method: 以超度量可交换模型为对象，把模型的参数维数表达为物种树叶数的函数，并按树的拓扑形状（毛毛虫树与平衡树两端）进行解析比较与证明，从而给出维数的上界与下界结论。

Result: 在给定叶数条件下，超度量可交换模型的维数在毛毛虫树上取得最大值，在平衡树上取得最小值，从而证实了两个相关猜想。

Conclusion: 树形结构本身显著影响超度量可交换模型的维数：越"细长"的树维数越高，越"对称"的树维数越低。这为系统发育模型的参数可辨识性与复杂度分析提供了明确依据。

Abstract: Two conjectures of Allman, Long and Rhodes are proven. The ultrametric exchangeable model captures the symmetries in allele distributions of ultrametric mechanistic models of coalescence and substitution in mathematical phylogeny. The dimension of this model with respect to the species tree leaf count is shown to be greatest for caterpillar trees and least for balanced trees.
