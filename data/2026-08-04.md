### [1] [An Information Theory Treatment of Animal Movement Tracks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.16290)
*Wayne M Getz*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种基于香农信息论的精细尺度（秒级）动物运动轨迹分割新方法，通过定义最小统计运动元素（StaMEs）和"词"层级，利用聚类分析识别典型活动模式（CAMs），并引入熵度量、编码效率和Jensen-Shannon散度来优化参数选择和评估分割质量。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 动物运动追踪技术已从小时级进步到秒级定位，但轨迹分割分析方法的尺度仍停留在亚小时到分钟级别，两者之间存在明显滞后。需要一种新方法将分割分析推进到秒级水平，以充分利用高分辨率定位数据中的行为信息。

Method: 本文首先回顾和更新了运动路径分割的相关概念，然后提出利用聚类分析组织最小时级统计运动元素（StaMEs，长度为μ步），并将其编码为更高级的"词"（长度为mμ步）。词聚类的质心被定义为典型活动模式（CAMs）。方法还引入熵度量、编码效率计算和Jensen-Shannon散度来评估参数选择和聚类方法的优劣。

Result: 与传统的行为变化点分析和隐马尔可夫模型分割方案不同，该方法能够提供运动路径的熵度量、计算StaMEs和CAMs的编码效率、评估将m个StaMEs字符串分配到CAM类型的错误率，并通过Jensen-Shannon散度比较不同参数组合的最佳选择。

Conclusion: 该理论为应对全球变化对动物运动和跨景观重新分布的影响提供了又一有力分析工具，有望将轨迹分割分析推进到秒级精细尺度。

Abstract: Position recordings of the two-dimensional tracks of animals moving over landscapes has progressed over the past three decades from hourly to second-by-second locations. Track segmentation methods for analyzing the behavioral information in such relocation data has lagged somewhat behind, with scales of analysis currently at the sub-hourly to minute level. A new approach is needed to bring segmentation analysis down to a second-by-second level. Here, a fine-scale approach is presented that rests heavily on concepts from Shannon's Information Theory. In this paper, we first briefly review and update concepts relating to movement path segmentation. We then discuss how cluster analysis can be used to organize the smallest viable statistical movement elements (StaMEs), which are $μ$ steps long, and to code the next level of movement elements called ``words'' that are $m μ$ steps long. Centroids of these word clusters are identified as canonical activity modes (CAMs). Unlike current behavioral change point analysis and hidden Markov model segmentation schemes, the approach presented here allows us to provide entropy measures for movement paths, compute the coding efficiencies of derived StaMEs and CAMs, and to assess error rates in the allocation of strings of $m$ StaMEs to CAM types. In addition our approach allows us to employ the Jensen-Shannon divergence measure to assess and compare the best choices for the various parameters (number of steps in a StaME, number of StaME types, number of StaMEs in a word, number of CAM types), as well as the best clustering methods for generating segments that can then be used to interpret and predict sequences of higher order segments. The theory presented here provides another tool in our toolbox for dealing with the effects of global change on the movement and redistribution of animals across altered landscapes.

### [2] [GRAIN: Molecules Are Not the Right Granularity -- Active-Ingredient Modeling for Safe Medication Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00098)
*Juao Fan,Jinhan Li,Shengxin Zhu*

Main category: q-bio.QM

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有安全感知的推荐系统在两种粒度上操作：药物代码（将每种药物视为不可分割的token）或分子子结构（比药理学相互作用知识实际组织的粒度更细）。作者认为活性成分是被忽略的关键粒度，这一粒度既能反映药理学交互知识，又能避免代码级聚合丢失预测信号的问题。

Method: GRAIN框架包含三个核心组件：(1) 选择性状态空间骨干网络，线性时间处理长而稀疏的访问序列；(2) 联合目标函数统一三种知识源——药物级DDI图、通过RxNorm标准化得到的成分级DDI图、以及EHR衍生的共处方图；(3) 比例控制器，根据验证集DDI率自适应调整准确率-安全性权衡而非预先固定。

Result: 在严格匹配的设置下（相同预处理、队列、词汇表、数据划分和评估代码），GRAIN在MIMIC-IV上全面超越MambaHealth基线：Jaccard从0.4488提升至0.4983，PRAUC从0.6911提升至0.7485，F1从0.5989提升至0.6453，同时将药物级DDI率从0.1875降至0.0948。此外定义了成分级DDI率，这是药物代码级评估不可见的安全度量。

Conclusion: 成分级归一化能恢复代码级聚合丢失的预测信号，且与精确的序列建模互补而非竞争。活性成分是药物推荐中连接药理学知识组织与预测性能的关键桥梁粒度。

Abstract: Medication recommendation from electronic health records must balance predictive accuracy against the risk of adverse drug-drug interactions (DDIs) under polypharmacy. Existing safety-aware recommenders operate at one of two granularities: the drug code, which treats each medication as an indivisible token, or the molecular substructure, which is finer than pharmacological interaction knowledge is actually organized. We argue that the active ingredient is the missing granularity, and introduce GRAIN, a medication recommendation framework built around it.
  GRAIN encodes longitudinal patient trajectories (diagnoses, procedures, past medications) with a selective state space backbone that handles long, irregular visit sequences in linear time. On top of it we introduce a joint objective unifying three knowledge sources aligned to a common medication vocabulary: a drug-level DDI graph, an ingredient-level DDI graph obtained by normalizing medication codes to active ingredients via RxNorm, and an EHR-derived co-prescription graph. A proportional controller adapts the accuracy-safety trade-off to the observed validation DDI rate rather than fixing it a priori.
  Under strictly matched settings -- identical preprocessing, cohort, vocabulary, split, and evaluation code -- GRAIN improves over a re-implemented MambaHealth baseline on MIMIC-IV across all standard multi-label metrics (Jaccard 0.4488 to 0.4983, PRAUC 0.6911 to 0.7485, F1 0.5989 to 0.6453) while reducing the drug-level DDI rate from 0.1875 to 0.0948. We further define an ingredient-level DDI rate, a safety measure invisible to drug-code-level evaluation. The results indicate that ingredient-level normalization recovers predictive signal erased by code-level aggregation, and that it is complementary to, rather than in competition with, accurate sequence modeling.

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种基于波形形状（是否存在拐点）来判断生物振荡是否为谐波振荡的新方法，并应用于昼夜节律研究。通过对蓝细菌和哺乳动物SCN的生物发光记录以及Goodwin模型的数值与半解析分析，发现昼夜节律核心振荡器的波形在该定义下是谐波的。

  <summary>Details</summary>
