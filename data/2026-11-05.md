### [1] [AcupunctureBench: A Multimodal Benchmark for Evaluating Generative Models in the Acupuncture Domain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05399)
*Shusheng Li,Yifei Qu,Zichen Han,Xingye Zhang,Zhongxin Wei,Yicheng Qiao,Jiaxin Shen,Changshuai Zhang,Siyuan Guo,Haiyan Ren,Xingfang Pan,Yangyang Liu,Zhaoshui He,Shuguo Hu,Wenjun Tan*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文提出 AcupunctureBench，首个独立构建的针灸领域大规模多模态基准，涵盖文本、图像、视频三种模态，包含58,526条问题样本和1,216条视频样本，并据此评测了14个语言模型、14个多模态模型和4个视频基础模型。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 大语言模型在中医领域应用日益广泛，标准化基准评测逐渐出现，但针灸作为中医重要组成部分，现有评测基准在该领域存在空白。

Method: 构建覆盖针灸理论、经络腧穴、临床推理、中医证候、腧穴实操等多维知识的多模态基准；设计人机协同的数据构建流程，提出高效去重策略以减少冗余样本，并广泛采用数据合成以降低数据泄漏风险；任务类型包括客观题、主观问答、病例分析、图像理解、图像推理和视频理解。

Result: AcupunctureBench 共含58,526条问题样本与1,216条视频样本；基于该基准评测了14个大语言模型、14个大型多模态模型和4个视频基础模型，并发布了首个针灸领域专用评测网站。

Conclusion: 该工作填补了针灸领域标准化评测基准的空白，为针灸多模态模型能力评估提供了规模化、专业化平台。

Abstract: As large language models (LLMs) are increasingly applied in the field of traditional Chinese medicine (TCM), standardized benchmark evaluations for TCM have gradually emerged. Acupuncture is a crucial component of TCM. However, existing evaluation benchmarks are lacking in this domain. To address this gap, this paper proposes AcupunctureBench, the first independently constructed large-scale multimodal benchmark for the acupuncture domain, covering text, image, and video modalities. The AcupunctureBench spans multidimen sional knowledge in acupuncture, including acupuncture theory, meridians and acupoints, clinical reasoning, TCM syndromes, and practical acupoint operation. To achieve large-scale construction while ensuring medical professionalism, this work designs a human-machine collaborative data construction pipeline and proposes efficient deduplication strategies to reduce redundant samples, while extensively adopting data synthesis to mitigate data leak age risks. AcupunctureBench contains a total of 58,526 question samples and 1,216 video samples, covering multiple task types including objective questions, subjective question answering, case analysis, image understanding, image reasoning, and video understanding. Based on this benchmark, this paper evaluates 14 large language models, 14 large multimodal models, and 4 video foundation models, and further releases the first dedicated evaluation website for the acupuncture domain.

### [2] [Deep-Learning Algorithm for Indeterminate Thyroid Nodules: Comparison and Combination with Molecular Testing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05400)
*Jichen Yang,Jeffrey Ashton,Jikai Zhang,Maciej A. Mazurowski,Benjamin Wildman-Tobriner*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 在466例Bethesda III甲状腺结节中，基于常规超声的CNN（AUC 0.73）与分子检测（AUC 0.55）判别效能无显著差异，但特异性更高（0.95 vs 0.32），且能进一步将分子阳性结节分成高风险与低风险亚组，可互补于分子检测。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Bethesda III（细胞学不确定）结节恶性风险居中，临床上依赖分子检测来决定手术还是随访，但分子检测费用高、可及性有限。作者希望探索用常规超声图像训练的CNN能否对这类结节进行危险分层，并与分子检测进行比较与组合。

Method: 回顾性纳入418例患者、466条结节记录（均接受Afirma或ThyroSeq分子检测）。以确定性质结节上预训练的CNN为基础，通过软标签和迁移学习适配到不确定结节。去除重复记录后，119个有手术病理的结节用于开发，47个作为独立测试集。评估AUC、敏感度、特异度、PPV/NPV，并分析DL输出与分子结果的联合分层及logistic回归模型（DL输出、结节大小、分子结果）。

Result: 测试集上DL模型AUC 0.73（95% CI 0.51–0.94），分子检测AUC 0.55（0.38–0.71），差异无统计学意义（P=0.15）。在沿用原算法阈值时，DL特异度显著更高（0.95 vs 0.32，P<0.001），PPV更高（0.67 vs 0.21），敏感度较低（0.44 vs 0.78，P=0.25），NPV相近（0.88 vs 0.86）。6例DL阳性结节均为分子阳性；DL输出将分子阳性结节划分为高风险组（4/6恶性）与较低风险组（3/27恶性，P=0.01）。手术实施强烈受分子结果驱动（阳性者99%手术、阴性者19%），分子检测准确性依平台而异。包含DL输出、结节大小和分子结果的logistic回归剔除了DL项，且未优于单用DL输出。

Conclusion: 超声深度学习可与分子检测互补用于Bethesda III结节的风险分层，尤其能识别分子阳性结节中恶性风险较低者，有望减少不必要的手术；但研究样本量小、单中心回顾性、存在手术选择偏倚，需外部验证。

Abstract: Thyroid nodules with indeterminate cytology (Bethesda III) carry an intermediate risk of malignancy, and molecular testing is widely used to decide between surgery and surveillance, but it is costly and not universally available. We investigated whether a convolutional neural network (CNN) applied to routine ultrasound images can stratify Bethesda III nodules, and how it compares and combines with molecular testing. We retrospectively identified 466 nodule records from 418 patients who underwent molecular testing (Afirma or ThyroSeq). A CNN pre-trained on determinate nodules was adapted to indeterminate nodules with soft labels and transfer learning. After removal of duplicate records, 119 nodules with surgical pathology were used for development and 47 were held out for testing. On the test set, the deep learning (DL) model achieved an AUC of 0.73 (95% CI 0.51-0.94), compared with 0.55 (0.38-0.71) for molecular testing (P = 0.15). At a cut-off inherited from the original algorithm, the DL model was more specific than molecular testing (0.95 vs 0.32; P < 0.001), with a higher positive predictive value (0.67 vs 0.21), a lower sensitivity (0.44 vs 0.78; P = 0.25) and a similar negative predictive value (0.88 vs 0.86). All six DL-positive test nodules were molecular-positive, and the DL output split the molecular-positive nodules into a high-risk (4/6 malignant) and a lower-risk group (3/27 malignant; P = 0.01). Surgical verification was strongly driven by the molecular result (99% of positive vs 19% of negative results), and molecular-test accuracy depended on the platform. A logistic regression of the DL output, nodule size and the molecular result dropped the DL term and did not improve on the DL output alone. Ultrasound DL complemented molecular testing for Bethesda III nodules, in particular by identifying molecular-positive nodules at lower risk of malignancy.

### [3] [Morpheus.AI: agents autonomously generate, execute and refine virtual tissue models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05413)
*Prerana Chandratre,Anjali Sharma,Justin Bürger Michaelis,Diego Jahn,Jörn Starruß,Lutz Brusch,Simon Syga*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 将大语言模型（LLM）智能体与模拟器 Morpheus（Morpheus.AI）配对，可从十五项基准研究中自主构建虚拟组织模型，其中五项此前没有已发表的模型。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 虚拟组织模型能把观测数据与机制性假设联系起来，但构建这类模型需要同时具备生物学、数学与编程三方面的专业知识，门槛较高。作者希望降低建模门槛，使"计算建模"（in silico modeling）成为常规流程。

Method: 采用 LLM 智能体与 Morpheus 模拟器（Morpheus.AI）相结合的方式，让智能体自主完成模型构建；在十五项基准研究上进行评测，其中五项缺乏已发表模型作为参照，并让智能体实际运行所生成的仿真。

Result: 在使用前沿 LLM 的情况下，实际执行仿真的智能体在两小时内满足了 83–86% 的研究特定评判标准，且每个模型的成本低于 4 美元。

Conclusion: 此类智能体有望让虚拟组织模型的构建变得常规化、低成本化，从而让 in silico 建模进入日常科研实践。

Abstract: Virtual tissue models link observations to mechanistic hypotheses, but building them demands expertise in biology, mathematics and programming. Here, large language model (LLM) agents paired with the simulator Morpheus (Morpheus.AI) autonomously built such models from fifteen benchmark studies, five without a published model. With a frontier LLM, agents that ran their simulations met 83-86% of study-specific criteria in under two hours and for under US\$4 per model. Such agents could make in silico modeling routine.

### [4] [CellCanon: An Agentic Cellular World Model for Prediction, Explanation, and Observation-Driven Updating](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05890)
*Zhaokang Liang,Shuyang Zhuang,Xiaoran Jiao,Hao Chen*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 提出 CellCanon——首个"智能体式细胞世界模型"，统一单细胞表达预测、有证据支撑的解释与基于观测的更新，在 SciPlex3 留出条件上显著提升差异表达预测指标，并大幅降低 token 消耗。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 生物发现需要在未观测条件下推断细胞响应，需结合分子知识与新测量；现有方法难以将预测、可解释证据与持续更新统一起来。

Method: 构建条件级生物学证据复用机制指导基因级数值读取与单细胞表达生成；用共享世界状态连接预测、实测数据与来源可追溯的断言，支撑解释与修订；含 Predict、Explain、Update 三个能力。

Result: 在 339 个留出 SciPlex3 条件、3 个细胞系上，差异表达 AUPRC 与 Macro-F1 较最佳基线分别提升 63.3% 与 17.3%，方向 Macro-F1 提升 7.2%；相比逐基因推理，Predict 与 Explain 的 token 用量分别下降 99.77% 与 98.41%；来源基准上 Explain 全部答对可答问题并识别全部证据不足案例；模拟反馈中 Update 仅用每种药物一个实测来源上下文即超越最佳微调基线，且不改变模型权重。

Conclusion: CellCanon 支持对细胞响应的累积性刻画，其预测与生物学判断可随证据积累而修订。

Abstract: Biological discovery requires using molecular knowledge and new measurements to infer responses beyond the conditions already observed. We introduce CellCanon, the first agentic cellular world model to unify single-cell expression prediction, evidence-grounded explanation, and observation-driven updating. Its predictive core reuses condition-level biological evidence across gene-specific numerical readouts to guide single-cell expression generation. A shared world state links predictions, acquired measurements, and source-linked claims, supporting explanation and revision. On 339 held-out SciPlex3 conditions across three cell lines, CellCanon improves differential-expression AUPRC and Macro-F1 over the best baseline for each metric by 63.3% and 17.3%, respectively, and direction Macro-F1 by 7.2%. Relative to gene-wise reasoning on matched drug-cell cohorts, measured language-model token use falls by 99.77% for Predict and 98.41% for Explain. On the source-grounded benchmark, Explain correctly answers all answerable questions with supporting proofs and identifies all insufficient-evidence cases. In simulated experimental feedback, Update uses one measured source context per drug to exceed the best fine-tuned baselines in differential-expression AUPRC and Macro-F1 without changing model weights. Together, these capabilities support a cumulative account of cellular responses, with predictions and biological judgments that can be revised as evidence accumulates.
