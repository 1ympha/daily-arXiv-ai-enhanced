### [1] [Evolving view on phylogenetic networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04149)
*Claudia Solis-Lemus*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文综述系统梳理系统发育网络（phylogenetic networks）的表示类型分类，回顾从杂交检测到多物种网络溯祖等推断方法的演进，并指出统计可识别性与计算可扩展性两大选择压力推动了各方法学谱系的融合。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 杂交、渐渗和水平基因转移等网状过程无法被二叉分支树完整表示，需要系统发育网络来刻画；同时网络推断领域方法众多、术语与模型类别混杂，缺乏统一视角，作者希望厘清各类网络表示及其可解释性与可估计性。

Method: 综述性分析：对显式网络的若干主要类别进行类型学划分，区分其生物学可解释性与从经验数据中准确估计的能力；并按时间线梳理主要推断方法，包括杂交检测检验、基于距离与子图的合并方法、多物种网络溯祖下的概率方法、复合似然及分治框架。

Result: 作者归纳出方法演进受统计可识别性与计算可扩展性两项选择性压力驱动；并观察到原本分属群体遗传学、系统发育基因组学与数学网络理论的方法学谱系正在相互“渐渗”，逐步统一到共享框架中。

Conclusion: 研究正迈向“网络思维”范式：统一框架可整合序列层面与物种层面的网状过程、提升对系统性误差的稳健性、改进面向基因组规模数据的算法，从而把生命之树扩展为更丰富、更交织却更清晰的演化图景。

Abstract: Reticulate processes such as hybridization, introgression, and horizontal gene transfer cannot be fully represented by a bifurcating tree. Enter phylogenetic networks: first as split graphs to visualize tree discordance, then as explicit probabilistic models that capture biological phenomena. Here, we describe the broad taxonomy of network representations, distinguishing the principal classes of explicit networks, their biological interpretability and our ability to accurately estimate them from empirical data. We also trace the evolution of the main network inferential methods from hybrid detection tests, distance- and subgraph-based amalgamation methods, probabilistic approaches under the multispecies network coalescent, composite-likelihood and divide-and-conquer frameworks, while highlighting the selective pressures of statistical identifiability and computational scalability that have shaped this evolution. As we move towards a network thinking paradigm, previously isolated methodological lineages from population genetics, phylogenomics, and mathematical network theory are now introgressing, uniting diverse network models into a shared framework that can integrate sequence- and species-level reticulate processes, increase robustness to systematic errors, and refine algorithms for genome-scale data, expanding the tree of life into a richer, more entangled yet clearer picture of evolution.

### [2] [Basic Reproductive Numbers in Host-Parasite Systems with Demographic Bistability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04990)
*Carli Peterson,Erika Gallo,Christopher Kribs*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 研究人口统计双稳态（不动点与周期循环）如何影响基本再生数ℛ0的计算与解释，通过蜱-宿主模型与Nicholson-Bailey宿主-拟寄生物模型对比，发现入侵阈值是否依赖吸引子取决于病原传播结构。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: ℛ0广泛用于判断传染病能否在种群中持续存在，但当无病的人口统计动态存在多种稳定吸引子时，ℛ0的解释会变得不清晰，因此需要厘清人口统计双稳性与传播结构对入侵阈值的共同作用。

Method: 构建两个离散时间带感染的宿主-寄生物模型：一个由伯氏疏螺旋体传播启发的蜱-宿主模型，一个含种内竞争与感染的Nicholson-Bailey宿主-拟寄生物模型；对每个模型用下一代算子方法分别计算对应不同无病吸引子的再生数，并比较其入侵阈值。

Result: 蜱-宿主模型中，基于稳定人口周期计算的再生数与基于正共存不动点计算的结果不同，存在感染在某一人口统计吸引子下可入侵、而在另一吸引子下不可入侵的参数区域；相反，宿主-拟寄生物模型中，宿主驱动的入侵阈值在正共存不动点与仅宿主的周期循环上必然相同。

Conclusion: 人口统计双稳性本身并不必然产生依赖吸引子的入侵阈值，人口动态对入侵的影响取决于病原传播的结构；特别是完整的宿主-媒介-宿主传播路径可能使不同人口统计吸引子之间的差异改变入侵阈值。因此在存在多种稳定无病状态的种群中解释ℛ0时，必须同时考虑人口动态与传播结构。

Abstract: The basic reproductive number, $\mathcal{R}_0$, is widely used to determine whether an infectious disease can persist in a population. However, its interpretation may become unclear when the underlying disease-free demographic dynamics exhibit multiple types of stable attractors. In this study, we investigate how demographic bistability between fixed points and periodic cycles affects the calculation and interpretation of $\mathcal{R}_0$. We consider two discrete-time host-parasite models with infection: a tick-host model motivated by Borrelia burgdorferi transmission and a Nicholson-Bailey host-parasitoid model with intraspecific competition and infection. For each model, we calculate reproductive numbers associated with different disease-free attractors using the next-generation operator method and compare their invasion thresholds. In the tick-host model, the reproduction number calculated on the stable demographic cycle differs from that calculated at the positive coexistence fixed point, resulting in parameter regions where infection can invade at one demographic attractor but not the other. In contrast, in the host-parasitoid model, the host-driven invasion threshold is necessarily the same at the positive coexistence fixed point and on the host-only periodic cycles. These contrasting results demonstrate that demographic bistability alone does not necessarily produce attractor-dependent invasion thresholds. Rather, the effect of demographic dynamics on invasion depends on the structure of pathogen transmission. In particular, a complete host--vector--host transmission pathway can allow differences between demographic attractors to alter the invasion threshold. Our results emphasize the importance of considering both demographic dynamics and transmission structure when interpreting $\mathcal{R}_0$ in populations with multiple stable disease-free states.

TL;DR: 该研究提出贝叶斯优化的双二阶滤波器-Burg（BOB）模型，用于刺激锁时瞬态EEG的实时相位估计，并在theta节律与alpha节律上验证其以远低于深度模型的标定时间取得相当或更优的性能。

Under these testing conditions, BOB achieved matched or better performance than these models at a fraction of the calibration time.

### [4] [BEND 2.0: Benchmarking the Capabilities Needed to Design DNA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03806)
*Andreas Bjerregaard,Rachael DeVries,Dionysia Danai Brilli,Panagiotis Antoniadis,Yan Li,Anders Krogh,Wouter Boomsma,Ole Winther,Frederikke Isa Marin*

Main category: q-bio.GN

TL;DR: 本文提出 BEND 2.0 基准，用于评测基因组基础模型预测 DNA 变异效应并指导序列设计的能力，发现模型选择与表征访问方式均影响性能，基因组模型已显示通用潜力但任务远未饱和。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 构建 BEND 2.0（BENchmark for DNA language models），基于 5 个实验数据集设计 8 项任务，同时考察（1）模型预测 DNA 改变效应的能力与（2）预测能否指导序列设计；并对比不同模型、不同表征访问方式（如冻结表征上的线性读出 vs 模型原生输出）、以及功能性后训练的影响，同时检验线性读出在实验与基因间的迁移性。

Abstract: Genomic foundation models are increasingly used to propose DNA sequences and edits. But successful design requires capabilities that current benchmarks do not measure. Linear readouts fitted to frozen representations can match or exceed models' native outputs, and functional post-training substantially improves the models' capabilities. Linear readouts transfer well across experimental assays and genes, suggesting that models capture functional patterns beyond the data used to fit individual predictors. At the same time, strong prediction of average effects can conceal weaker prediction of context-specific effects, and accurate prediction of combined mutation effects does not imply accurate prediction of their interactions. In design tasks, model predictions can reduce the number of candidates needed to find a desired response, but still leave substantial room for better design choices, particularly for context-specific and multi-cell objectives. Our benchmark reveals that genomic models now display signs of general utility but have yet to saturate on performance in even simple tasks.

### [5] [Visualization and Characterization of Gene Group Distribution Patterns Across Genomes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04037)
*Yanying Wu*

Main category: q-bio.GN

TL;DR: 作者构建交互式网页工具把任何基因组成员标到果蝇染色体带型图上，发现基因家族成员间距分为短程（中位1.8 kb）与长程（中位2.4 Mb）两个群体，短程群体是真实的成簇信号；该双群体现象在人类及另外六个模式生物中同样存在。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 同一基因家族的成员常聚集在染色体上，但"成员之间究竟相距多远、间距是否存在规律"这一问题在不同基因组之间很少被一致地测量。作者希望提供一个可视化的探索工具，并系统量化基因家族的空间分布模式，同时检验看似有规律（如均匀间隔）的视觉模式是否只是偶然。

Method: 构建交互式网页应用，将FlyBase的1396个基因组的成员绘制在果蝇带型染色体图上并列出每个成员的邻居；统计相邻成员的间距分布，并用与染色体臂匹配的随机对照检验均匀间隔与长程群体是否源于偶然。随后把同一分析流程扩展到人类、小鼠、斑马鱼、线虫、酿酒酵母、拟南芥和玉米共八个真核生物，比较各基因组中两组间距的混合曲线在染色体间是否恒定。

Result: 在RAB GTPase中观察到两段5.08 Mb的间距仅相差634 bp的惊人对称基因集；相邻成员间距呈短程（中位1.8 kb）与长程（中位2.4 Mb）双峰分布。随机对照显示，无最小距离过滤时的均匀间隔以及长程群体在很大程度上可由偶然产生，而短程群体是真实的成簇信号。八个基因组均出现同样的两个群体；混合曲线可分为染色体间基本恒定（I型：果蝇、玉米、拟南芥、线虫）与随染色体变化（II型：人、小鼠、斑马鱼、酵母）两类。短程群体的尺度与各基因组中基因的间距相符，长程群体在果蝇中符合随机分布，在其他基因组中与随机分布一致。

Conclusion: 跨八个真核生物，基因家族成员普遍落在两个距离群体中；短程群体反映真实的成簇，长程群体基本符合随机放置。研究提示视觉上显著的规律（如均匀间隔）必须经过随机性检验才能解释；所开发的工具与分析流程为跨物种探索和比较基因成簇提供了简便途径。

Abstract: Genes of the same family often lie close together on a chromosome, yet how far apart family members sit, and whether their spacing follows any pattern, has rarely been measured consistently across genomes. We built an interactive web application that places the members of any of the 1,396 FlyBase gene groups on the banded ideogram of the fruit fly (Drosophila melanogaster) and lists each member's neighbours. While browsing it, we found strikingly symmetric gene sets in which consecutive members are almost evenly spaced. In the RAB GTPases, two 5.08 Mb spacings differ by only 634 bp. We also found that distances between neighbouring members fall into two populations: short-range (median 1.8 kb) and long-range (median 2.4 Mb). Although an arm-matched random control suggests that the even spacing (without a minimum-distance filter) as well as the long-range population arise largely by chance, the short-range population turns out to be a genuine clustering signal. We then extended the analysis to human and six other model organisms (mouse, zebrafish, Caenorhabditis elegans, Saccharomyces cerevisiae, Arabidopsis thaliana and maize), and every genome showed the same two populations. The mixture curve of the two is nearly constant across chromosomes in some genomes (Type I: fly, maize, Arabidopsis, C. elegans) but varies across chromosomes in others (Type II: human, mouse, zebrafish, yeast). Thus, across eight eukaryotes, family members lie in two distance populations. The scale of the short-range population follows the spacing of genes in each genome, and the long-range population matches random placement in fly and is consistent with it in the other genomes. Our results also show that visual patterns such as even spacing must be tested against chance before they are interpreted. Our tool and approach give a simple way to explore and compare gene clustering across species.
