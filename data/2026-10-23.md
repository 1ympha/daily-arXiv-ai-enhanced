### [1] [Modelling Toxin-Induced Intraspecific Personality Variation in Predator-Prey Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30431)
*Jingyi Jin,Tian Xu Wang,Hao Wang*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 在毒素依赖的捕食者-猎物模型中同时引入猎物与捕食者的"个性"（胆大/胆怯）差异，用彩虹鳟和水生昆虫受甲基汞暴露的数据参数化，并用准稳态约化分析平衡点结构与稳定性，发现两种个性对种群动态方向相反。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 个性特征（如胆大与胆怯）可通过个体行为差异影响种间相互作用，环境毒素也可能改变这些特征，但现有捕食者-猎物模型很少考虑动物个性。作者希望刻画毒素与个性变异共同作用下的种群动态。

Method: 在毒素依赖的捕食者-猎物模型基础上，同时加入猎物与捕食者的个性变异：猎物个性影响出生与生长，捕食者个性影响功能反应；以彩虹鳟与水生昆虫暴露于甲基汞的情形进行参数化。再用准稳态近似将模型约化为二维系统，分析其正性、有界性与平衡点存在性，并依据存在性分类数值研究所有可行平衡点的局部稳定性，划分灭绝、仅猎物存续、共存与双稳态的参数区域。

Result: 猎物与捕食者个性对种群动态影响方向不同：猎物个性倾向于减小生物量波动、促进稳定共存；捕食者个性则可能增大波动并导致持续振荡。这些效应主要出现在对淡水生态系统具有环境相关性的低到中等毒素浓度下。平衡点结构由毒素水平与两类个性对比共同决定，并识别出局部稳定灭绝、仅猎物存续、共存与双稳态的参数区域。

Conclusion: 毒素诱导的个性变异能够定性改变捕食者-猎物动态，因此个性结构应纳入毒素影响的生态建模。

Abstract: Personality traits, such as boldness and shyness, can affect interspecific interactions through differences in individual behaviour. Environmental toxins may also alter these traits. However, few predator-prey models have considered animal personality. In this study, we extend a toxin-dependent predator-prey model by incorporating personality variation in both prey and predators. Prey personality affects birth and growth, whereas predator personality affects the predator functional response. The model is parameterized for rainbow trout and aquatic insect prey exposed to methylmercury. Numerical results show that prey and predator personality have different effects on population dynamics. Prey personality tends to reduce biomass fluctuations and promote stable coexistence. In contrast, predator personality can increase biomass fluctuations and lead to persistent population oscillations. These effects are mainly observed at low and intermediate toxin concentrations that are environmentally relevant to freshwater ecosystems. Using a quasi-steady-state approximation, we reduce the model to a two-dimensional system and study its positivity, boundedness, and the existence of equilibria. The equilibrium structure depends jointly on the toxin level and the prey and predator personality contrasts. Based on this existence classification, we numerically investigate the local stability of all feasible equilibria and identify parameter regions corresponding to locally stable extinction, prey-only persistence, coexistence, and bistability. Overall, toxin-induced personality variation can qualitatively change predator-prey dynamics.

### [2] [Report the tolerance, not the count: exact phylogenetic distance deconvolution with incomplete references](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30776)
*Haotian Tang*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 该论文研究系统发育距离反卷积中解释一个距离谱所需的最少严格内部源点数量，并量化参考集不完整带来的代价；给出正有理约化树上的精确算法、缺失参考下的W[1]难性，并将算法用作诊断工具，发现"缺失哪些参考"比"缺失多少"更重要。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 作者希望厘清距离反卷积中最小内部源点数的可计算性与参考集缺失的影响，并检验这一量在真实数据上是否可观测、点估计式的源点数报告是否有意义。

Method: 在正有理约化树上设计2^O(K)·poly(B)位复杂度的精确算法，允许自由正均值的悬挂枝与任意非空观测叶集；用参数化复杂度证明缺失参考且悬挂枝固定时的W[1]难性；随后把该算法作为仪器，定理化"移除参考只会降低最小源点数"，并用23,545次抽样、20–353个参考的对抗性实验和真实16S面板（10,575个参考，随机丢弃7,931个或按整支系丢弃）做实证。

Result: 缺失参考方向为定理且在全部23,545次抽样中成立；两个对抗性选取的参考在中位数即可破坏最小解释的唯一性，且该数目不随面板规模增长（Spearman 0.203, p=0.15），而随机缺失成本随规模线性增长；随机丢弃真实面板中的7,931个参考仅损失1.8%的容差，按整支系丢弃则损失90%；已发表的296例距离估计全部超出该容差，已发表启发式推断的混合在282/296例中比生成数据的配置更贴合观测。

Conclusion: 源点数在点估计意义下不可观测：方法应报告"给定规模解释成立时的误差容差"，因为该容差无需真值与误差模型即可在真实数据上测量，而缺失参考的组成结构比数量更关键。

Abstract: Phylogenetic distance deconvolution represents mixture-to-reference distances by weighted sources placed on a reference tree. We study the minimum number of strictly interior sources that explains a distance profile, and what incomplete references cost.
  On a positive-rational reduced tree we give an exact algorithm with 2^O(K) poly(B) bit complexity for a free positive mean pendant and any nonempty set of observed leaves. With arbitrary missing references and a prescribed pendant the problem is W[1]-hard in the source budget K, even on positive binary trees supplied with a strict explanation using at most K+1 sources.
  We then use the algorithm as an instrument. Removing references can only lower the minimum source count, never raise it. The direction is a theorem at every error tolerance and held without exception in 23,545 draws, so which references are missing matters more than how many. Two adversarially chosen references suffice, at the median, to destroy uniqueness of the minimum explanation, and that number does not grow with panel size (Spearman 0.203, p = 0.15, over 20 to 353 references) where random loss scales linearly. Discarding 7,931 of 10,575 references from a real 16S panel at random costs 1.8% of the tolerance at which one source suffices. Discarding comparably many as whole clades costs 90%.
  That tolerance needs neither ground truth nor an error model, which makes the model measurable on real data. Published distance estimates exceed it in 296 of 296 cases. The mixture inferred by the published heuristic reproduces real observations better than the configuration that generated them in 282 of 296. The phenomenon is not unobservable in practice. It is unobservable through a point estimate of the count. Methods for this problem should report the tolerance at which an explanation of a given size exists.

### [3] [From Turing Patterns to Spike Self-Replication and Nucleation in a Growing-Domain Tulip Breaking Virus Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30855)
*Fanya Xia,Chunyi Gai*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文在小病毒扩散系数的渐近极限下，研究郁金香碎色病毒（TBV）反应-扩散模型中局域尖峰斑图的形成，用匹配渐近展开推导尖峰自复制与尖峰成核两种分岔机制的临界条件，并结合图灵失稳分析给出斑图形成机制的相图，发现图灵失稳区域完全位于尖峰成核可发生的参数范围内。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 已有 TBV 反应-扩散模型能生成花瓣上的条纹色素斑图，但随花瓣（区域）生长而不断产生新尖峰的机制尚不清楚；两种可能机制——尖峰自复制与尖峰成核——的竞争关系及判据缺乏定量刻画，需要弄清哪种机制先发生以及与图灵失稳的关系，以理解病毒感染郁金香色素斑图的形成与演化。

Method: 在小区间半长 L 缓慢增大、病毒扩散系数很小的渐近极限下，用匹配渐近展开分析尖峰斑图：将尖峰自复制刻画为内层尖峰核心问题的鞍结分岔，将尖峰成核刻画为非线性外层问题的折叠（fold）；据此推导两种机制的临界条件，并构造相图预测随 L 增大哪种机制先出现、或成核是否缺失；再与均匀稳态的图灵失稳分析结合，得到斑图形成机制的相图。

Result: 得到了尖峰自复制与尖峰成核的临界条件，以及随 L 增大时预测先发生何种机制（或成核缺失）的相图；与图灵失稳分析结合后给出斑图形成机制的相图。一个有趣的发现是：在 TBV 模型中，图灵失稳区域完全落在尖峰成核可能发生的参数区域内。

Conclusion: 尖峰自复制（内核鞍结分岔）与尖峰成核（外层问题折叠）是 TBV 模型中随区域增长产生新尖峰的两种不同分岔机制，可用渐近分析给出判据与相图；图灵失稳参数区被尖峰成核区完全包含。这些结果为理解病毒感染郁金香色素斑图的形成与演化机制提供了进一步的理论线索。

Abstract: A recently proposed reaction-diffusion model for the tulip breaking virus (TBV) has been shown to generate striped pigmentation patterns on growing tulip petals. In this paper, we investigate the formation of localized spike patterns in this model in the asymptotic limit of small viral diffusivity. It is known that two distinct bifurcation mechanisms can occur which generate new spikes as the domain half-length $L$ slowly increases: spike self-replication and spike nucleation. Self-replication occurs through a saddle-node bifurcation of the inner spike core problem, whereas nucleation is associated with a fold of the nonlinear outer problem. Using matched asymptotic expansions, we derive critical conditions for these mechanisms and construct phase diagrams that predict which mechanism occurs first as $L$ increases, or whether nucleation is absent. We further combine these results with a Turing instability analysis of the homogeneous steady state to obtain a phase diagram of pattern-forming mechanisms. An interesting finding is that, for the TBV model, the Turing-unstable region lies entirely within the parameter regime where spike nucleation is possible. These results provide further insight into the mechanisms underlying the formation and evolution of pigmentation patterns in virus-infected tulips.

### [4] [Generalized modeling reveals stabilizing mechanisms in cross-feeding microbial communities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31154)
*Tom Clegg,Thilo Gross*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文构建并分析了一个刻画资源介导的竞争与交叉喂养相互作用的广义微生物群落动力学模型，在不同规模、结构与非线性的模型集合中评估群落稳定性，揭示了系统规模、自限制、代谢效率与可塑性以及层级营养结构对稳定性的关键作用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 理解微生物群落为何稳定、为何能抵抗扰动是微生物生态学的核心问题，但传统理论方法通常需对模型的结构、参数和函数形式作严格假设，难以获得一般性洞见。

Method: 采用广义建模方法，构建涵盖资源介导的竞争与交叉喂养相互作用的广义微生物群落动力学模型，并在规模、结构和非线性动力学各异的模型大集合上分析群落稳定性。

Result: 分析表明群落稳定性受系统规模的约束，并识别出关键的新陈代谢与动力学驱动因子，突出自限制、代谢效率和可塑性的核心作用；同时还发现群落结构可调节稳定性，群落能够通过组织为层级化的营养结构来抵消去稳定的交叉喂养环路。

Conclusion: 研究为微生物群落稳定性提供了新认识，并提供了一个灵活的分析框架，可供未来研究探索群落对扰动的响应。

Abstract: Understanding what makes microbial communities stable and able to resist perturbation is a fundamental question in microbial ecology. Theoretical approaches to this problem typically require making strict assumptions about the structure, parameters and functional forms used in models, hindering general insight. Here, we address this problem by developing and analyzing a generalized model of microbial community dynamics, capturing resource-mediated competitive and cross-feeding interactions between populations. The generalized modeling approach allows us to assess the stability of microbial systems across large ensembles of models with variable size, structure and non-linear dynamics. Our analysis shows how community stability is constrained by system size and identifies its key metabolic and dynamical drivers, highlighting the central roles of self-limitation, metabolic efficiency and plasticity. Furthermore, we show how the structure of communities mediates their stability, allowing communities to counteract the destabilizing cross-feeding loops through organization into hierarchical trophic structures. Together, these results provide new insight into the stability of microbial communities and offer a flexible analytical framework for future work to explore the responses of communities to perturbation.
