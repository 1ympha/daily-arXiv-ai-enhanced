### [1] [ST-FateFlow: Continuous Gene-Spatial Reconstruction with Differentiation-Potential Learning from Time-Resolved Spatial Transcriptomics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22256)
*Zhe Yu,Xiaobo Sun,Hao Wu*

Main category: q-bio.GN

TL;DR: 本文提出ST-FateFlow，一种基于流匹配（flow matching）的框架，用于联合学习基因表达与空间坐标的速度场，实现时间分辨空间转录组数据的连续动态建模与分化潜能推断。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 时间分辨空间转录组能同时刻画细胞状态与空间组织随时间的变化，但现有技术依赖破坏性采样，只能在少量离散时间点、来自独立样本上观测，且跨时间点没有细胞配对信息。这导致难以重建未观测阶段的基因表达与组织结构，也难以刻画分化相关的动态过程。

Method: ST-FateFlow采用流匹配框架：一是联合学习基因表达速度场与空间坐标速度场，用以插值缺失时间点并连续建模转录演化与组织重塑；二是独立学习一个以时间为条件的分化潜能函数，其数值可将细胞状态沿推断的分化进程排序，并支持分化相关转录变化分析。

Result: 在多个时间分辨空间转录组数据集上，ST-FateFlow能有效捕捉协同的基因表达动态与空间动态；案例研究表明，所学到的潜能景观与已知的发育和再生轨迹相一致，并有助于识别与分化进程相关的候选基因。

Conclusion: ST-FateFlow通过流匹配实现基因-空间连续动力学与分化潜能的联合学习，为无配对、离散采样的时间分辨空间转录组数据提供了插值与分化轨迹推断的统一建模方案。

Abstract: Time-resolved spatial transcriptomics provides a powerful view of dynamic biological processes by measuring cellular states together with their spatial organization across time. However, destructive profiling and experimental cost restrict observations to a few discrete time points collected from independent samples, with no known cell pairing across time. This makes it difficult to reconstruct gene expression and tissue organization at unobserved stages and to characterize differentiation-associated dynamics. Here, we introduce ST-FateFlow, a flow-matching framework for continuous gene-spatial dynamics and differentiation-potential learning. ST-FateFlow jointly learns gene-expression and spatial-coordinate velocity fields, enabling interpolation of missing time points and continuous modeling of transcriptional evolution and tissue remodeling. In parallel, it independently learns a time-conditioned differentiation potential, whose values organize cell states along inferred differentiation progressions and support the analysis of differentiation-associated transcriptional changes. Across multiple time-resolved spatial transcriptomics datasets, ST-FateFlow effectively captures coordinated gene-expression and spatial dynamics. Case studies further show that the learned potential landscapes are consistent with known developmental and regenerative trajectories and help identify candidate genes associated with differentiation progression.

### [2] [OCCAM: Improved Single-Cell Representation Learning and Perturbation Effect Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21959)
*Ihab Bendidi,Kian Kenyon-Dean,Alisandra K. Denton,Auguste Genovesio,Emmanuel Noutahi*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 该论文提出Occam——一个基于多层感知机（MLP）的框架，用于细胞表征学习与遗传扰动的效应预测；它在跨细胞情境、跨基因组合的泛化上表现优于再训练竞品与直接迁移基线。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 预测遗传扰动的效应需要模型能泛化到未观察过的细胞情境与基因组合，而已有方法多局限于已观察的情境与组合，跨情境和组合泛化能力不足。

Method: Occam以MLP为核心，通过去噪学习细胞表征，并融合基因互作特征、文本特征与蛋白序列特征；在小样本跨情境设定下（扰动与细胞情境各自在训练中出现但从未共同出现）进行预测，并对双扰动通过组合单基因响应来建模。

Result: 在扰动信息聚合表征得分与批整合下的细胞类型保持上取得最佳；跨情境小样本预测优于四个重训练竞品及同一扰动在其他细胞系的效应直接迁移；无微调时在留出细胞系中区分扰动最优；双扰动预测较竞品与单基因效应简单相加降低表达误差，且对强/弱加性响应均有效；但精确预测表达水平仍然困难。

Conclusion: 结果表明，简单的MLP即可有效支撑跨情境、跨基因组合的细胞表征学习与扰动效应预测。

Abstract: Predicting the effects of genetic perturbations requires models that generalize beyond observed cell contexts and gene combinations. We introduce Occam, a multilayer perceptron (MLP) framework for representation learning and perturbation-effect prediction. Occam combines cell representations learned through denoising with gene interaction, text, and protein-sequence features. It achieves the highest aggregate representation scores for perturbation information and cell-type preservation with batch integration. In cross-context few-shot prediction, a perturbation and a cell context are each observed during training, but never together. Occam better captures the measured response patterns for these unobserved combinations than four retrained competitors or direct transfer of the same perturbation's measured effects from other training cell lines. Without fine-tuning, Occam best distinguishes perturbations among the evaluated models and simple baselines in cell lines held out from predictor training, across seen and unseen assays. Predicting expression levels accurately in these settings remains challenging. For double perturbations, which target two genes simultaneously, Occam composes learned single-gene responses to better capture the combined response and reduce expression error compared with competing models and summed single-gene effects. These gains extend to both strongly additive and weakly additive responses. Together, these results support simple MLPs for cell representation learning and perturbation-effect prediction across contexts and gene combinations.

Cocho,Javier de las Heras,Niklas Darin,Dorothea Möslinger,Peter Witters,Ina Knerr,Giancarlo La Marca,Ans T. van der Ploeg,Hidde H. Huidekoper,Terry G. J. Derks,Francjan J. van Spronsen,Sandra D. Körver-Keularts,Katja Benedikte Prestø Elgstøen,George J. G. Ruijter,J J M Jans,Amy Harms,Thomas Hankemeier,Maurizio Scarpa,Ines Thiele,Shamima Rahman,Stefan Kölker,Ronan M. T. Fleming*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 论文提出一个基于基数优化的数学框架，用于罕见病多中心临床研究设计中的实验室选择与患者招募分配决策。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 罕见病临床研究面临合格患者数量有限、地理分布广的挑战，需多中心协作，并依据产能、成本和能力战略性地选择分析实验室；现有启发式方法难以兼顾临床相关性、可行性、预算与物流因素。

Method: 构建基数优化（cardinality optimisation）数学模型，整合招募产出、预算约束与实验室产能限制；在Recon4IMD项目中实例化（24个医疗中心、8个代谢组学实验室、目标招募1900名确诊患者），并与比例启发式和贪心边际分配基线进行仿真对比。

Result: 在多中心仿真场景中，优化模型持续获得更高招募产出；无约束条件下代谢组学分析成本降低约30%；在实验室产能逐步收紧时，比例启发式与贪心基线均变为不可行，而优化模型仍保持可行性并控制成本。

Conclusion: 集成优化策略能有效改善资源受限的复杂研究环境中的患者招募、平台选择与样本分配，具有实际推广价值。

Abstract: Clinical research in rare diseases is challenged by the limited availability and broad geographical distribution of eligible patients. This necessitates multi-centre collaboration and strategic selection of analytical laboratories based on capacity, cost, and capability. We present a mathematical framework based on cardinality optimisation to support key operational decisions in study design, balancing clinical relevance, feasibility, budgetary constraints, and logistical factors. The framework was implemented in the Reconstruction and Computational Modelling for Inherited Metabolic Diseases (Recon4IMD) project, aiming to recruit 1,900 diagnosed patients across 24 healthcare centres and eight metabolomic laboratories. Compared to both proportional heuristic and greedy marginal allocation baselines, the proposed optimisation model consistently achieved higher recruitment yields in simulated multi-centre scenarios and reduced metabolomic analysis costs by approximately 30% under unconstrained conditions. Notably, both the heuristic and greedy allocation baselines became infeasible under progressively tighter laboratory capacity constraints, whereas the optimised model maintained feasibility and cost control. These findings highlight the value of integrated optimisation strategies for improving recruitment, platform selection, and sample allocation in complex, resource-limited research settings.
