### [1] [Hepatitis C Virus Genotyping with a Transformer Neural Network](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19415)
*Ariella Aro,Taimá Furuyama,Marcelo R. S. Briones,Luis Mário R. Janini,Isabel M. V. Guedes de Carvalho,Fernando Antoneli*

Main category: q-bio.GN

TL;DR: 本文研究了基于Transformer的模型在丙型肝炎病毒（HCV）基因型和亚型分类中的应用。通过对2,881条HCV全基因组序列进行微调，发现同质片段化和平衡样本分布的微调策略能达到98.48%-100%的精确度，最佳亚型分类方案达到99.89%准确率和99.87%精确度；而数据不平衡的片段化策略精确度仅为48.12%，表明数据预处理策略对模型性能至关重要。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探索Transformer模型在基因序列分类中的适用性，特别是评估不同微调策略（片段化方法、数据量、标签方式）对HCV基因型和亚型预测性能的影响，并找出最优的数据处理方案。

Method: 使用来自洛斯阿拉莫斯HCV序列数据库的2,881条全基因组序列（包含基因型1-6），采用多种数据集配置进行Transformer模型微调，比较不同片段化方式、每文件数据量和标签策略对分类性能的影响，并排除了样本不足的基因型7和8。

Result: 同质片段化和平衡样本分布使基因型分类精确度达到98.48%-100%；数据不平衡和任意样本分配时精确度仅48.12%。亚型分类最佳方案达到99.89%准确率和99.87%精确度。增加额外基因型会导致性能轻微下降，基因型5因低频出现误差率高。

Conclusion: 当微调数据集包含适当片段化、分布和标签的基因序列时，基于Transformer的神经网络能够实现高性能，是HCV基因型和亚型分类的有前景方法。数据预处理策略（片段化和样本平衡）是决定模型性能的关键因素。

Abstract: This study aims to explore the applicability of Transformer-based models for genetic sequence classification by evaluating their performance in predicting hepatitis C virus (HCV) genotypes and subtypes after fine-tuning. A total of 2,881 HCV whole-genome sequences obtained from the Los Alamos HCV Sequence Database were used, including genotypes 1 to 6 and all confirmed subtypes. Genotypes 7 and 8 were excluded due to an insufficient number of samples. The fine-tuning process was based on several datasets that differed in fragmentation method, data volume per file, and labeling. In genotype classification, fine-tuning strategies employing homogeneous fragmentation and balanced sample distribution resulted in higher performance, with precision ranging from 98.48% to 100%. In contrast, fine-tuning conducted using a fragmentation strategy that caused data imbalance, along with an arbitrary distribution of samples across training files, achieved a precision of 48.12%, which is considered low compared with other models. This configuration, which was also manually evaluated, resulted in a high error rate in genotype 5 prediction due to its low frequency in the datasets used. In subtype classification, the best-performing fine-tuning approach achieved 99.89% accuracy and 99.87% precision. Models that included additional genotypes showed a slight decrease in performance due to the increased complexity of the task. This study demonstrates that, when fine-tuning datasets contain properly fragmented, distributed, and labeled genetic sequences, Transformer-based neural networks can achieve high performance and are a promising approach for HCV genotype and subtype classification.

### [2] [A stochastic dose-response framework for environmentally persistent pathogens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19607)
*Mahmudul Bari Hridoy,Arik Hartmann,Kate E. Langwig,Joseph R. Hoyt,Lauren M. Childs*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文提出了一个随机剂量-反应框架，整合宿主感染动态与环境病原体储库，用于研究环境持久性病原体的入侵、传播和灭绝风险，并以蛇真菌病为案例验证了季节性在疫情动态中的关键作用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 环境持久性病原体通过直接接触和环境污染间接传播，其感染风险取决于剂量与暴露时机，但现有模型对剂量-反应、随机性和季节性的综合影响理解不足。

Method: 构建随机连续时间马尔可夫链模型，显式建模环境病原体储库，采用分支过程近似估计灭绝概率，并纳入宿主易感性与接触率的季节性变化，结合数值模拟与全局敏感性分析。

Result: 数值模拟显示剂量-反应函数影响疫情爆发起点和感染水平；季节性创造了高/低灭绝风险窗口，灭绝风险强烈依赖病原引入的季节时机和传播途径；全局敏感性分析揭示了随机性、非线性剂量-反应和季节时序对疫情动态的综合影响。

Conclusion: 随机剂量-反应框架有效阐明了环境传播疾病系统的入侵与持久性机制，强调了剂量-反应非线性和季节性时机对疫情控制与风险评估的重要性。

Abstract: Infectious diseases caused by environmentally persistent pathogens can strongly affect host populations as transmission occurs not only through direct host-host contact but also via indirect exposure to contaminated environments. While in some systems environmental reservoirs help sustain exposure even when infected host numbers are low, reliance on environmental transmission pathway may also increase pathogen extinction risk. Infection risk may depend on both pathogen dose and timing of exposure. Thus, understanding how dose-response, stochasticity, and seasonal changes in host susceptibility or contact rates shape pathogen invasion and persistence remains an important challenge for environmentally transmitted disease systems. To address this, we develop a stochastic dose-response framework that integrates host infection dynamics with an explicit environmental pathogen reservoir. Transmission occurs through both direct contact with infectious hosts and indirect environmental exposure, with infection probability governed by dose-response functions. We focus on stochastic continuous-time Markov chain formulation and use branching process approximation to estimate disease extinction probabilities when infected hosts or environmental pathogen loads are low. We extend the framework to include seasonality in host susceptibility, environmental contact, and host-host contact. As a case study, we apply the model to snake fungal disease. Numerical simulations show that dose-response influences epidemic takeoff and infection levels, while seasonality creates windows of high and low extinction risk. These extinction risks depend strongly on the route and timing of introduction within the seasonal cycle. These results, coupled with global sensitivity analysis, illustrate how stochasticity, nonlinear dose-response, and seasonal timing shape outbreak dynamics for environmentally persistent pathogens.

### [3] [Correlations at criticality in ecological communities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20086)
*Akiva Goldberg,Nadav M. Shnerb*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文表明，生态群落即使接近动力学稳定性的临界边界，也不一定会表现出预测的强成对丰度相关性。原因是光谱软性（soft mode）不一定保证随机可见性——软模式必须获得足够的环境强迫才能主导其他模式产生的涨落背景。作者在三种生态场景（合成可行群落、迁移组装群落、反复多样化群落）中验证了这一机制，均未发现近临界系统应有的近完美成对相关性。因此，接近生态临界性不一定在等时成对相关性中可见。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 生态群落不断被入侵、排斥和多样化重塑，自然驱动它们趋近于动力学稳定性的边界。在此边界附近，软模式（soft mode）弛豫越来越慢，在随机强迫下预计会主导涨落，将动力学降维到一维，并产生强烈的正负丰度相关性。这些相关性因此被提议作为迫近转变（临界状态）的信号。作者想要检验这一预期是否始终成立。

Method: 作者通过理论分析指出：光谱软性并不保证随机可见性（stochastic visibility）——软模式必须接收足够的环境强迫，才能主导其余模式产生的涨落背景。随后在三种生态场景中检验了这一机制：合成可行群落、通过区域库迁移组装的局部群落、以及反复多样化产生的群落。

Result: 在全部三种生态场景中，群落都趋近于边际稳定性，但并未出现单模式图像所预测的近完美成对相关性。这表明即使在任意接近临界点的条件下，谱软性也不一定表现为可观测的涨落主导模式。

Conclusion: 接近生态临界性不必然在等时成对相关性中可见。单模式图像是一个过于简化的预期，实际中软模式的随机可见性取决于其接收环境强迫的程度。因此，依赖成对相关性作为临界转变信号的预警方法需要重新审视其有效性边界。

Abstract: Ecological communities are continually reshaped by invasion, exclusion, and diversification, processes that naturally drive them toward the boundary of dynamical stability. Near such a boundary, a soft mode relaxes increasingly slowly and, under stochastic forcing, is expected to dominate the fluctuations, effectively reducing the dynamics to one dimension and generating strong positive and negative abundance correlations. Such correlations have therefore been proposed as signatures of an imminent transition. Here we show that this expectation can fail even arbitrarily close to criticality. The reason is that spectral softness does not guarantee stochastic visibility: the soft mode must receive enough environmental forcing to dominate the fluctuation background generated by the remaining modes. We demonstrate this mechanism in three ecological scenarios: a synthetic feasible community, a local community assembled by immigration from a regional pool, and a community generated by repeated diversification. In all three, communities approach marginal stability without developing the near-perfect pairwise correlations predicted by the single-mode picture. Thus, proximity to ecological criticality need not be visible in equal-time pairwise correlations.

### [4] [Novel models of trait evolution via an expansion of Lande's fitness function: The Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process meets the Little Prince's boa](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20232)
*Jose M Ponciano,Claire Godineau,Laura Jimenez,Nicholas Kortessis,Rosana Zenil-Ferguson,Robert D Holt*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 该论文重新审视并推广了Lande（1976）的适应性地形图理论，将其从单一适应度组分扩展到两个适应度组分的情形。研究发现双组分适应度函数的最优值是各组分最优值的加权平均，权重由稳定选择强度决定；时空异质性会产生不对称、双峰等多种新型适应度函数形状，并衍生出一类新的随机微分方程进化模型。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Lande 1976年关于表型适应性地形图的经典论文奠定了进化变化理解的基础，但其原始推导仅针对单一适应度组分。自然系统往往涉及多个适应度组分（如生存和繁殖）的共同作用，需要将理论推广到多适应度组分的情形，以更真实地反映进化动态并提供新的可检验预测。

Method: 作者将Lande原始的Wright基因型适应性地形图方程推广到两个适应度组分的情形，推导了更新的适应度函数形式；通过数学分析揭示其最优值、形状（对称性、双峰性等）与选择强度之间的关系；结合Lande的方法生成Ornstein-Uhlenbeck（OU）模型；并使用已发表文献中的案例研究进行演示。

Result: 推导出双适应度组分的适应性地形图：其最优值为各组分最优值的加权平均（权重为各组分稳定选择强度）；时空异质性导致适应度函数产生新的形状（不对称、双峰或缺失）；产生了此前未被认识的一类随机微分方程模型族，为非高斯适应度函数提供了机制性解释。

Conclusion: 该研究为非高斯适应度函数（自然界中常被观察到）提供了机制层面的理论依据，为检验产生非高斯适应度函数的替代模型提供了路径，并为未来研究生态与进化动态的相互作用（如进化拯救）奠定了理论基础。

Abstract: Adaptive topographies form the foundation for much of our understanding of evolutionary change. Lande's 1976 influential paper on the adaptive topography of phenotypes demonstrated how the concept is inherent in both phenotypic and genetic models of evolution, and how the concept can be used to test evolutionary hypotheses given data. Here, we revisit and generalize Lande's original derivation of an equation analogous to Wright's genotypic adaptive topography to the case of two fitness components. A move to two fitness components yields novel predictions about the shape and mechanistic underpinnings of the adaptive topography. The optimum of this updated fitness function is a weighted average of the optima of the two fitness components, with weights given by the relative strengths of stabilizing selection on each component. Temporal or spatial heterogeneity in the strengths of selection for each fitness component create novel shapes (asymmetry, bi-modality, or lack thereof) of the overall fitness function, a possibility demonstrated with a case-study from the published literature. Finally, when combined with Lande's approach to generate an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (OU) model for the evolution of the average phenotype, our fitness formulation leads to a previously unrecognized family of stochastic differential equation models of trait evolution. These results provide mechanistic justification for non-Gaussian fitness functions (often observed in natural systems), provide a path for testing alternative models generating non-Gaussian fitness functions, and pave the way for future study of the interplay of ecological and evolutionary dynamics, such as in the study of evolutionary rescue.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Abstract: Summary: Dynamic scientific machine learning (SciML) models that combine mechanistic ordinary differential equations (ODEs) with machine learning (ML) components have applications ranging from learning unknown biological processes to integrating auxiliary data modalities into dynamic modelling. Availability and implementation: PEtab SciML is available on GitHub (https://github.com/PEtab-dev/petab_sciml).
