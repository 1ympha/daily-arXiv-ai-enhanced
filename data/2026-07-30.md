### [1] [Hybrid SINDy-EnKF in Learning Chikungunya Dynamics from Incomplete, Noisy or Partially Observed Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27137)
*Bernard Asamoah Afful,Changhong Mou,Luis Gordillo*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种结合SINDy和EnKF的混合数据驱动模型框架，用于在部分观测和噪声条件下改进基孔肯雅病毒传播的动力学子模型预测与状态重建。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有基孔肯雅病毒传播的机理模型依赖不确定参数或部分观测数据，限制了理论模型在疾病传播理解与预测中的应用。

Method: 将稀疏非线性动力学识别（SINDy）与集合卡尔曼滤波（EnKF）相结合，利用EnKF对含噪观测数据进行同化，修正SINDy模型的预测状态并推断未观测变量。

Result: 该方法在部分可观测条件下提高了预测精度，并能良好重建未观测轨迹；单独SINDy对噪声高度敏感，而嵌入EnKF后有效克服了这一问题。

Conclusion: 混合SINDy-EnKF框架能够在实际流行病监测常见的有限观测条件下实现更准确的传播预测与状态推断。

Abstract: Current mechanistic models for the transmission dynamics of the Chikungunya virus (CHIKV) rely on uncertain parameters or partially observed data. This limitation challenges the use of theoretical models for understanding and forecasting disease spread. Here we present a hybrid, data-driven model framework that combines Sparse Identification of Nonlinear Dynamics (SINDy) with the Ensemble Kalman Filter (EnKF) for sequential data assimilation. Our numerical experiments show that this approach improves prediction accuracy and provides a good reconstruction of unobserved trajectories under partial observability, a common constraint in real-world epidemiological surveillance. SINDy can be applied to epidemic trajectories, recovering the underlying equations in noise-free conditions. However, standalone SINDy is highly sensitive to noise, leading to spurious terms and poor performance. Hence, we embed the identification procedure within an EnKF framework, which assimilates noisy observations to correct forecast states from the SINDy-derived model and to infer unobserved state variables.
