### [1] [Large-scale AI-Ready Data for Anti-Cancer Drug Response Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11444)
*Vincent Lavelle,Yitan Zhu,Kaitlyn Marlor,Thomas Brettin,Rick Stevens*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文通过大规模整合PharmacoDB等药理学数据源，大幅扩展了IMPROVE基准数据集，加入了超过5万种化合物和数百万条药物响应测量数据，并评估了该扩展数据集对药物响应预测模型性能的影响。结果显示，在药物盲和不相交数据划分下模型泛化能力显著提升，扩展数据集可作为社区资源用于抗肿瘤药物发现。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 药物响应预测模型在抗癌药物发现中潜力巨大，但受限于数据集规模小、癌症和化学空间覆盖率不足，以及缺乏统一的基准评估框架。现有IMPROVE项目提供了标准化评估协议，但训练数据的规模和多样性仍是制约模型泛化能力的关键瓶颈，亟需更大规模、更多样化的数据资源。

Method: 基于IMPROVE基准框架，大规模整合来自PharmacoDB及其他较小数据源的药理学数据，构建包含数百万药物响应测量、多组学覆盖和超5万种化合物的扩展数据集；随后使用扩展数据集和原始IMPROVE数据集分别训练DRP模型，在统一测试集上采用药物盲、癌症盲和不相交数据划分等多种评估策略进行性能对比。

Result: 癌症盲评估下扩展数据集与原始基准性能相当；但在药物盲和不相交数据划分下，基于扩展数据集训练的模型表现出一致的性能提升，表明模型对未见化合物的泛化能力显著增强。

Conclusion: 扩展后的IMPROVE数据集为DRP模型开发提供了更丰富的数据基础，可作为社区共享资源，有助于推动新型抗肿瘤药物的发现。

Abstract: Drug response prediction (DRP) models are an active area of research in pharmacogenomics, with growing potential to accelerate the identification of effective anticancer drugs. However, their predictive performance is often constrained by limited dataset scale and insufficient coverages of cancer and chemical spaces. In addition, inconsistent benchmarking practices hinder reliable comparison across models. Standardized frameworks, such as the Innovative Methodologies and New Data for Predictive Oncology Model Evaluation (IMPROVE) project, provide unified data schemas and evaluation protocols for consistent benchmarking, but improving model generalizability requires larger and more diverse training data. In this work, we substantially expand the IMPROVE benchmark through large-scale integration of pharmacogenomic data, primarily from PharmacoDB, together with additional smaller data sources. The expanded resource includes millions of drug response measurements, broader multi-omics coverage, and a major increase in chemical diversity, adding more than 50,000 compounds. To evaluate the impact of the new dataset compared to the original IMPROVE benchmark dataset, we trained DRP models using the two datasets and assess their prediction performance using a common test set and several evaluation strategies, including drug-blind, cancer-blind, and disjoint data splits. While cancer-blind performance remained comparable to the original benchmark, models trained on the expanded dataset showed consistent improvements in drug-blind and disjoint settings, indicating enhanced generalization to previously unseen compounds. These results position the expanded dataset as a community resource that provides a richer foundation for developing DRP models intended to aid in the discovery of novel anticancer drugs.

### [2] [Probing and steering biology across Boltz-1s trunk-diffusion boundary](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11475)
*Piotr Jedryszek,Tongmeng Xie,Adam Winnifrith,Alexander Hasson,Weronika Ślesak,George Wicks,Toby Winnifrith,Oliver M. Crook*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文通过线性探针、稀疏自编码器（SAEs）和因果干预分析了Boltz-1（AlphaFold3类结构预测器）中Pairformer躯干与扩散模块之间的信息传递。发现二级结构信息在跨架构边界时几乎不变地传递，而序列化学信息被显著衰减；且在躯干最终单表示上的因果干预显示，可解码的方向并不一定具有因果影响力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 理解AlphaFold3类模型中生物信息如何从表示躯干跨越到扩散模块的架构边界，这是一个尚未被充分理解的关键问题。该问题对于解释和提升结构预测模型的可靠性具有重要意义。

Method: 使用线性探针（linear probes）、稀疏自编码器（SAEs）和因果干预（causal interventions），对Boltz-1的Pairformer躯干和扩散模块的逐残基激活进行分析。测试了二级结构、无序性、氨基酸身份、信号肽、二硫键注释等生物属性的可解码性，并对躯干最终单表示进行方向干预以检验因果影响。

Result: 1) 躯干中几何（二级结构、无序性）和序列化学（氨基酸身份、信号肽、二硫键注释）均可线性解码；2) 扩散模块中二级结构几乎不变地传递，而序列化学信息显著衰减；3) 螺旋和卷曲方向的干预可剂量依赖性地改变预测结构，但高预测性的β链方向（F1=0.82）干预后无显著影响，表明线性可解码不等于因果影响力；4) 稀疏SwissProt注释的探针得分显著低于密集DSSP标签；5) 监督探针优于单个SAE特征。

Conclusion: 线性可解码性不一定意味着在该位点具有因果影响力，这强调了需要结合探针分析和因果干预来全面理解模型内部表征。作者发布了训练的SAE、激活数据和代码以促进进一步研究。

Abstract: AlphaFold3-class structure predictors pair a representational trunk, which processes sequence and context, with a diffusion module, which generates atomic coordinates. How biological information changes as it crosses this architectural boundary remains poorly understood. We analyze per-residue activations from the Pairformer trunk and diffusion module of Boltz-1 using linear probes, sparse autoencoders (SAEs), and causal interventions. From the trunk, both geometry (secondary structure, disorder) and sequence chemistry (amino-acid identity, signal peptides, disulfide-bond annotations) are linearly decodable. In the diffusion module, the two diverge. Secondary structure transfers essentially unchanged, whereas sequence chemistry is strongly attenuated. We then test whether decodable directions can steer the model, intervening on the final trunk single representation that conditions the diffusion module. Helix and coil directions change predicted structure dose-dependently against matched-norm random controls, but a beta-strand direction that is highly predictive (F1 =0.82) produces no measurable increase in strand content: linear decodability does not imply causal influence at the site we tested. The same probes also score markedly lower against sparse SwissProt annotations than against dense DSSP labels, because unannotated residues that the model gets right are charged as false positives; such scores are therefore lower bounds. Finally, supervised probes outscore single SAE features wherever a label already exists. We release the trained trunk and diffusion SAEs, Boltz-1 per-residue activations, and the analysis code.

### [3] [A multiscale theory based on metabolic scaling connects forest dynamics to tree-size distributions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11918)
*Christian Grilletta,Tommaso Anfodillo,Gaia Pasqualotto,Samir Suweis,Andrea Rinaldo,Amos Maritan,Davide Bernardi*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文提出了一个最小空间显式动力学框架，通过解析推导树木大小分布，揭示了森林群落中两个不同幂律标度区间的涌现机制，并展示了边界扰动对森林内部生态平衡的深远影响。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 物种大小、丰度与资源可用性之间的标度关系是生态学中最稳健的经验规律之一，但缺乏从生态过程出发的机制性解释。作者旨在弥合实证标度规律与生态机制之间的鸿沟。

Method: 构建了包含种子扩散、光照限制生长、局域竞争及基于代谢标度原理的全局资源约束的最小空间显式动力学框架，并推导出树木大小分布的解析解。

Result: 发现稳态下树木大小分布呈现两个不同幂律区间，其指数由资源竞争与空间竞争的相对强度控制；交叉点由种子注入与局域资源可用性的相互作用决定。此外，边界扰动可打破种间生态平衡，其影响可传播至远超单一植物扩散范围的森林内部深处。

Conclusion: 该工作为森林标度规律提供了统一的动力学视角，揭示了标度指数与森林状态之间的明确联系，其方法论可推广至更广泛的生物群落研究。

Abstract: Scaling relations linking species size, abundance, and resource availability are among the most robust empirical regularities in ecology. However, a mechanistic explanation for how these community-level laws emerge from ecological processes remains elusive. Here, we address this gap by developing a minimal spatially explicit dynamical framework for forest communities that incorporates seed dispersal, growth limited by local light availability, local competition, and global resource constraints grounded in metabolic scaling principles. By deriving an analytical solution for the tree-size distribution, we show that its stationary state exhibits two distinct power-law regimes whose exponents are controlled by the relative strength of resource and spatial competition. The crossover between these regimes is set by the interplay between seed injection and local resource availability, establishing an explicit link between the scaling exponent of the size distribution and forest condition. Finally, we show that boundary disturbances can break the ecological balance between competing species and induce effects that propagate deeply into the forest bulk, far beyond the single-plant dispersal range. Together, these results provide a unifying dynamical perspective on forest scaling laws with potential applications to a broad range of biological communities.

### [4] [Dynamics of fluctuating populations in multi-state switching environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12208)
*Mauro Mobilia*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 该论文研究多状态随机切换环境下两种竞争菌株（一株生长稍慢于另一株）的种群动力学，将环境建模为具有有限个中间状态及各自承载力的多状态切换模型，作为粗粒化的"丰盛-饥荒"周期描述，分析切换速率和承载力分布对种群统计量、固定概率和平均固定时间的影响。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 微生物种群通常在波动环境中进化，传统模型多采用二值切换（丰盛/稀缺）描述环境波动，但实验表明真实的"丰盛-饥荒"环境具有更复杂的中间状态和时间动态，需要更精细的模型来刻画环境渐变对菌株竞争的影响。

Method: 构建一个包含有限个中间状态、每个状态具有各自承载力的多状态随机切换模型，将该模型解释为粗粒化的"丰盛-饥荒"周期；通过计算分析和解析手段，研究切换速率与承载力分布对种群大小统计量、固定概率和平均固定时间的影响，并与二态环境模型的结果进行对比。

Result: 刻画了多态波动环境中的种群动态特征，揭示了切换速率和承载力分布如何影响种群统计量、固定概率和平均固定时间；通过与二态环境对比，阐明了环境波动的频率和幅度对粗粒化"丰盛-饥荒"周期中种群动力学的影响。

Conclusion: 多状态波动模型能更准确地描述"丰盛-饥荒"周期中的菌株竞争动态，环境波动的频率和幅度是影响种群演化结果的关键因素，该模型为理解复杂波动环境中的微生物进化提供了理论框架。

Abstract: Microbial populations generally evolve in fluctuating environments under time-varying conditions. These are often described by binary switching models, sometimes seen as coarse-grained feast-famine cycles, in which resource availability switches abruptly between abundant and scarce conditions. However, experimental studies suggest that feast-famine environments actually exhibit more complex temporal dynamics. Here, we study how two strains, one growing slightly slower than the other, compete for the same resources in fluctuating environments comprising a finite number of intermediate states, each having its own carrying capacity. Environmental switching between these states and their carrying capacities represents gradual changes in nutrient availability. This class of multi-state stochastic switching models can be interpreted as a coarse-grained description of feast--famine cycles and allows us to investigate strain competition under the gradual recovery and depletion of resources. By computational and analytical means, we characterise the population dynamics in these multi-state fluctuating environments. In particular, we study how the switching rates and distribution of carrying capacities affect the population-size statistics, fixation probability, and mean fixation time. By comparing these results with their counterparts in binary environments, we clarify how the frequency and amplitude of environmental fluctuations influence population dynamics in coarse-grained feast-famine cycles.
