### [1] [A Correspondence Score for Validating Learned Patient Representations in Clinical Tabular Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27489)
*Majid Lotfian Delouee,Sjors G. J. G. In 't Veld,Martijn C. Schut*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种对应分数（correspondence score），用于评估患者表征是否保留了原始临床数据中的相似性关系。该方法在四个全血细胞计数数据集上，对五种表格基础模型和六种经典变换方法生成的表征进行了评估，发现基础模型更稳定地保留患者关系，中间维度表现最佳。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 尽管患者相似性识别对队列发现、临床决策支持和个人化医疗至关重要，但现有研究未充分验证学习得到的患者表征是否真正保留了原始临床数据中的相似性关系。这使得在下游任务使用前难以判断表征质量的好坏。

Method: 提出一个对应分数，包含互补的关系保留度量指标，并通过贝叶斯优化估计数据集特定的权重，以直接评估表征对原始相似性关系的保留程度，且不依赖于标注结果。在四个血常规数据集上，系统比较五种表格基础模型和六种经典转换方法生成的表征。

Result: 不同方法和表征维度下的对应分数差异显著。中间维度通常最能保留患者关系，过强降维或扩展均会引入偏离。基础模型比经典方法更一致地保留对应关系，改进幅度约为5%到125%以上。

Conclusion: 提出的对应分数可在下游使用前评估表征质量，支持对表征方法和维度的明智选择，为临床表征质量评估提供了实用工具。

Abstract: Identifying patients with similar clinical characteristics is important for cohort discovery, clinical decision support, and personalized medicine. However, it is unclear whether learned patient representations preserve the similarities present in the original clinical data. We propose a correspondence score to directly assess how well these relationships are preserved, independently of labeled outcomes. The score combines complementary relationship-preservation metrics, with dataset-specific weights estimated through Bayesian optimization. We evaluated the approach on four complete blood count datasets using representations generated by five tabular foundation models and six classical transformation methods. Correspondence differed substantially across methods and representation dimensionalities. Patient relationships were generally best preserved at intermediate dimensionalities, while stronger reduction or expansion introduced greater deviations from the original data structure. Foundation models preserved correspondence more consistently than classical methods, with improvements ranging from approximately 5% to over 125%. The proposed score provides a way to evaluate representation quality before downstream use and supports informed selection of representation methods and dimensionalities.

### [2] [CIR-DDG: backbone-agnostic residual correction of antibody-antigen affinity changes with explicit cross-chain geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27530)
*Weilun Yu,Zhiheng Zou,Yonggui Huang,Honggang Qi,Geguang Pu,Yu Xiao,Gang Xu,Jiangtao Wang,Xi Chen*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文提出CIR-DDG，一种轻量级残差适配器，结合固定基础预测器与22个可解释的跨链距离、接触密度和位点-伙伴上下文描述符，改进突变诱导的蛋白质-蛋白质结合自由能变化预测。在SKEMPI 2.0的343个复合物评估中，对所有六个测试骨干网络在抗体-抗原界面突变上均有提升（Spearman相关提升0.0346-0.1296，RMSE降低0.0074-0.0408 kcal/mol）；在独立的SARS-CoV-2 RBD-ACE2深度突变扫描基准（3669个替换）上无需重训练即可迁移，绝对值界面Spearman相关提升0.026-0.081。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 突变诱导的蛋白质-蛋白质结合自由能变化预测对抗体亲和力成熟至关重要，但标签稀缺和复杂的界面几何限制了泛化能力。现有异构预测器在处理三维复合物时，可能在最终标量输出中丢失与突变最相关的跨链信号。

Method: 提出CIR-DDG轻量级残差适配器，将固定基础预测与22个可解释描述符（包括跨链距离、接触密度和位点-伙伴上下文）相结合，在基础预测器之上进行残差修正。

Result: 在SKEMPI 2.0的343个复合物五折评估中，六个骨干网络的抗体-抗原界面突变预测均有提升：Spearman相关提升0.0346-0.1296，RMSE降低0.0074-0.0408 kcal/mol。在独立的SARS-CoV-2 RBD-ACE2深度突变扫描基准（3669个替换）上无需重训练即可迁移，四个可评估骨干网络的界面Spearman相关均提升0.026-0.081。

Conclusion: 交叉验证探测、等容量控制和特征消融实验支持显式几何特征的互补性；学习到的几何修正可泛化到SKEMPI热力学测量之外的数据集，验证了方法的通用性和有效性。

Abstract: Motivation: Accurate prediction of mutation-induced protein--protein binding free-energy changes is important for antibody affinity maturation, yet scarce labels and complex interface geometry limit generalization. Heterogeneous predictors may process three-dimensional complexes without preserving the cross-chain signals most relevant to a mutation in their final scalar output. Results: We introduce CIR-DDG, a lightweight residual adapter that combines a fixed base prediction with 22 interpretable descriptors of cross-chain distance, contact density and site--partner context. In complex-level five-fold evaluation on SKEMPI 2.0 measurements from 343 complexes, CIR-DDG improved all six tested backbones on antibody--antigen interface mutations: Spearman correlation increased by 0.0346--0.1296, while RMSE decreased by 0.0074--0.0408\kcalmol. Cross-validated probing, equal-capacity controls and feature ablations support the complementarity of explicit geometry. On an independent SARS-CoV-2 RBD--ACE2 deep-mutational-scan benchmark of 3669 substitutions, the fold-specific adapters transferred without any retraining: the absolute interface Spearman correlation increased by 0.026--0.081 for all four evaluable backbones, showing that the learned geometric correction generalizes beyond SKEMPI thermodynamic measurements. Availability and implementation: CIR-DDG is available at https://github.com/ecnuabmlab/CIR-ddG.

### [3] [Efficient Auto-Interpretability of AI Models in Biology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27754)
*Piotr Jedryszek,Oliver M. Crook*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文提出了一套完整的稀疏自编码器（SAE）潜变量分析流程，包含跨种子词典稳定性优先排序、入侵者检测和可证伪预测三个步骤，并在Boltz-1 Pairformer模型上验证了其有效性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: SAE等可解释性方法有望推动AI模型在生物学等领域的科学发现，但一个潜变量只有在满足三个条件时才真正有用：连贯性、可描述性和描述具有预测力。然而这些问题常被混为一谈，缺乏系统化的分析流程。

Method: 将三个问题整合为统一流水线：1) 跨种子词典稳定性优先排序，筛选值得投入资源的潜变量；2) 入侵者检测任务，判断潜变量激活示例是否共享可识别模式；3) 独立步骤提出候选生物学描述并转化为可在硅片内验证的可证伪预测。

Result: 在Boltz-1 Pairformer上部署后，稳定性优先排序仅需约4.4倍更少的潜变量评估次数，实测成本降低5.2倍，同时恢复超过一半的潜变量；外部检验显示表面特征与其声称注释显著富集。结果还揭示跨种子稳定性可能偏向选择某些特征类型（如结构相关特征）而忽视其他类型（如功能相关特征）。

Conclusion: 本文为SAE潜变量分析提供了实用的端到端流程，显著提升了效率并验证了可解释性结果的可靠性，同时指出了跨种子稳定性选择偏差这一潜在张力，为后续研究指明了方向。

Abstract: Sparse autoencoders (SAEs), and other interpretability methods could turn AI models in Biology and other fields into engines of scientific discovery by explaining the superhuman capabilities of those models. However, a latent is only useful if we know three things: whether it is coherent, whether it can be described, and whether that description has predictive power. These questions are routinely conflated. We assemble them into a single pipeline and report the practical innovations each stage required. First, cross-seed dictionary stability prioritises which latents are worth spending resources to investigate. Second, an intruder-detection task asks whether a latents activating examples share a recognizable pattern. Third, a separate pass proposes a candidate biological description which we convert into falsifiable predictions which can be tested in silico. Deployed on the Boltz-1 Pairformer trunk, stability prioritisation finds interpretable latents using about 4.4 times fewer latent evaluations each, and at 5.2 times lower measured cost, while recovering over half of them, and the external check shows the surfaced motifs are significantly enriched for their claimed annotations. The results also suggest a possible tension: the cross- seed stability might be selecting for some types of features, like structure-related ones, much more than others, such as function-related features.

</details>
