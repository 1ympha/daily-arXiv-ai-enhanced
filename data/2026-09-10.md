### [1] [Persistence of n-Species Lotka-Volterra Models with Periodic Pulses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09343)
*Eleanor Courcelle,Jane Shaw MacDonald,Swati Patel*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文研究了受周期性乘法脉冲扰动影响的n物种种群模型的永久性问题，建立了一般性充分条件，并证明了在扰动下的鲁棒永久性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 周期性脉冲干预在生物种群管理中普遍存在，如化疗、农药施用和传染病治疗。需要一般性框架分析此类受重复离散扰动生态系统的持续性问题。

Method: 将脉冲系统转化为关联的自治连续时间动力系统，利用该对应关系将经典永久性理论扩展到周期性脉冲模型；在灭绝集的Morse分解上以加权长期增长率为条件，分离连续动力学与周期脉冲的贡献。

Result: 给出了永久性的充分条件；证明相同条件在连续动力学、脉冲周期和脉冲效应的充分小扰动下蕴含鲁棒永久性；以两个基于生物防治的Lotka-Volterra模型（化疗敏感与耐药癌细胞的竞争、农药与寄生蜂综合防治农业害虫）进行了说明。

Conclusion: 干预频率和强度与底层生态相互作用共同决定种群是否共存或被排除，为分析受重复离散扰动的生态系统的持久性提供了通用框架。

Abstract: Periodic impulsive interventions arise naturally in the management of biological populations, including chemotherapy, pesticide application, and infectious-disease treatment. We develop general conditions for permanence in n-species population models subject to periodic multiplicative pulse disturbances. Our main result provides a sufficient condition for permanence in terms of weighted long-term growth rates on a Morse decomposition of the extinction set, explicitly separating the contributions of continuous population dynamics from those of the periodic pulse. To establish this result, we transform the impulsive system into an associated autonomous continuous-time dynamical system and use this correspondence to extend classical permanence theory to periodically pulsed models. We further show that the same conditions imply robust permanence under sufficiently small perturbations to the continuous dynamics, pulse period, and pulse effects. We illustrate the framework with two Lotka-Volterra models motivated by biological control: competition between chemotherapy-sensitive and chemotherapy-resistant cancer cells, and integrated control of an agricultural pest using pesticides and parasitoids. These examples demonstrate how intervention frequency and intensity interact with underlying ecological interactions to determine whether populations coexist or are excluded. Our results provide a general framework for analyzing persistence in ecological systems subject to repeated discrete disturbances.

### [2] [Population Ecology of Tunes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09501)
*John M. McBride,Armand. M. Leroi*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文利用约2万首爱尔兰传统曲调13年的每周流行度数据，通过生态学出生过程模型检验中性、频率依赖和逐曲选择假说，揭示文化选择与多样性共存的机制。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 文化进化中的核心问题是：在选择作用下，文化曲库如何维持多样性。作者希望以爱尔兰传统音乐为可量化系统，探究文化变体的进化规律及一首曲调为何能脱颖而出。

Method: 使用约20,000首爱尔兰传统曲调13年的每周流行度数据，拟合生态学出生过程模型，分别在中性、频率依赖和逐曲选择三种假说下进行检验；结合社会与旋律特征解释适应度差异，并通过适应度随时间的变化与录音对比来识别机制。

Result: 发现曲调内在适应度存在差异，29%的适应度方差可由社会与旋律特征的混合解释；部分曲调因"组曲"传统通过联动被带动，类似遗传学中的选择性清除；通过适应度变化识别出一首长期沉寂曲调借由热门录音走红的机制；尽管存在方向性选择，曲库多样性仍因新作不断涌现而增加。

Conclusion: 选择与多样性可以共存于同一文化生态系统；爱尔兰传统音乐是研究文化变体进化、理解曲调走红原因的定量可处理系统。

Abstract: How cultural repertoires maintain diversity under selection is a fundamental question in cultural evolution. We address this using thirteen years of weekly popularity data for approximately 20,000 Irish traditional tunes, fitting ecological birth-process models under neutral, frequency-dependent, and per-tune selection hypotheses. We find strong evidence that tunes differ in intrinsic fitness - some are systematically more likely to be learned than others. We find that 29% of the variance in fitness can be explained by a mixture of social and melodic features. Some tunes appear to be carried along via linkage due to the tradition of playing tunes in sets, analogous to selective sweeps in genetics. By measuring changes in fitness over time and comparing this with recordings we precisely identify the mechanism by which a long-dormant tune can become fit through a popular recording. Despite the directional selection, repertoire diversity increases, driven by the continual arrival of new compositions. These results demonstrate that selection and diversity can coexist in a cultural ecosystem, and establish Irish traditional music as a quantitatively tractable system for studying the evolution of cultural variants and understanding what makes a tune stand out.

### [3] [Multi-Task Bacterial Colony Detection and Classification Using YOLOv8 with Edge Optimization for Resource-Constrained Deployment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09818)
*Belaguppa Manjunath Ashwin Desai,Rohan Rajesh,Shreyas Murthy,Pronama Biswas,Revathi Vaithiyanathan*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文基于AGAR数据集提出多任务深度学习框架，自动完成菌落计数（CFU）与菌种分类。基线多任务CNN因缺少空间定位在密集菌落场景表现受限，改用1024×1024输入的YOLOv8实现实例级检测，分类准确率98.13%、计数准确率98.27%。随后通过剪枝、ONNX转换与低精度推理优化，在树莓派4B上评估部署性能，为资源受限场景下的优化策略选择提供参考。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 人工菌落计数与分类是微生物学中的关键任务，但耗时费力，且在菌落密集的培养皿上易出现人为误差。因此需要自动化方法提升效率与准确性，并解决模型在资源受限设备上实际部署的问题。

Method: 使用AGAR数据集（18,000张图像，9,202训练/3,067测试）。先构建采用全局回归的自定义多任务CNN作为基线；针对其在密集菌落场景的不足，改用YOLOv8目标检测架构，输入分辨率1024×1024，实现实例级菌落检测与标签分配。之后通过非结构化与结构化剪枝、ONNX转换及FP32/FP16/INT8低精度推理进行优化，并在树莓派4B上评估。

Result: YOLOv8模型分类准确率达98.13%，计数准确率（10个菌落容差内）达98.27%。树莓派4B上，ONNX FP32与FP16在推理速度（约6.4s）与精度（MAE约2.20）间取得最佳平衡；非结构化剪枝保持精度（MAE约2.01）但无运行时收益；结构化剪枝导致精度显著下降（MAE约6.3）。

Conclusion: 实例级菌落检测对架构压缩较为敏感。ONNX FP32/FP16是资源受限实验室部署的较优选择，非结构化剪枝可保精度但难提速，结构化剪枝则明显损伤精度。研究为资源受限部署中的优化策略选择提供了实践指导。

Abstract: Manual counting and classification of bacterial colonies are critical yet labor-intensive tasks in microbiology, prone to human error particularly on densely populated plates. This work proposes a multi-task deep learning framework trained on the Annotated Germs for Automated Recognition (AGAR) dataset (18,000 images; 9,202 training / 3,067 testing) to automate Colony Forming Unit (CFU) enumeration and species classification. A custom multi-task CNN employing global regression served as the baseline, but demonstrated limited performance in clustered colony environments due to the absence of spatial localization. To address this, a YOLOv8 object detection architecture was adopted with high-resolution 1024x1024 inputs, enabling instance-level colony detection and label assignment. The model achieved a classification accuracy of 98.13% and a counting accuracy of 98.27% (within a 10-colony margin), demonstrating strong predictive capability. To bridge the gap between model performance and practical deployability, the trained model was optimized through unstructured and structured pruning, ONNX conversion, and reduced-precision inference (FP32, FP16, INT8). On a Raspberry Pi 4B, ONNX FP32 and FP16 variants offered the best balance between inference speed (~6.4s) and accuracy (MAE ~2.20). Unstructured pruning preserved predictive accuracy (MAE ~2.01) without runtime gains, while structured pruning resulted in significant accuracy degradation (MAE ~6.3), revealing the sensitivity of instance-level colony detection to architectural compression. These findings provide practical guidance for selecting optimization strategies in resource-constrained laboratory deployments.

### [4] [ADMET-EvO: a self-evolving scientific agent for sustained research across heterogeneous tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10121)
*Yiling Zhou,Yilin Wang,Jianmin Wang,Heqin Zhu,Zirui Wang,Chang-yu Hiesh,Kejun Ying,Jiaqi Wang,Yuzhi Xu,Tingjun Hou,Odin Zhang*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: ADMET-EvO是一个"证据门控"智能体，通过积累证据来修正假设与实验策略，在TDC的22项ADMET基准任务上取得96.77的最高任务归一化得分，并将累计拟合时间减少72.2%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 科学智能体可以超越自动化建模，利用积累的证据修正其研究问题与实验策略；难点在于如何在异构任务间持续这种适应性，同时避免决策过度拟合内部验证集。ADMET预测涉及多样的测定、数据集与化学域，是一个严苛的检验场景。

Method: 提出ADMET-EvO：一个证据门控智能体。它形式化端点、生成可证伪假设，并在数据、特征与模型三个轴上测试干预；将支持、拒绝与不确定的结果向前传递，以指导下一轮循环。

Result: 在22项任务的TDC ADMET基准上，任务归一化得分最高，为96.77；证据引导的选择在预设的非劣性边界内将累计拟合时间减少72.2%；另外形式化了43项毒性相关任务并构建了端点专属预测器。

Conclusion: 结果表明ADMET-EvO能够积累证据、修正策略并随时间扩展其研究范围。

Abstract: Scientific agents can move beyond automated model building by using accumulated evidence to revise both their questions and experimental strategies. The challenge is sustaining this adaptation across heterogeneous tasks without overfitting decisions to internal validation. Absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion and toxicity (ADMET) prediction provides a demanding setting across diverse assays, datasets and chemical domains. We therefore developed ADMET-EvO, an evidence-gated agent that formalizes endpoints, generates falsifiable hypotheses and tests interventions across data, feature and model axes. It carries supported, rejected and inconclusive outcomes forward to guide each new cycle. Across the 22-task Therapeutics Data Commons (TDC) ADMET benchmark, ADMET-EvO achieved the highest task-normalized score of 96.77. Evidence-guided selection reduced cumulative fitting time by 72.2% within a predefined non-inferiority margin. It also formalized 43 toxicity-related tasks and constructed endpoint-specific predictors. Together, these results show how ADMET-EvO can accumulate evidence, revise its strategy and expand its research scope over time.
