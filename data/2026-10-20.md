### [1] [Reducing double-dipping in single-cell topic models with three-way count splitting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24130)
*Zheng Qu*

Main category: q-bio.GN

TL;DR: 在单细胞主题模型中，用同一批counts既拟合主题又做检验会导致零假设基因被大量假阳性拒绝；作者提出用三分法（third-split）配合beta-binomial thinning把编码与检验的counts分离，将零基因集的拒绝率从36%–38%降到7.7%，并在LDVAE、scETM上复现，同时发现一个无标签的干扰素程序在狼疮和COVID-19队列的留出counts中发生偏移，供体级主题usage可预测疾病（AUROC 0.96）。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 主题模型从scRNA-seq中学习基因程序后，同一批counts又被用来检验哪些基因和供体与该程序相关，这种"数据复用"会破坏检验的校准。count splitting是标准补救办法，但已有工作未测试"编码器读取每个细胞counts"（如变分自编码器式主题模型）的情形——此时编码器能看到被检验基因自身的留出count，导致标准的二路拆分也失效。

Method: 在104,400细胞的狼疮队列上，每次运行加入300个置换生成的零基因；比较naive检验、常规二路拆分（two-way split）与新的三分拆分：用单独三分之一的counts编码主题usage，并采用beta-binomial thinning，使编码阶段完全接触不到被检验的count；在LDVAE和scETM两个模型上重复验证；再在狼疮队列与预先设定的COVID-19队列中用留出counts检验无标签干扰素程序，并用供体级主题usage预测疾病。

Result: 名义5%水平下，naive检验拒绝了36%的零基因-主题对，二路拆分为38%（因编码器看到被检验基因自身的留出count）；三分法+beta-binomial thinning降至7.7%（模型外基因为6.7%）；LDVAE与scETM为7.0–7.3%（对应naive为51%–59%）。一个无标签干扰素程序在两个队列的留出counts中均发生偏移；供体级主题usage预测疾病AUROC 0.96，优于细胞类型比例（两队列分别为0.86和0.58）；校准与预测不依赖具体模型。

Conclusion: 数据复用下的零假设检验需要把被检验基因的counts完全排除在编码阶段之外，三分法+beta-binomial thinning可将近乎全部的假阳性（36%→7.7%）消解，且该策略跨模型通用。作者明确不主张更好的聚类、预测或主题稳定性，仅主张检验校准的修复以及干扰素程序与疾病关联的可复现性；代码、图表和结果表以MIT许可公开。

Abstract: Topic models learn gene programs from single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) data, and the same counts are then used to test which genes and donors are associated with those programs. Count splitting is the standard remedy for this reuse, but it has not been tested when an encoder reads each cell's counts. On a 104,400-cell lupus cohort with 300 permuted null genes per run, naive tests rejected 36% of null gene-topic pairs at a nominal 5% level, and the usual two-way split rejected 38%, because the encoder sees the tested gene's own held-out count. Encoding topic usage on a separate third of the counts, with beta-binomial thinning, lowered this to 7.7% (6.7% for genes outside the model); for two other models, LDVAE and scETM, the rate was 7.0-7.3% (naive 51-59%). A label-free interferon program shifts in held-out counts in the lupus cohort and in a pre-specified COVID-19 cohort, and donor-level topic usage predicts disease (AUROC 0.96) beyond cell-type proportions (0.86 and 0.58 in the two cohorts). Calibration and prediction did not depend on the model. We do not claim better clustering, prediction or topic stability. Code, figures and result tables are public (MIT license; DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23307193).

### [2] [gpuGEM: GPU-based linear optimisation of genome-scale metabolic models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24106)
*Farid Zare†,Timothy Hulshof†,Ines Thiele,Ronan M. T. Fleming*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: gpuGEM is an open-source Python library that accelerates genome-scale metabolic model (GEM) optimization on NVIDIA GPUs via cuOpt, with size-adaptive settings, achieving 1.1x–94x speed-ups over HiGHS and outperforming Gurobi on 3/10 models (1.6x–2.5x).


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Genome-scale metabolic models keep growing in size, and repeated-optimization workflows increasingly strain CPU linear-programming solvers, creating a need for GPU-accelerated optimization tooling that is openly available and validated against established solvers.

Method: The authors developed gpuGEM, an open-source Python library built on NVIDIA cuOpt for GPU-based GEM optimization, incorporating size-adaptive solver settings. They benchmarked it on a ten-model suite spanning four orders of magnitude in problem size, comparing against the faster of HiGHS dual-simplex and interior-point methods per model, and against Gurobi; they also evaluated an end-to-end candidate-gene prioritisation workflow.

Result: gpuGEM achieved speed-ups of 1.1x to 94x relative to the faster HiGHS method for each model, and outperformed Gurobi on three of ten models with 1.6x to 2.5x speed-ups. In the candidate-gene prioritisation workflow, end-to-end query time dropped from 41 s to 21 s.

Conclusion: GPU-based optimization via cuOpt offers substantial and size-dependent gains for GEM workflows, making gpuGEM a practical, freely available (MIT-licensed, pip-installable) alternative or complement to CPU solvers such as HiGHS and Gurobi, though it requires NVIDIA GPU hardware (Volta or newer), CUDA 12 and cuOpt 26.6+.

Abstract: Summary: Genome-scale metabolic models increasingly challenge CPU linear-programming solvers in repeated-optimisation workflows. We present gpuGEM, an open-source Python library for GPU-based GEM optimisation using NVIDIA cuOpt, with size-adaptive settings validated against HiGHS and Gurobi. Across a ten-model benchmark suite spanning four orders of magnitude in problem size, gpuGEM achieved speed-ups of 1.1x to 94x relative to the faster of the HiGHS dual-simplex and interior-point methods for each model. gpuGEM also outperformed Gurobi on three of ten models, with speed-ups of 1.6x to 2.5x. In a candidate-gene prioritisation workflow, gpuGEM reduced end-to-end query time from 41 s to 21 s. Availability and Implementation: gpuGEM is released under the MIT licence at https://github.com/Digital-Metabolic-Twin-Centre/gpuGEM and on PyPI (pip install gpugem). An archived release(v0.1.2) is available at https://zenodo.org/records/23422813. gpuGEM requires an NVIDIA GPU, Python 3.11 or later, CUDA 12 and cuOpt 26.6 or later. cuOpt requires a GPU with Volta architecture or newer (compute capability 7.0 or newer). Contact: ronan.mt.fleming@gmail.com Supplementary information: Supplementary data are available at Bioinformatics online.

### [3] [Simulation-free approximate Bayesian computation for stochastic reaction networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26361)
*Zekai Li,Mauricio Barahona,Philipp Thomas*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 提出免模拟的近似贝叶斯计算框架SFABC，利用化学主方程构造可解凸优化约束来接受参数样本，避免Gillespie模拟，理论上收敛到精确后验，精度相当而计算成本大幅降低。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 从单细胞数据推断随机反应网络是核心问题。基于模拟的推断（如ABC）依赖大量Gillespie模拟，计算代价高昂；而似然本身不可解，需要绕开模拟与显式似然。

Method: SFABC框架：基于化学主方程（CME）构建可处理的凸优化问题及其约束，用这些约束判定采样参数是否与观测数据一致，从而实现免模拟、免显式似然的近似贝叶斯推断，且后验近似可系统收紧。

Result: 理论上后验近似可系统收紧并证明收敛到精确贝叶斯后验；合成基准上精度与标准模拟类算法相当而计算成本显著降低；并成功应用于基因转录爆发动力学的实验单细胞数据分析。

Conclusion: SFABC为随机反应网络的贝叶斯推断提供了无需模拟的高效替代方案，兼具理论保证与实用精度，适用于快照、时间分辨和细胞周期分辨的单细胞数据。

Abstract: Uncovering stochastic reaction networks from data is central to single-cell biology. Simulation-based inference has evolved into a flexible paradigm for this purpose, circumventing the computational intractability of the likelihood. Notably, approximate Bayesian computation performs this inference by carrying out extensive Gillespie simulations, usually at a high computational cost. Here, we propose a simulation-free approximate Bayesian computation (SFABC) framework that avoids both simulation and explicit likelihood solutions of the network dynamics. Instead, SFABC exploits the Chemical Master Equation to formulate a tractable convex optimization with ensuing constraints, which are then used to accept sampled parameters consistent with the observed data. The resulting posterior approximation comes with theoretical guarantees: it can be tightened systematically, and it provably converges to the exact Bayesian posterior distributions. Using synthetic benchmarks, we show that SFABC matches the accuracy of standard simulation-based algorithms at a substantially reduced computational cost. We then showcase the applicability of SFABC to experimental systems biology by analyzing gene transcription bursting kinetics from snapshot, time-resolved, and cell-cycle-resolved single-cell data.

### [4] [scCTM: Continuous Thought Machine Reconstruction of Glioma Cell Fate Dynamics Attractor Landscapes and Algorithmic Perturbation Signatures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23927)
*Abicumaran Uthamacumaran*

Main category: q-bio.OT

TL;DR: 该文提出 scCTM 框架，将 CTM（连续思维机）、自编码器、连续 actor-critic 策略及复杂度/可解释性分析结合，用于从静态单细胞快照预测胶质瘤细胞命运动态；在两个恶性细胞数据集上实现 AUROC≈0.99，并给出隐状态吸引子盆地与 BDM 扰动排序结果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 单细胞测序通常为静态快照，细胞命运可塑性只能间接推断，流形几何、预测建模与机制性干预排序之间存在脱节；作者希望构建一个可预测、可解释、可做干预排序的统一复杂度理论+机器学习框架。

Method: scCTM 整合 Sakana AI 的 Continuous Thought Machine（CTM）与自编码器（CTM-AE），并叠加基于同步性的隐状态上的连续 actor-critic 策略（CTM-RL）以预测单细胞命运动态；可解释性采用积分梯度与解码器权重，辅以网络科学分析、经验性 Waddington 式景观（吸引子）重建，以及事后 Block Decomposition Method（BDM）扰动分析。评估使用两个恶性细胞二次分析数据集：GSE210567 干样 vs 分化 H3K27M 细胞（18,490 细胞）与纵向 H3K27M 初发 vs 复发轨迹（20,000 细胞），任务为二分类。

We developed scCTM, a complexity theory and machine learning framework that combines the Sakana AI Continuous Thought Machine (CTM) with autoencoding, a continuous actor-critic policy over synchronization-derived latent states for predicting single-cell fate dynamics. We also incorporated integrated-gradient and decoder-weight explainability, network science, empirical Waddington-like landscape (attractor) reconstruction, and post-hoc Block Decomposition Method (BDM) perturbation analysis. The complexity framework was evaluated in two malignant cell secondary analyses: GSE210567 stem-like versus differentiated H3K27M cells (18,490 cells) and longitudinal H3K27M initial versus recurrent disease trajectory (20,000 cells) from Sussman et al. Out-of-fold CTM-RL latent states produced potential basins across PCA, UMAP, and PHATE embeddings. BDM edge perturbation analysis further identified recurrent high-impact network interactions, including epigenetic/chromatin-associated signatures involving ARID1B and developmental or neuronal-state genes such as AUTS2, CTNND2, DPP6, NRXN1, and CADM2. These in silico findings support CTM synchronization as an interpretable latent-state representation and predictive forecasting of cell fate dynamics, as well as BDM perturbation signatures as a complementary in silico intervention ranking layer for systems medicine.

### [5] [The resource cost of cooperative invasion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26235)
*Abhishek Chowdhury*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 在有结构的接触网络中，协作谱系可借助“群体性”与背叛者竞争而获得优势。本文在固定种群规模下，量化了实现该优势所需的接触强度差异、入侵时间与测量代价：在六位点路径上，弱选择下所需的最小接触对比度随b/c→1分别以656ε⁻²（均匀放置变异）与4050ε⁻³（新生子代变异）增长；全局约束迫使弱耦合两组与严格镜像对称；测量精度固定时，总试验时长的标度行为为ε⁻³，因此“最省、最快、最易测量”的网络互不相同。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 弄清在有限种群、固定规模与真实测量成本约束下，接触网络究竟需要多强的接触差异、多长的入侵时间才能让协作谱系稳定获得相对于背叛者的优势，并比较不同变异引入方式与不同测量目标下的最优网络设计。

Method: 采用弱选择（b/c=1+ε）理论分析：个体按平均化协作收益繁殖，子代通过双向等权接触替换邻居；以接触对比度（最强/最弱接触）为度量，在六位点路径网络上分析单协作者的入侵与反向背叛者入侵。分别考虑均匀放置变异与新生子代变异，利用全局界推导弱耦合双组结构与最小对比度接触的镜像对称性；在镜像对称、强分离接触族内，以线性或指数收益加权优化接触与选择强度，并分析无选择情形下的平均吸收时间与固定精度下的试验总时长缩放。

Result: 最小接触对比度：均匀放置时约656ε⁻²，新生子代变异时约4050ε⁻³；额外标度来源于稀有替换既保护位点又抑制该处变异到来。全局界要求足够小ε下形成两组弱耦合且最小对比度接触严格镜像对称。均匀引入下，额外对比度将无选择时的平均灭绝/固定时间从12/ε缩短至6/ε，并最终破坏最快设计的镜像对称；以独立完成入侵的固定结果估计均匀引入优势定义第三种最优。固定相对精度且对比度为最小值固定倍数时，主导总试验时长按ε⁻³增长。

Conclusion: 协作优势的实现在网络接触结构、入侵时间与测量代价之间存在本质权衡：最省接触（最低对比度）、最快固定/灭绝与最高测量效率对应不同的网络设计。有效测量可以接受较小的协作优势以换取更短的试验；设计者需按目标（省接触、快入侵或低测量成本）分别选网，而不存在同时最优的单一网络。

Abstract: A contact network can favor cooperation by sheltering a lineage until its descendants compete with defectors as a group. We determine the contact strengths, invasion times and measurement effort needed to realize this advantage at fixed population size. Individuals reproduce according to averaged cooperation payoffs, and offspring replace neighbors through contacts of equal weight in both directions. Contact contrast is the ratio of strongest to weakest contact. Under weak selection on a six-site path, the least contrast needed to favor a single cooperator over the reverse invasion by a defector grows as $656ε^{-2}$ for uniformly placed mutants and $4050ε^{-3}$ for mutations in newborn offspring, as the benefit-cost ratio $b/c=1+ε$ approaches one. The extra power arises because rare replacement protects a site while suppressing mutation arrival there. Global bounds force two weakly coupled groups and exact mirror symmetry of the least-contrast contacts for sufficiently small $ε$. Under uniform introduction, additional contrast shortens the leading mean time to extinction or fixation in the absence of selection, from $12/ε$ toward $6/ε$ reproduction attempts, and eventually breaks the mirror symmetry of the fastest design. Estimating the uniform-introduction advantage from fixation outcomes of independent completed invasions defines a third optimum. Within the six-site family with mirror-symmetric, strongly separated contacts, we optimize contacts and selection strength with reproduction weighted linearly or exponentially by payoff. At fixed relative precision, with contrast a fixed factor above its minimum, the leading summed trial duration grows as $ε^{-3}$. Efficient measurement accepts a smaller advantage in exchange for shorter trials. The cheapest, fastest and most efficiently measured networks therefore differ.
