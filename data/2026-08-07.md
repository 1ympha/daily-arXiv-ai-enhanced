*Martin A Nowak*

Motivation: 直接互惠是合作进化的重要机制，但合作是否能胜出取决于进化动态的细节。作者希望寻找一种简单的进化过程，能够普遍驱动直接互惠达到最大收益，跨越所有类型的社会困境。

Method: 基于突变和成对比较的进化过程模型。突变用于在策略空间边界附近采样新策略；成对比较包含选择强度参数。研究不同突变率、选择强度和大群体规模下，记忆-1和记忆-2策略在四种社会困境中的表现。

Conclusion: 简单的进化过程（突变+成对比较）具有惊人的能力，能够驱动直接互惠在所有社会困境中达到最大收益，其中记忆-2策略是实现这一目标的可靠选择。

Abstract: Direct reciprocity is a mechanism for evolution of cooperation based on repeated interactions between the same individuals. For large population sizes, intermediate to high mutation rates and intermediate to strong intensities of selection, we find that the process leads to communities of strategies that reach maximum payoff in Prisoner's Dilemma, Snowdrift, Stag Hunt and Harmony games. Maximum payoff in all four games is consistently achieved if players have access to memory-2 strategies. Memory-1 strategies have the capacity to resolve all four social dilemmas, but they are usually defeated by a ``Hold-trap'' in Snowdrift games.
