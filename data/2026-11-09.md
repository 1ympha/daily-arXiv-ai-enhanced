### [1] [Synchronized Optimal Transport Reconstructs Coupled Transcriptomic and Chromatin Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08853)
*Jingfeng Wang,Yanxiang Zhao,Qing Nie,Zixuan Cang*

Main category: q-bio.GN

TL;DR: 提出COATI方法，将非平衡同步动态最优传输扩展为时间依赖的跨模态映射，在RNA与ATAC各自的状态空间中重建耦合轨迹，并能推断细胞群体质量变化。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 单细胞RNA与ATAC数据只能提供不同发育阶段的离散快照，且测序是破坏性的，阶段间的轨迹需从独立采样的细胞群体推断。现有方法要么只在单一模态内推断连续动态，要么将RNA与ATAC整合到共享表示中，难以刻画两种模态各自状态空间中的耦合动态。

Method: COATI（Cross-Omics Aligned Trajectory Inference）把非平衡同步动态最优传输推广为时间依赖的跨模态映射，从而在互不相同的RNA和ATAC状态空间中同时重建相互耦合的轨迹；非平衡形式允许推断群体质量的变化。在合成基准与四个发育数据集上，通过留出阶段重建和谱系分析进行评估。

Abstract: Single-cell RNA and ATAC data collected across developmental stages provide time-course snapshots of transcriptional and regulatory states. Since sequencing is destructive, trajectories between these snapshots must be inferred from independently sampled cell populations. Existing approaches typically infer continuous dynamics in a single modality or integrate RNA and ATAC in a shared representation. Here we present Cross-Omics Aligned Trajectory Inference (COATI), which extends unbalanced synchronized dynamical optimal transport to time-dependent cross-modal maps for reconstructing coupled trajectories in distinct RNA and ATAC state spaces. COATI improved reconstruction of cell populations at held-out stages, including recovery of chromatin-state heterogeneity within cell types, and better preserved developmental lineage relationships. The unbalanced formulation allows the inference of population mass changes, which were shown to associate with DNA-replication programs at early stages and lineage-maturation programs later in development. The coupled trajectories enabled analysis of lineage-specific transcriptional and chromatin changes and their temporal relationships during development. Lineage responses inferred by COATI agreed with independent CRISPR perturbation results, and the inferred regulatory patterns were consistent with independent epigenetic data.

  <summary>Details</summary>

Abstract: Within many ecological populations, survival and reproduction exhibit a dependence on social interactions with relatives. As one example, resource excess is often shared according to genealogical proximity. When viewed as a branching system, an individual's offspring distribution acquires an effective dependence on genealogical structure. In the specific case that a large, local family structure leads to a large set of offspring, this can be viewed as a form of genealogical reinforcement. To capture this mechanism, we introduce a branching model of finite-depth genealogical dependence. Given partial observations of this reproducing population and its genealogy, we would like to reject the hypothesis of independent branching and estimate the strength of dependence. We present a statistical framework for detection and estimation, combining analytical results with simulation-based validation. We then identify our branching model with a tree-valued multitype branching process on a finite type space, providing a rigorous characterization of the expected structural reinforcement. Finally, we discuss how these tree-valued interactions can be accommodated within a broader class of stochastic processes.

### [3] [Resolving non-neutrality across scales in a hyperdiverse tropical forest](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09479)

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文借鉴统计力学思想，将随机矩阵滤波与空间显式中性模型结合，构建了在空间粗粒化下追踪种间空间关联的框架，并在巴罗科罗拉多岛热带森林的八次普查数据中识别出一个从"相互作用组织"到"有效中性组织"的连续跨接尺度（约20–35米）。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 不同生态过程作用于不同的时空尺度，宏观规律可由忽略微观细节的描述来刻画。但"在什么尺度上可以忽略物种差异、用中性模型作为有效描述"缺乏可操作的判定方法。作者希望提供一个定量框架，定位中性描述成立的特征尺度，同时不预设物种在个体层面等价。

Method: 将随机矩阵滤波（random-matrix filtering）与空间显式中性模型相结合，在空间粗粒化的过程中追踪种间空间关联（关联谱），并以巴罗科罗拉多岛热带森林的八次森林普查数据为实证对象，比较经验关联谱与中性预期的差异随粗粒化尺度的变化。

Conclusion: 中性可作为一种有效描述在森林集体组织层面涌现，其成立有一个特征尺度，但这并不意味着物种在个体层面等价。该框架为定位这一尺度提供了可操作的方法。

Abstract: Different ecological processes operate at different spatial and temporal scales. Macroscopic regularities can therefore be captured by descriptions that ignore microscopic details, even when those details are mechanistically relevant to ecological processes. Here, we develop a framework inspired by statistical mechanics to track interspecific spatial correlations under spatial coarse-graining. To do so we combine random-matrix filtering with a spatially explicit neutral model. Applying this approach to eight censuses of the Barro Colorado Island tropical forest, we observe a continuous crossover from an interacting to an effectively neutral organization. Below a few tens of metres (20--35~m), the empirical correlation spectrum differs from neutral expectations, revealing scale-dependent species-specific structure. Part of this structure can be attributed to environmental heterogeneity. However, residual spatial associations provide candidate signatures of biotic interactions or other unmeasured ecological, non-neutral processes. With increasing spatial coarse-graining, empirical and neutral correlation spectra progressively converge, and above the crossover scale the neutral model provides an accurate description of the measured collective organization. Neutrality thus emerges as an effective description of the collective organization of the forest above a characteristic scale, without implying that species are equivalent at the individual level. Our framework provides an operational way to locate that scale.

### [4] [Predicting fitness from genomic offset: a multivariate latent-trait framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09748)
*Olivier François*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文建立理论框架，将基因组偏移(genomic offset)与相对适合度损失联系起来，阐明其预测准确性的条件与局限，并指出基因组偏移应被理解为"遗传信息支持的适应性失配"度量，而非对实际适合度的直接预测。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 基因组偏移方法借助基因型-环境关联(GEA)量化环境变化的遗传后果，但其能预测适合度的条件尚不清楚，缺乏将基因组偏移与适合度损失联系起来的理论依据。

Method: 在稳定选择下，通过多元潜在适应性性状构建理论框架，把基因组偏移与相对适合度损失联系起来；分别在遗传架构平均意义与具体实现架构下分析二者关系，并识别两种可解释高预测精度的机制，同时推导预测准确性的边界。

Result: 在不存在直接环境效应的条件下，基因组偏移的平方与期望对数相对适合度损失（对遗传架构平均）呈线性关系，但对某个具体实现的遗传架构并不必然成立。两种互补机制可解释高预测精度：一是有效适应性维度足够大时，遗传介导的适合度响应集中于其边际期望，降低对具体遗传架构的依赖；二是即便适应性性状很少，只要完整适合度损失矩阵与GEA度量之间存在几何对齐，也能获得准确预测。第二种机制还可纳入直接环境效应及其与遗传介导响应的交互，其对齐程度决定预测精度被保持或削弱。文中进一步给出了刻画这些效应的预测准确性边界。

Conclusion: 研究澄清了基因组偏移预测所依赖的假设与局限，表明基因组偏移最适合解释为一种遗传信息支持的适应性失配度量，而非实际适合度或某一具体表型性状的直接预测指标。

Abstract: Genomic offset methods quantify the genetic consequences of environmental change using genotype environment associations (GEA), but the conditions under which they predict fitness remain poorly understood. Here, we develop a theoretical framework linking genomic offset to relative fitness loss through multivariate latent adaptive traits under stabilizing selection. We show that squared genomic offset is linearly related to expected log relative fitness loss when averaged over genetic architectures, provided that direct environmental effects are absent. However, this relationship does not necessarily hold for a realized genetic architecture. We identify two complementary mechanisms that can explain high predictive accuracy of genomic offset statistics. First, when effective adaptive dimensionality is sufficiently large, the genetically mediated fitness response concentrates around its marginal expectation, reducing the dependence on the particular genetic architecture. Second, even with few adaptive traits, accurate predictions can arise from geometric alignment between the complete fitness loss matrix and the GEA metric. This second mechanism also encompasses direct environmental effects and their interactions with genetically mediated responses, which may preserve or weaken predictive accuracy depending on their alignment. We further derive bounds on predictive accuracy that characterize these effects. Our results clarify the assumptions and limitations underlying genomic offset predictions and suggest that genomic offset is best interpreted as a measure of genetically informed adaptive mismatch, rather than a direct predictor of realized fitness or any particular phenotypic trait.
