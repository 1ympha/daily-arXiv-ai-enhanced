### [1] [Panoramic modelling of single-cell transcriptomes at scale with CellNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21943)
*Xiaoqing Lian,Pei Liu,Jiahui Tan,Luoda Tan,Chaoyi Li,Quan Zou,Chen Lin,Xiangxiang Zeng*

Main category: q-bio.GN

TL;DR: CellNet 通过整合 DNA 来源的基因身份信息与 RNA 表达测量，构建共享图像表征，实现单细胞转录组的全景建模。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 全面刻画细胞多样性对理解组织发育、识别疾病相关细胞群及研究功能特化基因程序至关重要。单细胞技术的进展虽揭示了更多细胞特异性转录本与分子关联，但现有基础模型仅能表征这一日益丰富的分子信息中的有限部分。

Method: 提出 CellNet，将 DNA 来源的基因身份与实测 RNA 表达整合进统一的图像表征中，实现单细胞转录组的全景建模；在涵盖 40 个物种、2.31 亿细胞图谱上进行预训练。

Result: 在评估队列中提升了稀有细胞群与状态相关状态的识别能力，同时保持有竞争力的通用细胞表征；可预测 total-RNA 数据中留出的编码基因表达及部分非编码靶标，并恢复空间测量中的谱系特异性表达模式；预测能力可跨物种延伸，包括无表达预训练暴露的靶标；其基因表征可检索已知非编码模块及发育或神经程序，包括来自稀疏检测的 RNA 查询。

Conclusion: CellNet 是一个可扩展的框架，能够通过日益全面的转录组测量来解读细胞多样性。

Abstract: Comprehensively characterizing cellular diversity is critical for understanding tissue development, identifying disease-associated populations and investigating gene programmes linked to specialized functions. Advances in single-cell profiling reveal additional cell-specific transcripts and molecular associations, yet many foundation models represent only a restricted portion of this increasingly rich molecular information. Here we present CellNet, which enables panoramic modelling of single-cell transcriptomes by integrating DNA-derived gene identities and measured RNA expression in a shared image representation. Pretrained on 231 million cell profiles spanning 40 species, CellNet improves rare-population and condition-associated state recognition in evaluated cohorts while retaining competitive general cell representations. It predicts withheld coding-gene expression and selected non-coding targets in total-RNA data, and recovers lineage-specific expression patterns in spatial measurements. Prediction also extends across species, including to targets without expression-pretraining exposure. Its gene representations retrieve known non-coding modules and developmental or neural programmes, including from sparsely detected RNA queries. Together, these results establish CellNet as a scalable framework for interpreting cellular diversity through increasingly comprehensive transcriptomic measurements.

### [2] [Documenting Trofinetide Treatment Phenotypes in Rett Syndrome with a Dual-LLM Consensus Pipeline](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22062)
*Daniel Palacios,Guantong Qi,Mikael Guzman Karlsson,Shawntel K. David,Mohammed Sameeruddin,Bernhard Suter,Zhandong Liu,Davut Pehlivan,Hu Chen*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 该研究开发了双大语言模型（LLM）共识流水线，从电子病历中提取18种trofinetide治疗反应表型，为Rett综合征的真实世界监测提供了可扩展的替代方案。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: trofinetide于2023年获批成为首个Rett综合征治疗药物，但缺乏可扩展的真实世界监测手段，因为人工病历审查耗时耗力。因此需要一种自动化方法来提取治疗反应表型。

Method: 分析了德州儿童医院108名确认持续使用trofinetide患者的26,763份临床记录。流水线先按关键词筛选记录，再通过两个LLM分类并要求二者共识，最后汇总为"患者×时间点"矩阵。架构和提示词先在腹泻开发集上选择，随后冻结并替换表型定义应用于其余17种表型，作为样本外测试。提示词比较评分基于183个专家标注单元格。

Result: 要求腹泻处于活动且严重状态的提示词表现最佳（准确率84.2±0.0%；F1=0.674），比正则表达式匹配高47个百分点（p<0.0001）。专家审阅每种表型的6个流水线判断（3正3负），与流水线一致率为90.2%（92/102）。13种Rett临床特征在每次就诊时被记录存在的比例为92-100%。

Conclusion: 将上下文内LLM推理与共识架构相结合，为真实世界证据提取提供了可扩展的替代方案，仅需通过配置即可适应新的表型和药物。需注意结果记录的是每次就诊时的"存在性"，而非变化方向判断。

Abstract: Objective: Trofinetide, approved in 2023 as the first Rett syndrome treatment, lacks scalable real-world monitoring because chart review is labor-intensive. We developed a dual large language model (LLM) consensus pipeline to extract 18 treatment-response phenotypes, comprising 4 adverse effects, 13 Rett clinical features, and medication refusal, from clinical notes. Materials and Methods: We analyzed 26,763 notes from 108 patients with confirmed sustained trofinetide exposure at Texas Children's Hospital. The pipeline filters notes by keyword, classifies each through two LLMs requiring consensus, and aggregates a patient-by-timepoint matrix. Architecture and prompt were selected on a diarrhea development set, then frozen and applied to the other 17 phenotypes with the phenotype definition substituted, as an out-of-sample test. The reported prompt comparison scores 183 expert-labeled cells. Results: A prompt requiring diarrhea to be active and severe performed best (84.2 $\pm$ 0.0% accuracy; F1 = 0.674), 47 percentage points above regular-expression matching ($p < 0.0001$). Expert reviewers judged six of the pipeline's calls per phenotype, three positive and three negative, and agreed with 90.2% (92/102). The 13 Rett clinical features are manifestations of the disorder itself rather than adverse effects of trofinetide. Gross and fine motor impairment, language/communication, and stereotypy were documented present in 92-100% of patients at every visit. Discussion: In-context LLM extraction captured multi-domain treatment responses. The outcome is documented presence at each visit, not a direction-of-change judgment. Conclusion: Coupling in-context LLM reasoning with a consensus architecture offers a scalable alternative to keyword and regular-expression extraction for real-world evidence, adapting to new phenotypes and medications through configuration alone.
