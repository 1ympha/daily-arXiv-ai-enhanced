### [1] [Navigating the Delicate Geometry of Beehive Mite Infestation with Optimal Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16316)
*Julia Saff,Bhargav R. Karamched*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文建立含真社会性Allee效应的蜂群-瓦螨动力学模型，区分“温和”与“强烈”杀螨剂，通过分岔分析与最优控制揭示化学干预的疗效-毒性权衡，并预测螨害压力将进化筛选出更小、天然抗螨的蜂群。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 瓦螨（Varroa destructor）对全球蜜蜂种群构成严重生存威胁，而化学干预在清除螨虫与蜂群健康之间存在矛盾，需要定量评估不同杀螨策略的疗效与毒性边界，以指导可持续的蜂群管理。

Method: 构建蜂群-螨互作的动力系统模型，引入真社会性Allee效应；将处理分为作用于游走螨的“温和”杀螨剂与穿透封盖巢房、作用于繁殖螨的“强烈”杀螨剂；采用分岔分析刻画根除边界与崩溃阈值，并基于Pontryagin极大值原理建立最优控制问题，进一步扩展模型纳入抗药性螨株。

Result: 分岔分析显示存在根本性权衡：强烈处理可通过移动跨临界分岔边界有效清除受保护的螨库，但对蜜蜂产生亚致死毒性；超过临界剂量会触发鞍结分岔，导致蜂群必然崩溃。最优控制的数值解表明，动态“冲击-维持”复方策略能在螨库清除与蜂群存续间取得最优平衡。纳入抗药性后，单一化学依赖使蜂群陷入高毒、几乎不可持续的“化学跑步机”。

Conclusion: 根除边界与蜂群承载能力密切相关，因此不受控制的螨害压力可能产生进化选择压力，倾向于筛选出更小、天然抗螨的蜂群，而非商业农业偏好的大规模蜂群；化学干预需在毒性阈值内采用动态复方策略才能维持蜂群可持续性。

Abstract: The parasitic mite Varroa destructor poses a severe existential threat to global honey bee Apis mellifera populations. In this paper, we present a dynamical systems model of hive-mite interactions incorporating a eusocial Allee effect to evaluate the efficacy of chemical interventions. We partition treatments into ``soft'' miticides (targeting phoretic mites) and ``harsh'' miticides (penetrating the capped brood to target reproductive mites). Our bifurcation analysis reveals a fundamental trade-off: while harsh treatments effectively eradicate the protected mite reservoir by shifting the transcritical bifurcation boundary, they impose sublethal toxicity on the bees. We analytically show that exceeding a critical dosage threshold culminates in a catastrophic saddle-node bifurcation that guarantees colony collapse. To navigate this toxicity limit, we formulate an optimal control problem using Pontryagin's Maximum Principle. Numerical solutions reveal that a dynamic ``shock and maintain'' cocktail strategy optimally balances reservoir clearance with hive viability. Expanding the model to include treatment-resistant strains demonstrates that single-chemical reliance forces the hive onto a highly toxic, nearly unsustainable chemical treadmill. Finally, our mathematical framework yields a striking ecological prediction: because the eradication boundary scales intimately with the colony's carrying capacity, unchecked mite pressure will likely exert evolutionary forces that select for smaller, naturally resistant hives over the massive colonies favored by commercial agriculture.

### [2] [Anomalous First Passage in Evolution: Edge-KPZ Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16499)
*Tetsuhiro S. Hatakeyama*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 中性Wright-Fisher演化在表型扩散突变下呈现反常首达时间，其均值约按(σ²)^(-3/2)标度，存在两个交叉区域，可用边缘-KPZ理论统一描述，弱选择下反常仍存在。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 演化速度取决于新表型出现的快慢，因此理解首个个体到达指定表型距离所需时间及其标度规律具有重要意义。作者旨在揭示中性演化下首达过程是否遵循常规扩散预期。

Method: 研究中性Wright-Fisher演化中的首达问题，分析首个个体达到给定表型距离的平均时间随突变方差σ²的标度；结合溯祖理论与稀有种群边缘的KPZ涨落，构建覆盖三个区域的边缘-KPZ理论，并检验弱选择下的稳健性。

Result: 尽管突变为扩散型，首达过程表现反常：平均首达时间约按(σ²)^(-3/2)标度。该区域被两个交叉点界定，两侧分别呈逆方差标度。边缘-KPZ理论统一刻画三个区域，且反常现象在弱选择下依然持续。

Conclusion: 中性Wright-Fisher演化中的首达时间由边缘KPZ涨落主导，而非简单扩散律；该反常标度规律在弱选择下稳健，为理解表型演化速率提供了新理论框架。

Abstract: The pace of evolution depends on how rapidly new phenotypes arise. We show that neutral Wright-Fisher evolution exhibits anomalous first passage despite diffusive mutations. The mean time for the first individual to reach a prescribed phenotypic distance scales approximately as $(σ^2)^{-3/2}$ with mutation variance $σ^2$. Two crossovers bound this regime, with inverse-variance scaling on either side. Combining coalescent theory with Kardar-Parisi-Zhang (KPZ) fluctuations at the dilute population edge, we develop an edge-KPZ theory of all three regimes. The anomaly persists under weak selection.

### [3] [Graph construction in QUBO-based recursive phylogenetic tree reconstruction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16640)
*Yoshiki Kanazawa,Ashish Joshi,Takahiko Koyama*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 本文在递归归一化割（Ncut）框架下比较不同序列-亲和度表示对系统发育重建的影响，发现图构建（亲和度表示）是重建精度的关键决定因素。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 系统发育重建的准确性不仅取决于建树方法，还取决于如何表示成对序列关系，因此需要评估不同序列到亲和度的表示方式在递归Ncut框架中的作用。

Method: 将递归Ncut的图划分子问题建模为QUBO模型并用模拟分岔（Simulated Bifurcation）求解，在模拟的氨基酸与核苷酸数据集上比较归一化比特分数亲和度、变换后的序列相似度及进化距离表示，考察交换后精化，并以邻接法（NJ）作为距离法对照。

Result: 亲和度表示显著影响内部分支划分的恢复，尤以核苷酸数据为甚；JC69局部亲和度随分歧增大仍保持较高精度，而归一化比特分数与BLAST核表示下降更明显；交换后精化通常改善恢复但不稳定；NJ在WAG和JC69距离下平均划分恢复高于对应递归Ncut，而递归Ncut在某些条件下对BLAST对数距离优于NJ。

Conclusion: 图构建是递归Ncut系统发育重建的重要决定因素，在Ncut中表现良好的表示未必能最准确利用底层成对距离，因此应联合评估成对表示、亲和度变换、优化与递归建树。

Abstract: Molecular sequence data are used to reconstruct evolutionary relationships among taxa, but reconstruction accuracy depends not only on the tree-building method but also on how pairwise sequence relationships are represented. We evaluated sequence-to-affinity representations in a recursive normalized-cut (Ncut) framework whose graph-partitioning subproblems were formulated as quadratic unconstrained binary optimization (QUBO) models and solved using Simulated Bifurcation. Using simulated amino-acid and nucleotide datasets spanning multiple tree-generation settings and evolutionary divergence, we compared normalized bit-score affinities with representations derived from transformed sequence similarities and evolutionary distances, examined post-swap refinement, and used neighbor joining (NJ) as a distance-based comparator.
  Affinity representation substantially affected internal split recovery, particularly for nucleotide data. JC69-based local affinities maintained comparatively high accuracy as divergence increased, whereas normalized bit-score and BLAST-derived kernel representations declined more markedly. Post-swap refinement generally improved recovery, but not consistently across individual reconstructions. NJ achieved higher mean split recovery than corresponding recursive Ncut reconstructions for WAG and JC69 distances across all evaluated conditions, whereas recursive Ncut outperformed NJ for BLAST-derived logarithmic distances under some conditions.
  These results show that graph construction is an important determinant of recursive Ncut-based phylogenetic reconstruction. A representation that performs well within Ncut does not necessarily provide the most accurate use of the underlying pairwise distances. Pairwise representation, affinity transformation, optimization, and recursive tree construction should therefore be evaluated jointly.

### [4] [Optical microelectrode arrays for differential readout of electrical and mechanical signals in cardiac cells](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16101)
*Alessandro Leronni,Rosalia Moreddu*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 提出一种无标记光学方法，可在光学微电极上同时读取心肌细胞的电兴奋与机械收缩信号，并通过对比搏动细胞与抑制收缩后的记录，将动作电位相关的静电转导与收缩驱动的膜位移分离。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 心肌细胞功能研究需要同时评估电兴奋与机械收缩，但现有方法通常需分别测量或具侵入性，缺乏无标记、高通量的同步检测手段。

Method: 利用光学微电极：细胞电活动改变局部电荷再分布并转化为荧光信号；细胞收缩引起膜位移，为光学读出增加机械分量。通过对比搏动状态与抑制收缩后状态的记录，分离两类信号来源，并阐释膜基光学器件的物理机制。

Result: 成功将动作电位相关的静电转导信号与收缩性驱动的膜运动信号区分开来，实现了电-机械双模态的无标记同步读出。

Conclusion: 该方法为心脏毒性筛选和机电传感提供了无标记、高通量的体外检测途径，同时揭示了膜基光学器件在心脏生物电子学与力学中的物理机制。

Abstract: Simultaneous assessment of electrical excitation and mechanical contraction is essential for understanding cardiac cell function, yet these two processes are commonly measured with separate techniques or invasively. Here, changes in cellular electrical activity modulate local charge redistribution in optical microelectrodes and are converted into fluorescence signals, while cell contraction induces membrane displacement that contributes an additional mechanical component to the optical readout. By comparing recordings obtained in beating cells with those acquired after inhibition of contraction, we separate action-potential-associated electrostatic transduction from contractility-driven membrane motion. The approach offers a label-free route to support high-throughput in vitro assays for cardiotoxicity screening and electromechanical sensing. Concurrently, it unfolds the physical mechanisms governing membrane-based optical devices deployed in cardiac cell bioelectronics and mechanics.

### [5] [GPCR Ligand Bioactivity Prediction with Physics-Informed Dual-State Query Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16468)
*Shuo Zhang,Huifeng Zhang,Rongqi Hong,Jian K. Liu*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 提出 Dual-State Query (DSQ)，一种将 MWC 别构模型嵌入神经网络的多模态架构，用于预测小分子对 GPCR 的生物活性谱，在激动剂子集上优于基线。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: GPCR 生物活性预测具有挑战性。现有深度学习方法忽视动态构象平衡，难以区分功能效力；而基于结构的方法受限于高质量活性态晶体结构稀缺及静态表征难以区分构象态。研究希望弥合黑箱预测与生物物理现实之间的差距。

Method: DSQ 是物理信息驱动的多模态架构，将 Monod-Wyman-Changeux (MWC) 别构模型显式嵌入神经网络。它不依赖显式 3D 结构，而是用可学习的正交查询提取活性和非活性受体态的解耦表征，并通过新型神经 MWC 门控模块，从配体-态亲和力与受体固有构象能垒间的热力学竞争中数学推导受体激活概率。同时引入对比排序目标以约束差异亲和力，保证物理一致性。

Result: 大量实验表明 DSQ 优于其他基线，尤其在激动剂子集上表现突出。额外同源分层、温度敏感性、扰动、聚类和效率分析显示，DSQ 提供了有用的热力学归纳偏置，同时也暴露出在低同源性受体上的明显局限。

Conclusion: DSQ 通过将 MWC 别构模型与多模态神经网络结合，为 GPCR 生物活性预测提供了具物理一致性的方法，提升预测性能并具热力学可解释性，但低同源性受体仍是限制。

Abstract: Predicting the bioactivity profiles of small molecules against G protein-coupled receptors (GPCRs) is a challenge in drug discovery. Although deep learning has accelerated the prediction of binding affinities, existing approaches often struggle to distinguish between functional efficacies because they neglect dynamic conformational equilibria. Furthermore, structure-based methods are frequently limited by the scarcity of high-resolution active-state crystal structures and the indistinguishability of conformational states in static representations. To bridge the gap between black-box prediction and biophysical reality, we propose Dual-State Query (DSQ), a physics-informed multimodal architecture that explicitly embeds the Monod-Wyman-Changeux (MWC) model of allostery within a neural network. Unlike conventional models that rely on explicit 3D structures, DSQ utilizes learnable orthogonal queries to extract disentangled representations of active and inactive receptor states. These latent representations are governed by a novel neural MWC gating module, which mathematically derives the probability of receptor activation from thermodynamic competition between ligand-state affinities and the receptor's intrinsic conformational energy barrier. A contrastive ranking objective is also introduced to enforce differential affinity constraints, ensuring physical consistency. Extensive experiments demonstrate that DSQ outperforms other baselines, particularly for the agonist subset. Additional homology-stratified, temperature-sensitivity, perturbation, clustering, and efficiency analyses show that DSQ provides useful thermodynamic inductive bias, while also exposing a clear limitation on low-homology receptors. The code is available at https://github.com/jiankliu/DSQ.

</details>
