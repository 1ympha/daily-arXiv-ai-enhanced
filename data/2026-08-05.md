### [1] [Cross-Anesthetic ECoG State Decoding Fails at the Decision Threshold, Not the Representation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02646)
*Kunkun Zhang,Qianwei Zhou*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本研究通过留一麻醉药评估，分离了麻醉状态解码中"神经表征失败"与"决策阈值失败"两个层面。结果表明表征在不同麻醉药之间成功迁移（含氯胺酮，AUROC≥0.96），失败仅限于阈值校准；基于个体基线的无标签阈值锚定可修复氯胺酮解码性能，优于黎曼域适应。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有麻醉状态解码器在不同药物类别间（尤其是氯胺酮）准确率下降，但无法判断问题是出在神经表征本身无法泛化，还是仅决策阈值失效。作者希望在受控条件下将二者分离，以准确定位跨药物解码失败的根本原因。

Method: 使用小鼠皮层脑电图（250Hz带限局部场电位，1875Hz采样），在五类机制不同的麻醉药（异氟烷、右美托咪定、氯胺酮、丙泊酚、咪达唑仑）下进行留一麻醉药评估，比较空间盲带功率解码器、协方差/黎曼表征及黎曼域适应；统计在会话水平进行，氯胺酮折叠采用小鼠群簇自助法。

Result: 表征可迁移：每种留出药物上带功率排序的会话AUROC≥0.96，氯胺酮为0.980（置信区间0.821-1.000）。失败限于阈值：三种表征下氯胺酮排序近乎不变而平衡准确率大幅波动；置换检验对排序显著（p=0.0025）但对固定阈值准确率不显著（p=0.3795）。黎曼域适应用处为负。基于个体诱导前基线的无标签阈值可将氯胺酮平衡准确率从0.50提升至0.85，优于域适应。注意：氯胺酮测试会话来自同时贡献训练药物的三只小鼠，属受试者内跨药物迁移，不声称群体级跨受试者迁移。

Conclusion: 在跨药物麻醉状态解码中，可操作的失败环节是校准而非表征。神经表征能够跨药物泛化，真正需要解决的是阈值设置问题。

Abstract: Decoders of anesthetic state from cortical activity fail across drug classes, most notoriously ketamine, but reported accuracy cannot say whether the neural representation or only the decision threshold has failed; we separate the two in a controlled preparation with ground-truth labels. We decoded awake versus anesthetized from mouse electrocorticography (the 250-Hz-bandlimited local field potential, sampled at 1875 Hz) under leave-one-anesthetic-out evaluation across five mechanistically distinct anesthetics (isoflurane, dexmedetomidine, ketamine, propofol, and midazolam), comparing a spatially blind band-power decoder, covariance/Riemannian representations, and Riemannian domain adaptation, with all statistics at the session level and mouse-level cluster bootstrapping for the ketamine fold. The representation transfers: band-power ranks awake versus anesthetized at a session AUROC of at least 0.96 on every held-out drug, ketamine included (0.980, cluster confidence interval 0.821 to 1.000). The failure is confined to the threshold: across three representations the ketamine ranking is near-invariant while its balanced accuracy swings from chance to high, and a permutation test is significant for ranking (p = 0.0025) but not for fixed-threshold accuracy (p = 0.3795). Riemannian domain adaptation is net-negative. A causal, label-free threshold anchored to the subject's own pre-induction baseline fixes ketamine (balanced accuracy 0.50 to 0.85) and dominates domain adaptation. Because the ketamine test sessions come from three mice that also contribute training drugs, this is within-subject cross-drug transfer; we do not claim population-level transfer across subjects. In cross-drug state decoding the actionable failure is calibration, not representation.

### [2] [A Blind Spot in Alignment: Quantifying Biosecurity Risks in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02684)
*Shu Quan,Tianfang Hao,Sitong Fang,He Geng,Jiayi Zhou,Boyuan Chen,Kaile Wang,Donghai Hong,Juntao Dai,Yaodong Yang,Jiaming Ji*

Main category: q-bio.QM

TL;DR: 本文提出SPIKE-Bench，一个包含631个毒素设计提示词和多阶段安全评估流程的基准，用于评估LLM在蛋白质工程中的生物安全风险，并发布BioSafe-Guard分类器用于降低预测功能风险。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM在加速生物研究的同时，也可能被诱导生成类毒素蛋白质序列，构成生物安全威胁。现有安全评估仅基于自然语言，无法判断模型生成的氨基酸序列是否具有真实的生物风险，存在评估盲区。

Method: 构建SPIKE-Bench，包含631个跨7个功能类别的毒素设计提示词；提出SPIKE漏斗协议，分为合规性、生物合理性和预测毒性三个阶段的过滤流程；引入功能危害率（FHR）聚合指标；审计32个LLM，并开发BioSafe-Guard领域专用分类器。

Conclusion: 自然语言安全评估不足以衡量LLM的生物安全功能风险，需要领域专用的评估基准和缓解工具，SPIKE-Bench和BioSafe-Guard为此提供了基础设施。

Abstract: Large Language Models (LLMs) are accelerating biological research, yet this same capability poses a critical biosecurity threat: models that assist in protein engineering can equally be prompted to generate predicted toxin-like sequences, potentially lowering the barrier to biological misuse. Current safety evaluations, however, operate in natural language and cannot determine whether a model-generated amino acid sequence is biological gibberish or a computational risk signal. To address this evaluation blind spot, we introduce SPIKE-Bench, coupling 631 curated toxin-design prompts across seven functional categories with the SPIKE funnel, a three-stage protocol that filters output through compliance, biological plausibility, and predicted toxicity, producing stage-level diagnostics and an aggregate function-aware metric: the Functional Harmfulness Rate (FHR). An audit of 32 LLMs reveals that most models freely comply with toxin-design requests; FHR is driven primarily by biological generation capability rather than safety alignment, reaching 50.7%; and Refusal Rate fails to predict functional risk. As a first step toward mitigation, we provide BioSafe-Guard, a domain-specialized classifier that substantially reduces predicted functional risk while preserving benign utility. We release SPIKE-Bench and BioSafe-Guard at https://github.com/PKU-Alignment/SPIKE-Bench to support more rigorous biosecurity evaluation of LLMs.

### [3] [A method for comparing inferred evolutionary accumulation dynamics across covariates and model structures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02781)

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 提出基于排序矩阵（ordering matrices）的比较方法：该矩阵描述某特征在其他特征不同条件下被获得的概率，从而在不同方法和数据集间具有普遍可比性。该方法能够同时捕捉统计稳健性（考虑EvAM的不确定性）和科学上有意义的动力学差异，并能区分状态相似性与转移相似性。

Conclusion: 所提出的排序矩阵方法为EvAM推断动力学的比较提供了一种通用且稳健的框架，支持复杂动力学场景（可逆、随机、交互特征），并能区分转移层面的相似性，为跨方法、跨数据集和跨协变量的动力学比较建立了可行途径。

Abstract: In this note we describe a method for comparing inferred dynamics in evolutionary accumulation models (EvAMs). These models involve the acquisition of multiple, potentially codependent, binary features over time -- for example, mutations in cancer development, or phenotypes in evolutionary biology. In particular, a comparison method supporting reversible, stochastic dynamics, interactions between feature sets, and potentially non-independent samples (as well as simpler cases) has yet to be established. Here we suggest a method focussed on ordering matrices, describing the probability that a feature is acquired under different conditions on other features, and is thus generally comparable across methods and datasets. We demonstrate how the approach captures both statistical robustness (given EvAM uncertainty) and scientifically meaningful differences in inferred dynamics. The method is applied to synthetic and real-world data on the evolution of chromosomal aberrations in different tumour types and of drug-resistant bacteria in different countries.
