### [1] [CellMSA: Context Modeling for Single-Cell Representation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38908)
*Suyuan Zhao,Minghao Liu,Yizhen Luo,Zaiqing Nie*

Main category: q-bio.GN

TL;DR: CellMSA借鉴蛋白质建模中的多序列比对(MSA)思想，通过检索跨批次、跨细胞类型的相关细胞作为上下文，构建上下文依赖的基因对表示并注入配对感知编码器，在大规模人类单细胞语料（约1.09亿细胞观测）上预训练，在多个基准上超越现有方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 单细胞转录组高维、稀疏且存在批次效应，表示学习困难。现有单细胞基础模型通常独立编码每个细胞，或仅对同一批次细胞建模去噪，未能充分利用跨批次与跨细胞类型的丰富关系信息来建模基因表达模式。作者认为，通过比较细胞间的一致性与变异，可捕获与细胞状态相关的细粒度基因-基因依赖，从而学到更高质量的表示。

Method: 受蛋白质建模中多序列比对(MSA)上下文的启发，提出CellMSA框架，将MSA式归纳偏置引入转录组建模。对每个目标细胞，从不同批次及生物学相关的细胞类型中检索相关细胞作为上下文，将跨细胞模式汇总为上下文依赖的基因对表示，再将该表示注入配对感知的目标细胞编码器中以进行细粒度表示学习。

Result: CellMSA在大规模人类单细胞语料上预训练，约1.09亿个细胞观测，其中包含6560万条原始观测。实验表明，该框架在多个基准上持续优于现有方法。

Conclusion: 跨细胞上下文建模（MSA式归纳偏置）能有效提升单细胞表示学习质量，CellMSA在多项基准上取得一致优势。代码已开源：https://github.com/PharMolix/CellMSA。

Abstract: Single-cell transcriptomics enables profiling of cellular states at unprecedented resolution, but its high dimensionality, sparsity, and technical batch effects pose significant challenges for representation learning. Existing single-cell foundation models typically encode each cell independently or only model cells from the same batch for denoising, thereby underutilizing the rich relational information across batches and cell types to model gene expression patterns. We argue that single-cell models can benefit from more informative cell-context modeling. By comparing consistency and variation across cells, models can capture fine-grained gene-gene dependencies associated with cell states, which are essential for learning high-quality representations. Inspired by the use of multiple sequence alignment (MSA) context in protein modeling, we propose CellMSA, a single-cell representation learning framework that introduces an MSA-inspired inductive bias into transcriptomic modeling. For each target cell, CellMSA retrieves relevant cells from different batches and biologically related cell types as context, and summarizes cross-cell patterns into a context-dependent gene-pair representation. This representation is then injected into a pair-aware target-cell encoder for fine-grained representation learning. We pretrain CellMSA on a large-scale human single-cell corpus of approximately 109 million cell observations, including 65.6 million primary observations. Experiments show that our framework consistently outperforms existing methods across multiple benchmarks. Code is available at the following repository: https://github.com/PharMolix/CellMSA.

### [3] [Evolutionary foraging in grids: Intermittent search dynamics emerge in finite, depletable landscapes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39239)
*Shailendra Bhandari,Alex Szorkovszky,Anis Yazidi,Pedro G. Lind*

Main category: q-bio.PE

TL;DR: 该研究通过进化模拟探讨有限可耗尽景观中搜索策略的演化，发现进化出的搜索轨迹更符合间歇性搜索动力学而非严格的无标度Lévy运动。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 觅食理论中，搜索策略在有限、可耗尽景观中如何演化仍是未解问题；作者希望在不预设幂律步长分布的前提下，检验进化轨迹究竟更接近间歇性搜索还是Lévy行走。

Method: 构建二维环形网格上的进化模拟，资源不可再生，呈均匀分布或Lévy尘埃分布；每个智能体携带可遗传基因组，编码步长、速度和转向角，适应度函数结合能量收益、移动成本与覆盖效率；对位移的二阶和四阶矩拟合间歇性搜索模型与Lévy行走模型。

Result: Lévy型随机行走对进化轨迹拟合良好（多数条件下平均调整R²>0.9），但间歇性搜索拟合更优（所有测试资源分布下平均调整R²>0.99）；该偏好跨网格大小与资源密度成立；在503×503网格、名义资源密度ρ=0.15下，每种环境5次独立进化运行重现此偏好；进化迅速将运动基因组调整为短位移并保留稀疏的长程迁移尾部。

Conclusion: 进化搜索更符合间歇性动力学，即局部开发穿插偶发转移；该框架为研究资源限制下的搜索规则提供受控环境，并可启发自主系统中的资源受限探索。

Abstract: How search strategies evolve in finite, depletable landscapes remains a question in foraging theory. We study this problem with an evolutionary simulation in which agents forage on a two-dimensional toroidal lattice containing non-renewable resources distributed uniformly or as Lévy dust. Each agent carries a heritable genome encoding step lengths, velocities, and turning angles, and selection acts on a fitness function combining energetic gain, movement cost, and coverage efficiency. By allowing movement traits to evolve without imposing a prescribed power-law step-length distribution, we test whether evolved trajectories are better described by intermittent-search or Lévy-walk dynamics. Our results indicate that evolved search is more consistent with intermittent dynamics than with strict scale-free Lévy motion in the finite depletion-driven landscapes considered here. We characterize the dynamics by fitting second- and fourth-order displacement moments to intermittent-search and Lévy-walk models. While a Lévy-like random walk fits the evolutionary trajectories well (mean adjusted $R^2$ > 0.9 in most tested conditions), intermittent search achieves a closer fit (mean adjusted $R^2$ > 0.99) for all tested resource distributions. This preference holds across the tested grid sizes and resource densities. Five independent evolutionary runs per environment on a 503 x 503 grid at nominal resource density $ρ$ = 0.15 reproduce this preference for the uniform environment and five Lévy-dust environments. Evolution rapidly reshapes the movement genome toward short displacements while retaining a sparse tail of longer relocations, consistent with local exploitation punctuated by occasional transfer. The framework provides a controlled setting for studying how search rules emerge under resource limitation and may inform resource-constrained exploration in autonomous systems.
