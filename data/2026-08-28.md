Motivation: 传染病流行病学正越来越多地采用AI方法进行预测、监测和疫情分析，但其可用时间序列数据规模远小于推动其他领域（如蛋白质结构预测、自然语言处理）AI进步的语料规模。由于真实流行病学数据在实际中无法达到训练大规模AI方法所需的规模，模拟数据成为可行的替代方案。

Method: Reservoir由一个随机模拟器生成，该模拟器包含真实的噪声和报告伪影，以及可配置时序、依从性和年龄依赖效力的干预措施。当前版本包含50万条疫情爆发轨迹，涵盖多样化的病原特征、人口结构和干预制度，每条轨迹都携带完整的真实标签。

Result: 当前版本包含500,000条疫情爆发轨迹，覆盖10亿模拟天，跨越多种病原特征、人口结构和干预制度。该数据集支持反事实实验、监测设计研究以及流行病模型的规模化训练，这些是真实世界数据集无法提供的。

Conclusion: Reservoir为流行病学AI研究提供了一个可扩展的模拟数据基础设施，能够在真实数据无法企及的规模上支持反事实实验、监测设计研究和流行病模型的训练，弥补了真实流行病学数据规模不足的瓶颈。

Abstract: Large-scale, standardized datasets have driven many advances in AI-based scientific modeling, from protein structure prediction to natural language processing. Infectious disease epidemiology is increasingly adopting AI methods for forecasting, surveillance, and outbreak analytics, but the time-series data available to train them remains orders of magnitude smaller than the corpora behind the advances seen in other fields. Because the scope of real-world epidemiological data cannot practically reach the scale needed to train truly large-scale AI methods, simulated data provides a possible alternative. Here we introduce Reservoir, a large open simulator and dataset of realistic epidemic simulations in which every trajectory carries complete ground-truth labels, including quantities that cannot be measured directly in a real outbreak, such as true infection counts, time-varying reproduction numbers, and counterfactual intervention effects. Reservoir is generated by a stochastic simulator with realistic noise and reporting artifacts, together with interventions with configurable timing, compliance, and age-dependent efficacy. The current release contains 500,000 outbreak trajectories spanning one billion simulated days across diverse pathogen characteristics, population structures, and intervention regimes. Reservoir enables counterfactual experiments, surveillance-design studies, and training of epidemic models at a scale real-world datasets cannot provide.
